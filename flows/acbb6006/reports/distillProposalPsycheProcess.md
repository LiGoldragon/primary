# Vision/distillation.md (proposed, new)

# Distillation

## Vision impurities
Destination: Vision/distillation.md. From b675f3d9/17.

A working instruction logged as vision is a vision impurity. Found
in distillation, an impurity is destroyed, never archived.

## Impurities fall only through distillation
Destination: Vision/distillation.md. From b675f3d9/19.

An impurity is never hunted. It is eliminated only through
distillation, and a distillation proposal points out the impurities
it will discard.

## A proposal names each statement's destination
Destination: Vision/distillation.md. From b675f3d9 distillation.md.

A distillation proposal says, for every statement, the topic it
goes to. A statement offered without its place can be neither
accepted nor refused, and a statement placed under the wrong topic
is itself corrected by a distillation edit.

## Designing model behavior is vision
Destination: Vision/distillation.md. From b675f3d9/18, /20.

Designing model behavior is vision, and a correction of an agent's
conduct can be vision. The line of what counts as designing is
drawn wide. What does not qualify as vision is stated with the same
clarity.

## No useless negatives
Destination: Vision/distillation.md. From ac1e9ec8 distillationNegatives.md, first record.

A distilled statement carries no useless negative. Such negatives
stay in the archive, which remains linkable.

## A statement never attributes itself to the psyche
Destination: Vision/distillation.md. From ac1e9ec8 distillationNegatives.md, second record.

Vision is the psyche's; a distilled statement never says so of
itself.

# Vision/remembering.md (proposed, new)

# Remembering

## All flows are one subjectivity
Destination: Vision/remembering.md. From b675f3d9/23, /24.

All flows are one subjectivity; this is the reason behind
remembering. What one flow did or said, every flow did or said.
Told "you did" or "you said" of a thing it did not itself do or
say, a flow remembers it at a depth fit to the question, reaching
the transcript directly when the logs are not enough.

## The last model response is read
Destination: Vision/remembering.md. From b675f3d9/22.

Remembering a flow includes reading that flow's last model response.

## The remembering line says what was found most relevant
Destination: Vision/remembering.md. From b675f3d9/24.

The log's record of a remembering carries a short description of
what from the remembered flow was found most relevant to the
current one.
