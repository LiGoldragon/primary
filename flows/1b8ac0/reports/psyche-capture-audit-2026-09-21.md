## Options to close each gap class — offered as forks, not decisions

**1 · The citation gap (30 of 31 records).**
`psyche-interraction` requires a provenance line but never requires a transcript locator. Options: (a) extend the provenance line to `-- psyche, STT. 0625c31b:422.` and make a record without one incomplete; (b) leave the skill alone and add the locator only where it is free (direct hearings), accepting that relays stay uncitable; (c) do nothing and accept that Vision's chain terminates at a flow-and-topic name. I have no verbatim from the living on which of these they want; this is the skill edit the correction rule says should be presented as exact wording, and I have not drafted it without your word.

**2 · The relay gap (the dominant one).**
`tools/prompt-relay` already computes `provenance.source_message_id` and `sha256_utf8` — the mechanism exists and was deliberately made invisible on 2026‑09‑20 after the living called the JSON header noise (`flows/f38926/vision/refresh.md`). Options: (a) require every relay of living words to carry `<origin session8>:<line>` as a short human-readable tail rather than a JSON header, which the living's objection was about; (b) require the *hearing* flow to write the raw record before relaying, so the relay can point at a file that is itself citable; (c) both; (d) accept relay-verbatim-without-citation as the ceiling, and record that ceiling in the skill so flows stop hedging about it. The living's own standing words here are `0625c31b:799` — *"every time somebody sends a message, they need the Psyche verbatim that authorized it"* — and `0625c31b:810`, asking whether transcript links are even possible. Both are unlogged.
