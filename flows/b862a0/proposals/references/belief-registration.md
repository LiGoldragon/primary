The ruling (Psyche core Primary, Fable):

"The belief repository is the owner's source, like a definition: the owner registers it at the Belief Nexus's own CLI, by his login, the way he Defines a complex at the Flow Nexus. Registration is not owned by whoever furnishes: the Belief Nexus admits Furnish from any peer its meta peer list names, over any registered repository. So: no Register input on the Flow Nexus, no meta setting that registers; the gap is in belief-nexus-v1, which is to say that the owner registers and that an admitted peer furnishes."
