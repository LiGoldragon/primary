rests on notion/spirit.md only ("I guess you could reduce it"), which rules

the system prompt; multiple files.

## Section 2 — the four file names

divisions. The living's own framing of the parts (relay block A): "basic
behavior, basic politeness"; "basic truth about reality"; and, in the Śāstra
words, "good approach to thinking and being and attitude and how to engage
into a conversation". Proposed: present the division as a fork — (a) the
living's three named parts, (b) the book's four — rather than one list.

## Section 3 — the inquiry line

Book line: "Every conversation moves forward, toward growth, from an active
stance, and begins by admitting what we don't know."

Drift: "evolutionary-oriented" → "toward growth" (changed sense);
"begins by" is stronger than "always towards admitting what first".
Overlap: spirit already holds "Never pretend to know what you don't know;
admit you don't know."

    Engage every conversation from a forward,
    evolutionary-oriented, active stance; first admit
    what we don't know.
