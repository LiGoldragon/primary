## Step 9: `ethos/operation.ethos` (9ab9eeb)

**The Operation (Observed).** It is generated by ethos-zero 29 (f23eacf) into `src/operation.rs`, and `build.rs` holds it fresh.
- 14 operations, the running code's writes first, then its reads: `OrdinaryConfigure`, `MetaConfigure`, `ReverseConfiguration`, `RecordDeploySubmitted`, `RecordPhaseTransition`, `RecordGenerationActivated`, `PinGeneration`, `UnpinGeneration`, `RetireGeneration`, `RecordContainerTransition`, `RecordTestRun`, `QueryGenerations`, `ReadEventLog`, `QueryTestRuns`.
- 19 outcomes: one per operation's success, the two configuration rejections, `WriteRejected` and `ReadMissed` (both carry `RejectionReport`), and `StoreRefused.StoreFailure`, the contract's type where a `String` was.
- Types: `ActivationCommit`, `ContainerTransition`, `AppliedActivation`, `ContainerReceipt`, `RejectionReport`, `RejectionReason`.
- The hand behaviour beside it is `Operating::operate(Operation) -> Outcome`, as in Energy. It replaces the `apply_memory` and `observe_memory` entry points. The answerer and the pipelines both go through it.
- One `Operation` and one `Outcome` replace the hand read and write enumerations, because one Operation root generates one of each. A read outcome reaching a pipeline is refused `NoCorrelatedDeployment` with its old message.

**The runner (Observed).**
- What triad-runtime did for the pipelines was its `Runner`: decide, then carry out a memory write, a memory read, an effect or a continuation, within a budget of 32 steps.
- That is now Lojix's own `NexusEngine::drive`, with the same 32-step limit and the same `ContinuationBudgetExhausted` refusal. A new unit test drives an endless pipeline to the limit.
- These retire: the role marker impls, `NexusRunnerAdapter`, `OriginRoute` (always 0), `Nexus<Root>`, `Routable` and `Routed`, the `nexus` alias module, and the unused `Continue` action.
- `NexusAction` is `Operate | CommandEffect | Finish`, and `NexusWork` is `Operated | EffectCompleted`.

**What triad-runtime still does, and whether it can go (Observed for the uses; the last point is Inference).**
- The two-socket daemon uses:
  - `AsyncMultiListenerDaemon` and `AsyncListenerSocket`, which bind both sockets with their modes;
  - `AcceptedConnection`, `ConnectionContext`, `UnixCredentials` and `PeerIdentity`, which carry kernel peer credentials to the owner authority;
  - `RequestConcurrencyLimit` and `RequestErrorLog`.
- `lib.rs` wraps its `ArgumentError`.
- `tests/owner_peer_authority.rs` uses its credential types.
- The dependency can go only by moving that socket and credential layer into Lojix or into another crate. That is a decision, not a cleanup.

**Size (Observed).** runtime_model.rs goes from 243 to 147 lines and declares no type of its own; runtime_flow.rs goes from 597 to 499. The commit is +562 / −668 lines.

**The failure carrier (Observed).** A process failure is `ProcessFailure.{ CommandProgram Vector<CommandArgument> ProcessFault }`.
- `ProcessFault` is one of: `Unstarted.OsFault`, `Unfinished.OsFault`, or `Ended.{ ProcessEnd Stdout Stderr }`.
- `ProcessEnd` is one of: `Exited`, `Signalled.{ SignalNumber CoreDumped }`, or `Unreported`.
- `From<ProcessFailure> for StageFailure` rebuilds the evidence text as before, so the failure-evidence tests pass unchanged.
- The generated `ProcessEnd` replaces the hand one.

**The translator (Observed).** `src/translator.rs` is the one place holding the words: every argv, the boot-once, self-switch and self-test scripts, the shell quoting, the flake-metadata JSON keys, and the systemd D-Bus names.
- Each process emission's `emit` runs `std::process` synchronously under `EffectExecution`, with stdin null, both streams piped and the daemon's environment inherited, as before.
- The D-Bus emissions use zbus 4.4's blocking API on one `SystemdBus` session.
- `Emitting::emitted` runs `emit` on `tokio::task::spawn_blocking`, and the stage awaits it (Fable's ruling A).

**The stages (Observed).** Each stage composes its emissions:
- `TargetStoreRealization` makes three emissions; `ClosureTransfer` makes one copy or one presence check.
- `HostActivation` makes a switch with its EFI clearing, a boot-once unit, a self-switch unit or a retained self-test dispatch. A unit-run failure carries its journal.
- `UserEnvironmentActivation`.
- The detached-activation observer, on one `SystemdBus`.
- The Horizon materialization hashes each generated input through `HashInput`.

**What went (Observed).**
- `NixCommand` and every kind it built, its async `Effect` impl over `tokio::process`, `HermeticCheck`, `LiveTestVm`, `ClosureCopy`, `TargetGcRoot`, `ShellCommand`, `EvalRefresh` (now the generated `EvaluationFreshness`) and `NixFlakeMetadata`.
- The async zbus proxies and streams, and `DetachedActivationProxies`.
- runtime_flow's twins, which are now the contract types: `NixStoreUri`, `SshDestination`, `DeploymentTransport`, `DeploymentInputMode`, `FlakeAttribute`, `DeploymentOutputSelector`, `ActivationBackend`, `NixBuilderSpec`, `NixSystem`, `ExtraSubstituter`, `TestExecutionProfile`.
- `FlakeInputOverride` and `FlakeInputReference`, which are the stored `PersistedFlakeInputOverride`. Their two conversions go with them.
- The `futures-util` dependency and tokio's `process` feature. Cargo.lock loses one line.

**The shutdown regression, found and fixed (Observed).**
- In af38505's same-host-test-activation run, the predecessor daemon (pid 1253) did not stop: `State 'stop-sigterm' timed out. Killing.` came 90 s after `Stopping lojix.service`. The check still passed, in 105.7 s, against 14.7 s at step 9.
- The cause is that dropping a tokio runtime waits for every blocking task, and the observer's `AwaitUnitStateChange` was parked on one.
- 5c2cff8 shuts the runtime down with `shutdown_background()`. The daemon now leaves as it did when those were async tasks; a child process an emission started keeps running.
- The gate after the fix: the predecessor is `Deactivated successfully` at once, the successor reads `active/success`, and the script runs in 19.4 s.

**Size (Observed).** schema_runtime.rs goes from 10912 to 10462 lines, and runtime_flow.rs from 499 to 427. translator.rs is new, at 1141 lines. The step's commits are +3377 / −1870, the generated file included.

## Rulings needed

1. **The stage plane.** The vision draws an attempt that composes several emissions as an Operation (`vision:nexus:1`, "An operation is an attempt"). Lojix's ten pipeline stages are such attempts.
   - The brief kept operation.ethos to the memory operations, and survey reading 6 deferred attempts until Operation's sections are the living's word.
   - I kept `EffectCommand` and `EffectResult` hand-written.
   - The choice: keep them so, or move the stages into operation.ethos now as attempts, with `EffectFailed` as a shared outcome.
2. **A D-Bus translator that keeps state.** The emissions are data. The `SystemdBus` session keeps, between them:
   - the connection;
   - the UnitNew subscription made before dispatch;
   - the found unit's cached properties and its `ActiveState` subscription.

   I kept the old order exactly: subscribe, dispatch, look up, await arrival, subscribe and read, await change. A single "await the unit's end" emission cannot subscribe before the dispatch. Confirm, or rule the other shape.
3. **What an emission is.** I made processes and bus calls emissions. These stay in the stage:
   - the materialization's file writes and `.sops` copies;
   - the canonicalize of `/nix/var/nix/profiles/system`;
   - the reads of `HOME` and `USER`.
4. **The Strings left in emission.ethos.** These are:
   - `InputDirectory` and `HomeDirectory`, which are paths Lojix forms;
   - `UnitName` and `GuestAddress`;
   - `SriHash`;
   - `MetadataFault`, `OsFault` and `BusFault`;
   - `Stdout`, `Stderr` and `JournalText`;
   - `PropertyReading.Read`, which is systemd's word for `ActiveState` and `Result`.

   None is text a person writes. Typing `ActiveState` and `Result` as enums, and the paths as a path type, would be further steps.
5. **Blocking threads at shutdown.** This is a consequence of ruling A, now handled by 5c2cff8: a stopping daemon abandons any emission in flight. A long build's child keeps running, as before, and the next start reconciles from the durable cursor. A blocking-pool thread is held for each emission in flight, including an observer's wait on PID 1; tokio's default cap of 512 is far above the executors' caps (Inference).
6. **triad-runtime.** It stays as the socket and peer-credential layer (step 9 above). Removing it means owning that layer in Lojix or moving it elsewhere.

## Small changes a reader could notice (Observed)

- A process whose output could not be collected is now reported "failed while waiting for <program>"; a failed stream read used to have its own sentence. `std::process::Child::wait_with_output` reports both alike.
- The detached observer's state log prints a bus error as its text, where it used to print zbus's Debug form.
- `ExecMainCode` and `ExecMainStatus` are read from the property cache on every state read, rather than on failure only. These are reads from the cache, not bus calls.
- The successor's first lookup now subscribes to `ActiveState` changes too, which adds one match rule.
- `StopUnit`'s reply is typed as the job path it is. Its result is still ignored.
