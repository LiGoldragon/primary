## Settled, by the vision or a prior ruling

1. **Stored order wins over wire order.** The wire breaks anyway for positional pairing, so its 17 enums take the stored (lifecycle, senior-first) order. The Memory then keeps its bytes. Generation keeps its optional closure path; RequestedGenerationArtifact folds into GenerationArtifact; the deploy-job record's upgrade is written and proved on its fixture.
2. **Query's three answers become variations at the root** (vision:nexus:0). `Query.Selection` answers `Queried`; `QueryDeploymentEvents` and `QueryTestRuns` are inputs of their own; each watch has its own output, in the past tense.
3. **WatchCacheRetention leaves.** It registers no watcher and nothing ever streams from it.
4. **The daemon answers through `input.answer(&answerer)`** (Orchestrate). NexusWork, NexusAction, the ingress and egress twins and triad-runtime retire, and with them the generic `Routed<Root>` and `Nexus<Root>` that Ethos cannot declare.
5. **operation.ethos follows the running code**: the 11 writes and 3 reads with their outcomes (Energy).
6. **Quarantine retires** (vision:memory:0, upgraded, never accommodated). An upgrade empties the quarantined-row family and reports what it drops. The raw-byte type Ethos lacks is then not needed.
7. **The offline tools are in scope** as the last steps: bootstrap, its journal Memory, and the legacy startup archive, which retires.
8. **DeploymentPhase and DeploymentLifecycle are one type** (a fact held twice).
9. **DeployAccepted stays**: an acknowledgement is the vision's own answer, and "Deployed" would be false at that moment.
10. **Memory keys, family names, schema version and upgrade bodies stay hand-written** beside the generated records (Orchestrate).
11. **The generator fault: ethos-zero 28 accepts an enum of one variant**, which the vision refuses; ingress has five. I fix it in ethos-zero 29 and tell Mind flowNexus Opus before its main moves.

## Questions

**A. Async effects and async answers.** ethos-zero 28 generates a synchronous `emit` and a synchronous `Answering`. Lojix's effects are builds, copies and activations that run for minutes, over tokio processes and D-Bus, and its `submit_test` is async. Three ways:
- (a) a synchronous `emit` run on a blocking thread, with std::process and zbus's blocking API, which is Curriculum's shape;
- (b) ethos-zero generates an async `emit` and an async `Answering`;
- (c) the Emission is carried by a translator process, as vision:nexus:1 draws it.

My reading: (a) now, because it is the smallest step and changes no generator, and (c) as the horizon. Answering stays synchronous, because admission writes the store and hands the job to its actor, as it does today.

**B. A foreign contract type in Lojix's Memory.** HorizonDefinition comes from horizon-lib, which is generated by ethos-zero 16. It is held both in Lojix's Signal and in its Memory. A layout change in that other repository then changes Lojix's stored bytes, and that is the reason quarantine exists. My reading: horizon-lib moves to 28 as its own step. Its 37 aliases become newtypes, and the bytes stay equal. Lojix's Memory then holds it inside a record of its own (`StoredLock.{ Lock }`), pinned by revision, so any layout change reaches Lojix only through a repin that carries an upgrade. Is a foreign type in Memory acceptable on those terms, or should Lojix's Memory declare its own copy of the definition?

**C. A subscription's stream.** `WatchDeployments` is answered once, and then `DeploymentPhaseObserved` frames stream on the same connection. The generated dispatch ties nothing to the subscription that asked for them, so the stream stays hand-written beside it, as a reaction output past the last input. My reading: accept that until Ethos has a subscription form (vision:nexus:0, "Observation by subscription").
