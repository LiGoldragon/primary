## Every flow only knows what it's told

Context: the living, to Psyche core Secondary 97bb3e, 2026-10-24, opening a change of tack toward reanimating the Psyche nexus.

> As you may or may not know (because this is going to be the subject), every flow only knows what it's told. We're kind of trying to emulate a thinking being but it's a thinking machine. I wouldn't say that it has volition, ambition, desire, or any of those things, which form a part of intelligence. We can't really use the word intelligence but it does think, or seems to.

## The system cannot tell what to remember, nor adapt its views to new input

> As you may have noticed (and this is sort of related to the topic at hand here), the system right now has a really hard time knowing what to remember and how to remember it. It also has a really hard time knowing how to adapt its views, which are represented by the context modules, to newly arriving input. How does the new stimuli affect the machine's views, which are its context? How is it incorporated, propagated, and recalled?

## Psyche, the nexus that manages the machines' context, is reanimated

> We know we have the harnesses skill interface, which is why we're using it because it's there, but it's a very unsophisticated system. We have a project which used to be called Spirit, which should have been changed to Psyche. I'm not talking about the psyche context repo or stuff like that. I'm talking about a psyche component, a nexus, software that was the original approach to managing the machines' context.

## An anatomy maps the machine's mechanisms to their human equivalents

> I guess I would like to decide on an ontology or an anatomy of the mechanical names and their sort of pseudo-human equivalent. That way we can use the anatomy of human consciousness/mind/psyche to map out the beginning of an anatomy of the thinking machine's equivalent mechanism. We're going to reanimate Psyche and it's going to be a whole lot more sophisticated eventually than using these context module files and all this jury rigging, duct taping, and hay wiring.

## The judge is several things in several places

> The judge is going to not just be one thing. It's going to be several things in several places and will probably use different kinds of models, such as system 1 models (which didn't exist or were not as prevalent as they are now when I was designing and making this).
