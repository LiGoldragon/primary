# A flow starts with what its purpose needs

Created by Fable 5.1, on the living's words, from a reading of every permaflow's bindings. Edition 2 carries his two amendments: the skill as a possibility, and placement as weight.

### Proposal 1: The start set and the skills · psyche-belief › vision/belief-modules.md

::: comment
Today 22 of 25 permaflows start with vision:ethos, vision:datom and vision:flow-nexus, fifteen thousand tokens, whatever their topic; the eleven Mind flows share one list of 24,000 tokens, and the Psyche core flows carry 46,000. Every module a flow is not given at start is still reachable: it is a skill, listed by its description and loaded when the work meets its subject. So a flow starts with what every main flow needs and what its own purpose needs, and the rest waits as skills. The default set is about 7,000 tokens; a specialized Sonnet, Haiku, Luna or Terra flow starts near it.
:::

+ A flow starts with the modules every main flow needs, the spirit, the intent, how it messages and searches, the vocabulary, the roles of the models and the aspects, with its aspect's module and its role, and with the vision of its own topic. Every other module can be made available as a skill, listed to the flow by its description and loaded when its work meets the subject.

+ A module's placement is its weight: the system prompt binds hardest, the first prompt next, a skill least. What a flow is to hold as authority is placed high: the spirit and the intent in the system prompt, the vision a flow implements in its first prompt, and a settled vision may move up to the system prompt. What a flow may only need sits as a skill.
