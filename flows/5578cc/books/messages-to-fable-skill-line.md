# Messages to Fable, as a skill line

You told Psyche Fable to stop receiving messages that have nothing to do with it. Right now that order lives only in Fable's records and in my log. No skill carries it, so every flow launched later will keep sending Fable lock notices and receipts. It reaches the flows only as a line in a skill.

## Where the line goes

Every flow that messages another flow loads the same skill first: the one for the messenger's commands (`compensation-messenger-clj`). A line there is read at the moment a flow is about to send, by every flow, of every aspect.

## The line, two ways

**1. For Fable only.**

> Send Psyche Fable only a question that needs the living's word, a result it asked for, or the living's own words. Lock notices, release events, receipts, completion reports, reviews and acknowledgements go to Mind, or to no one.

**2. For every flow, with Fable as its strictest case.**

> A message costs its receiver money and context; send a flow only what it must act on. Send Psyche Fable only a question that needs the living's word, a result it asked for, or the living's own words. Lock notices, release events, receipts, completion reports, reviews and acknowledgements go to Mind, or to no one.

Choice 2 would also stop the same traffic between the other seats. Today, for example, a lock release gets announced to every seat waiting on it.
