# Messages to Fable, in vision-flow

You said the rule on messages to Fable is a vision skill, not a compensation skill. A vision skill says what you want a part of the system to be. The compensation skills only work around how today's tools behave, so the rule doesn't belong there.

## Where it goes

The vision skill for Flow (`vision-flow`) already holds who speaks to whom: "Speech runs horizontally between aspects at equal rank and vertically within an aspect one rung at a time … Sol speaks to Opus, not to Fable; Fable is spoken to least." The new line goes right after that sentence.

## The line, two ways

**1. For Fable only.**

> Fable receives only a question that needs the living's word, a result it asked for, or the living's own words; lock notices, release events, receipts, completion reports, reviews and acknowledgements go to Mind, or to no one.

**2. For every flow, with Fable as its strictest case.**

> A message costs its receiver money and context, so a flow receives only what it must act on. Fable receives only a question that needs the living's word, a result it asked for, or the living's own words; lock notices, release events, receipts, completion reports, reviews and acknowledgements go to Mind, or to no one.

This replaces the earlier book «Messages to Fable, as a skill line», which put the line in the wrong skill.

Answer by commenting 1 or 2, or with your own wording.
