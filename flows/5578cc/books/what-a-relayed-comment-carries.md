# What a relayed comment carries

You saw your comments passed between seats buried in long links, times and labels:

> You really fucked up. You sent them giant hashes.

> And a bunch of timestamps and all a bunch of fucking useless garbage

No skill says what may go beside your words when a seat passes them on, so a seat filled in whatever its tools returned. My own messages did the same: commit ids, session ids, links. I have stopped.

## The line

It goes in the skill every seat loads when it talks with you or passes on your words, replacing the line that only says to keep the context short:

> Quote the living's words verbatim. Beside them send only what the reader needs to understand them: for a comment, the book's title and the few words he anchored it to. Times, ids, links, labels and correction notes are sent only when the receiver asked for them.

That skill is one of yours, so it changes only on your word.

Comment "ok", or give your wording.
