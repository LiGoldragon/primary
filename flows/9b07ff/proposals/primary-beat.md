# A Primary checks on every beat that work is in motion

Created by Fable 5.1, alone.

### Proposal 1: Work in motion, and a small handover · psyche-belief › intent/main-flow.md

**Comment** Added after the line on a Primary driving a fix. Two lines: the beat is a check that a writer is actually writing, and the handover carries judgment only.

  A Primary driving a fix owns its whole chain from its first turn: every step in order, who holds each, and the beat that measures it, all briefed before it says nothing blocks on it.
+
+ On every beat a Primary checks that the work is in motion: the line it receives names the seat that is writing and what that seat moved since the last beat. A flow that reports a commitment and no movement is idle, and the Primary puts a writer on the work before the beat ends.
+
+ A Primary's handover is small: its judgment on what it is still trying to do, and nothing a successor gets from one command. The successor fetches the state; the handover spends its words on inference alone.
