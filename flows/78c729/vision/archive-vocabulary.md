## Drifting context

Context: choosing among candidate terms (loose, carried, drifting context) for context passed from flow to flow by handover and messaging until it is lost.

"Yeah I think I like drifting context. Let's use drifting context. ... We have different kinds: you have good drifting context and bad drifting context. Usually when we say drifting context, I mean it depends on the context. No pun intended. If something is good and it's drifting context, the bad thing is that it's not actually made permanent. If it's bad then the good thing is that it's not made permanent and would disappear if you started a new flow.

It depends on what the situation is. There would be two kinds of drifting context, one of which is not as much of a problem in terms of whether it's a problem to lose it. In fact the bad drifting context, we would seek to lose it."
-- psyche, STT.
