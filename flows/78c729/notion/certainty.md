## Certainty, importance and term on psyche lines

Context: a fix to book rendering recurred after being lost; the living turns over why fixes are taken out.

"We have a reoccurring failure, which means that there's a deeper failure whereby the instructions, the fix that we would put in, end up being taken out. That is problematic so we maybe have to introduce this. This is why I had intent and vision to have different levels of certainty on instructions but it might not actually be enough. We might even need a certainty index or even more stuff.

It's kind of like this [Sema] language, the language of meaning, the syntax of meaning, whereby you can annotate, mark up any object, add notes, and then add notes on the notes, and so on, with all kinds of metadata: certainty levels, emotional commitment, lucidity level, and all kinds of crazy things that I don't even want to mention because it might confuse you.

I think we might need to introduce some kind of concepts like that in our vision landing so that we don't end up taking a line that's actually really important and quite certain (because it's sort of all treated homogeneously). There are some things that we are more clear about, more certain about. There are things that are more important or less important. There are things that are shorter-term or longer-term. There are things that are possible ideas, probable ideas, quite probable, and almost certain, and we're not differentiating between them."
-- psyche, STT. Transcription corrected: "sema" → "Sema".

## Context that rolls loose until it is lost

Context: the living reflects on a book-rendering fix that was lost, and on all psyche data being treated alike.

"I mean in a way, I kind of pointed out the most obvious problem here, which is the homogeneity of all that data, with all the data being treated the same. Well even though in this particular case it might have been something quite different (it might have just been that it was just a comment passed over from Flow to Flow by handover and by messaging), we could probably develop a vocabulary to talk about that kind of accidental training or I'm not sure. I'm trying to think of terminology here that would be adequate to talk about that kind of context that gets thrown around until it isn't anymore. Then it's just sort of like this ball that was rolling left and right on the dash of the car and then one day it just flew out the window because nobody tied it down."
-- psyche, STT.
