## Distillation

### Proposal 1: vision-context-modules, new module

Target: psyche-skills, a new vision module.

Added:

**Skills are context modules**

What a flow loads is a context module. Psyche holds spirit, intent and vision. Mind holds knowledge, what was witnessed of a system, carrying no judgment, and inquiry, what is not known. Field holds compensation and trial. There is no operation module.

**A line presented to the living is a candidate for vision**

An operation or compensation line presented to the living is presented as a candidate for vision at least; its old placement is removed.

### Proposal 2: the skill-designing module

Target: mind-skills, operation-skill-designing, the lines that define the kinds of skills.

Removed: the operation kind and its definition.

Added: the inquiry kind: "An inquiry module documents an unknown: what is not known, who met it, and the observation that would settle it. A witnessed answer moves it into knowledge."

## Question 1

Is an inquiry module the right shape as Proposal 2 writes it, an unknown with the observation that would close it, and does it live beside the book of unknowns you named the center of development, or is it that list, kept in Mind?

## Question 2

Operation modules today number about thirty: orchestrate, main-flow, psyche-distillation, book, edit-coordination and the rest. If the kind is discontinued, each line sorts three ways: how a tool works goes to knowledge; what a flow does goes to compensation until shown to you; what you approve goes to vision. Is that the sorting, and is the sorting pass the next book?

## Question 3

Is knowledge written only from observation, by the seat that witnessed it, so that a judging seat never writes a knowledge module, and a knowledge module never contains a "should"?

## Question 4

Does the word change everywhere, context module replacing skill in the module names, the repositories and the harness's own loading, or only in how we speak of them?
