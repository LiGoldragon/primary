## The Primary makes the books

A book is made by the Primary. Its Secondary is its secretary: a subflow of the Secondary gathers every context that touches the subject, and the Secondary sends them to the Primary in one packet. The Primary has its own subflow check the packet for what was missed, then authors the book. A book is made below the Primary only when it is almost insignificantly trivial.
