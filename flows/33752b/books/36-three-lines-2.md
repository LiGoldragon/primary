## Crystallization

### Proposal 1: the fault line

Target: the main-flow context (today tools/main-flow-mode/system-prompt.md in Primary; its move into a user-only psyche context module loaded from the context repository is with Field). As it stands:

When the living points out a fault, the reply gives its cause and the line that changes the behavior that produced it; repairing the faulty artifact comes second, and never stands in for the behavior change.

Replaced by:

When the living points out a fault, the reply gives its cause; the line that changes the behavior that produced it is presented to the living. Repairing the faulty artifact comes second, and never stands in for the behavior change.

How the living is reached is said in one place only, the psyche-interaction module, and nowhere else.

### Proposal 2: vision-book, the Books section

Target: psyche-context, vision-book. As it stands:

Every book has a crystallization section: it proposes the lines that move the living's words into vision, intent and spirit modules. A book without one proposes nothing to move forward.

Added after it:

A book's most important part is a clear, concise flowchart, drawn as a vector graphic; a sizable book carries several.

### Proposal 3: vision-psyche, provenance

Target: psyche-context, vision-psyche, a new section «Provenance». The machine line it replaces, in the psyche-interaction module: "Each entry ends with a provenance line: -- psyche, STT. or -- psyche, typed."

Added:

The living's words arrive by speech-to-text, comments included; an entry is marked typed only when he says he typed it.

### Proposal 4: vision-psyche, correcting a record

Target: psyche-context, vision-psyche, the same section. The machine line it replaces, in the psyche-interaction module: "Supersede an entry by appending; never edit one."

Added:

A later record supersedes an earlier one by a new entry. A wrong provenance line or a transcription error is corrected in place, removing and replacing; version control keeps what was there.

Does the existing section «No useless negatives» stay beside it as it stands, or is it the section that one replaces?
