## Distillation

### Proposal 1: vision-context, new module in psyche-context

Added:

**Context modules**

What a flow loads is a context module. Psyche holds spirit, intent and vision. Mind holds knowledge, what was witnessed of a system, and inquiry, what is not known and what would settle it. Field holds compensation.

**A line presented to the living is a candidate for vision**

A compensation line presented to the living is presented as a candidate for vision at least; its old placement is removed.

### Proposal 2: the skill-designing module, the kinds

Removed: the definitions of the operation and trial kinds.

Added: "An inquiry module documents an unknown: what is not known, who met it, and the observation that would settle it. A witnessed answer moves it into knowledge."

## Work that follows landing

Every operation and trial line is sorted: how a tool works goes to knowledge; what a flow does goes to compensation; what you have approved goes to vision, as the cleanup's second edition already proposes for thirty-one lines. The emptied types are deleted. The repositories are renamed psyche-context, mind-context and field-context; Field has that order now.
