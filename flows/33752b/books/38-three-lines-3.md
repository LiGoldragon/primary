## Crystallization

### Proposal 1: the fault line

Target: the main-flow context, intent-main-flow in psyche context, which the flow-starting tool will read from there once it takes its EDN configuration. As it stands:

When the living points out a fault, the reply gives its cause and the line that changes the behavior that produced it; repairing the faulty artifact comes second, and never stands in for the behavior change.

Replaced by:

When the living points out a fault, the reply gives its cause; the line that changes the behavior that produced it is presented to the living. Repairing the faulty artifact comes second, and never stands in for the behavior change.

### Proposal 2: vision-psyche, the living's words

Target: psyche context, vision-psyche, a new section «The living's words». The machine line it replaces, in the psyche-interaction module: "Each entry ends with a provenance line: -- psyche, STT. or -- psyche, typed."

Added:

The living's words arrive through speech-to-text and may be corrupted by it. An entry carries no mark of typing.

### Proposal 3: vision-psyche, a correction

Target: psyche context, vision-psyche, the same section. The machine line it replaces, in the psyche-interaction module: "Supersede an entry by appending; never edit one."

Added:

A correction removes and replaces. An addition is not a correction. Version control keeps what was there.
