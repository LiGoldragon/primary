Distillation

1

spirit-core, new line

psyche-skills, spirit-core, after the nuts-and-bolts line

No values in the runtime. The code is the machine that takes the configuration and turns it into the running system; every path, host, id, name, timeout, model and policy is fed in, and every feature has its switch. The one hard fact is the structure of the language itself. A constant that truly is structure stays.

2

vision-flow, new section

psyche-skills, vision-flow, after its last section

Everything that launches, names, delivers to, stops or retires a flow goes through Flow

A launcher script, a messenger or a hook calls Flow for these; none does them itself.

Rulings

  1. Figure 2 shows Flow bypassed rather than lacking: its source already launches, delivers, stops, retires and hooks, but the running Nexus is twelve versions behind the source, the CLI is in between, and nothing in Primary calls any of them. Is the next step to cut Primary over to Flow, moving launch, lifecycle and hooks out of the launcher scripts and the messenger into calls to Flow, before any other finding here is fixed?

  2. The two Herdr patches are welds into a component that is not ours. The quiet-hours patch is used by nothing now; the allowlist patch holds a Flow fact, which binary a flow runs. Are both removed, with the allowlist rebuilt as Flow's own check before Flow calls Herdr's unpatched start?

  3. The model and effort per layer live in five places that disagree. Is the one place the skill variables file, with the voice table, the Curriculum roles, the CriomOS-home list and Flow's defaults all reading from it, or is the one place Flow's configuration?

  4. The reminder hook and the Field heartbeat are the two cars that start when they have gas. Do both get a switch and their values fed in now, as the first application of Proposal 1, or does that wait for the Flow cutover, since both would be Flow's to run?

  5. Flow's Report, Stop and lock release accept the flow id the payload names, with no check of who sent it. Is a peer check a design of its own, to be brought to you as a book before anything is built, or a correction to make now?
