## Distillation

### Proposal 1: intent-psyche, new section

Target: psyche-skills, intent-psyche, after «Vision is short-term or long-term».

Added:

**What the living reviews is psyche**

Anything the living has reviewed is psyche: a line he approves is a vision statement at least, whatever module proposed it, and leaves the mind or field module it sat in. A line he has not reviewed is a machine line.

### Proposal 2: intent-psyche, the line between spirit and intent

Target: psyche-skills, intent-psyche, beside «What the living reviews is psyche». The ladder in knowledge-psyche reads "**Spirit** — philosophy. Almost never changes."; it stays, and the rule itself is yours:

Added:

**Spirit holds beyond this system**

Spirit is philosophy that holds beyond this system. A rule about this system, however broad, is intent at most, on its subject.

Why: this flow placed a rule about vision in spirit twice; the ladder marks spirit only as higher, not as universal.
