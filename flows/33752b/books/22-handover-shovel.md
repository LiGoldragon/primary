# Handover and the seat without a shovel

## Figure 1: what a handover carries

```
   old flow at its limit
   +---------------------------+
   |        handover           |
   |                           |
   |  judgment    |  lookup    |
   |  -----------  |  --------  |
   |  what I hold | what was   |
   |  open and    | committed  |
   |  why         | and pushed |
   |  what I read | which      |
   |  in his      | files are  |
   |  unsettled   | dirty      |
   |  words       | which      |
   |  what I      | books are  |
   |  would do    | out        |
   |  next, and   | the queue  |
   |  what not    |            |
   |      |       |     |      |
   |   written    |  one tool  |
   |   by the     |  call by   |
   |   flow       |  the       |
   |              |  successor |
   +---------------------------+
   today: trial-succession says one
   vision block, three flows back;
   operation-main-flow says summary
   = subflows in order, what each did
```

## Distillation

### Proposal 1: operation-main-flow, the summary becomes a handover

Target: mind-skills, operation-main-flow, section «Flow summary».

Removed: When asked to summarize the flow, the main flow writes `summary.md` in `FLOW_DIRECTORY`. Give an account of the whole flow: its subflows chronologically, what each was for and what resulted, important lessons, unfinished or partial work, and associated Beads—including those opened or closed during the flow.

Added: When told to hand over, the main flow writes `handover.md` in `FLOW_DIRECTORY`: only what takes judgment. What it holds open and why; how it reads the living's words that are not yet settled; what it would do next and what it would not, and why. Nothing a tool call returns: no chronology, no commits, no paths, no states.

### Proposal 2: trial-succession, the successor's start

Target: field-skills, trial-succession, the line "Carry one concentrated vision block with the verbatim psyche from the last three flows."

Removed: Carry one concentrated vision block with the verbatim psyche from the last three flows.

Added: A successor starts from its predecessor's handover and one tool-made sheet of the lookups: dirty paths, open books, the queue.

### Proposal 3: vision-flow, new section

Target: psyche-skills, vision-flow, after the section on waking and the queue.

Added:

**A Fable seat holds no shovel**

A Fable Flow has hardly any tool. The questions it poses in a turn are forwarded by hooks, researched and answered by lower seats, and returned in its queue at its next turn beside new psyche, results and anything else queued for it. Its context goes to judging, inferring and summarizing.

## Question 1

Is the handover of Figure 1 the right split: the flow writes only its judgments, open matters and its reading of your unsettled words, and the successor gets every fact from one tool call? Proposal 1 writes that split into the main-flow skill, replacing the chronological summary.

## Question 2

Does the handover still carry your verbatim words? The flow's psyche records already hold them; the successor can read those files. If the handover carries only the flow's judgment about them, Proposal 2 drops the verbatim block entirely.

## Question 3

What does the Fable Flow keep? Writing its own records and its book is judgment; sending a message is a shovel the hook could hold. Does it keep only the writes of its own lane, with hooks publishing the book and sending its replies, or does it keep the messenger too?

## Question 4

Who answers a forwarded question: its Secondary, which packages for it today, or any lower seat whose topic it is, chosen by the hook? And does an answer come back as a result in the queue, one of the five request types already in vision-flow, or as a type of its own?

## Question 5

Is there one Fable Flow, or one per aspect? If more than one, does each have its own queue and hooks, or one queue sorted to seats by topic?
