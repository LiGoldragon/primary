## Distillation

### Proposal 1: spirit-core, new line

Target: psyche-skills, spirit-core, after "The build target is the design than which none better is possible …".

Added: The envisioned is the goalpost and never a blocker; the makeshift is what is done for now. A flow holds both and never reads their difference as a contradiction.

### Proposal 2: knowledge-vocabulary, two terms

Target: mind-skills, knowledge-vocabulary.

Added: Makeshift: the non-ideal shape chosen for now, a vision until it is built. Envisioned: the eventual state, the goalpost.

## Question 1

Is every vision statement marked envisioned or makeshift, or only where the two differ for the same subject, with the unmarked statement read as envisioned?
