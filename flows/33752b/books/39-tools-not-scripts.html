Tools instead of scripts

Mind Tools surveyed the transcripts of both harnesses, a relayed count: in the Claude transcripts about two thousand inline Python calls, thirteen thousand heredocs, twelve thousand text-construction commands; in the Codex transcripts twenty-nine thousand executed commands. The counts overlap and measure recurrence, not missing tools. What recurs, by kind: reading transcripts and task outputs; selecting a pane or a profile from a roster; projecting a version or a digest from a derivation or a binary; assembling a page by hand.

Crystallization

1

one transcript query, across every root

the transcript tool, Mind

The declared transcript shim is Claude-only and absent from the path; the extractor has provenance but not the query. Short term: one query surface over both harnesses and all three roots, returning a command paired with its result, its status, its source line and the flow's public identity from the existing registry. Long term, from your words: transcript data moves into a Nexus database, and files stop proliferating.

2

identity and launch preview through Flow

Flow and the census, Mind Core

The capability exists; what is missing is a callable surface by the public three-field identity, returning ambiguity and absence explicitly, and a validate-or-preview entry that reuses the launcher's parsing with no side effect. Runtime ids stay internal.

3

declared, installed, running: one evidence tool

component-evidence, Field

Give it a tool-inventory input: source path and revision, expected command, resolved executable, version probe, observed digest, active configuration, bounded witness; unknown and missing stated as such. First finding it would have caught: the skill variables declare two Codex transcript roots holding nine old files, while the live root, unnamed anywhere, holds a hundred and ninety-eight.

4

structured task results in the book pipeline

the book renderer and publisher, the publication owner

Expose structured task-result retrieval and reusable page assembly in the existing pipeline; the hand assembly witnessed in one of this flow's own book subflows is the case.

5

what is not built

No general safe-edit tool, no generic probe runner, no second identity registry, no blanket script cleanup: ordinary parser tests, apply_patch, shell utilities and the standing publisher cover what the survey saw, and nothing inspected was shown unused.
