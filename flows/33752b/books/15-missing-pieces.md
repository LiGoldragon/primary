## Questions

Question 1. The complete concept. For a design in this system, what is the golden system it is judged against: the Ethos of the whole (signal, operation, memory), the anatomy of the idea as asked in the psyche skill, or a written model made per topic before designing?

Question 2. Rittel's objection: what one needs to know depends on one's idea of the solution. Is the list of unknowns made once before design, or remade at every edition as the idea sharpens?

Question 3. Where does the list of unknowns live so a later flow finds it: as the questions of a first edition only, in the flow log, or as a record of its own beside the design?

Question 4. When a question is unanswered, does the flow wait for you, or proceed on an assumption it states? Spec Kit gates the build on no open marker; another current guide says proceed and document the assumption.

Question 5. Who judges that the pieces are enough: the author, the reviewing Primary, or Jev as the judge of what enters?

Question 6. You said this sits really high in the system prompt. Spirit already says «Never pretend to know what you don't know; admit you don't know.» Is the concept below spirit, universal, or intent, because it names this system's golden model?

Question 7. For the second edition, which should be read deeper: Pólya, Brooks, Alexander, Dewar, Parnas, Rittel, or the machine work on asking and abstaining?
