The five stages a startup must pass

Each stage either advances cleanly or fails leaving specific, named evidence behind — never a silent retry.

StageMust be establishedA failure preserves
ComposeOrdered source material, valid skill selections, content identitiesThe requested composition and the exact unresolved source
Create & bindNative session, Flow identity, aspect, model, power, directory, pane and terminal agreeThe partial session and its binding evidence
SubmitOne intended initial submission accepted by the exact empty native sessionThe attempt and its native acceptance evidence
ExpandRequired bodies and launch context appear in the transcript, correct orderThe specific missing, changed, duplicate, or misplaced part
ReadyIdentity/title readback, accepted context, required routing gates passEvery unresolved gate; predecessor remains available

An accepted skill reference is not an expansion receipt. A process existing is not a binding receipt. A title is not identity. A machine saying “ready” is not independent evidence for the gates above it.

Refresh without splitting the conversation

The predecessor stays available until the successor's readiness gates actually pass — a refresh is never ready merely because another pane exists, and retiring a route needs its own separate authority.

What messaging actually is, right now

  • No “message everyone.” Every recipient is resolved individually, by exact binding — busy is not unavailable, and an ambiguous identity is never permission to pick a different one.

  • A pane is not a route. A ready terminal in Herdr does not by itself establish a usable registration for durable messaging.

  • Fallback is explicit, not a workaround:

    “That's okay. You're all allowed to bypass failing messages and send each other straight into your panes. I just want you guys to be able to communicate with fallback.”

Did committing discard psyche?

Complete coverage of every historical utterance, unavailable session, and word never recorded at all remains unknown; that is a different, open question from whether anything was destroyed.
