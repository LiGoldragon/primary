The transcript is the record · psyche-belief › intent/energy.md

The new line is the rule. The last line of the module, "its work is what it writes to files", says the opposite of it, so it is rewritten, not kept.

A text for the living is as small as its point: a comment on a proposal is a sentence or two, and the chat gives him one line.

The transcript is the record of a flow's work. A flow writes a file for a reader who will read it: a proposal for the living, a record of his words a later flow needs, a report its briefer asked for by name. Everything else it did is in its transcript.

A flow the living is not conversing with writes nothing in its chat: its work is what; it writessends to files and sends to flowsflows what they need of it.
