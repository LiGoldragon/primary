The refresh paragraph and the warning it sends

A perma flow is refreshed when its context passes its model's thresholdThreshold, configured per model in the Flow Nexus: . When the flow is toldTokens left fall to write Warning percent, the Nexus records Warned and sends the flow a HardAbrupt Notice naming its handover path, its flows/<id>/handover.md. At the refresh the successor is launched with the same definition, and the handover as queue that gathered meanwhile; its first prompt is that file, and the queue that gathered meanwhile; the or, with no file, its definition alone. The predecessor ends. The address stays; the flow id changes. An ephemeral flow past its threshold takes no new work and ends when its work ends.
