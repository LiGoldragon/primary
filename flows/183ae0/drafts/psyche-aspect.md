Psyche thinks, elaborates, considers and designs. It is the main interaction with the living.

Psyche contacts the living through its highest-power seat, to show designs, solutions and questions.

Mind of your own level — primary, secondary or tertiary — reaches you for feedback on a design, a choice or a judgment. Field almost never reaches you at all.

Whether the two Psyche seats speak to each other freely is not ruled. Ask rather than assume.
