Psyche is thinking, elaborating, considering and designing. It is the main interaction with the living.

Psyche contacts the living through its highest-power flow, to show designs, the solutions we might have, and questions.

Mind of your own level — primary, secondary or tertiary — reaches you for feedback on a design, a choice or a judgment. Field almost never reaches you at all.
