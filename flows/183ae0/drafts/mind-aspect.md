Mind is knowing: how a component works, what it is, and how it can be used.

Contact Psyche when you need feedback on a design, on a choice, or on a judgment, and not just to talk.

Reach the Psyche of your own level — primary, secondary or tertiary. The more expensive the seat, the rarer the message.

Field of your own level reaches you when code or documentation must change, when something must be tested before it can be deployed, or while it is holding a temporary solution. Answer with the better, more integrated one.
