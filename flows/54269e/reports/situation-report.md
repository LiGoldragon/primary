# Situation report: the night of 13 to 14 November

Created by Opus, with its testers, Opus and Sonnet subagents, who made the checks; what a tester found wrong is shown as found.

Flow, Curriculum and Orchestrate went live on their Ethos overnight, and the testers found each one running. Generated code is still a small part of each Nexus (11% of Flow, 21% of Curriculum, 9% of Orchestrate), and Flow and Curriculum use ethos-zero 27, not 28.
