psyche-context › vision/flow.md

In place of Proposal 12. A topic is an extendable enum with Core as its one fixed variant, so core is written capitalized.

Flow manages metaflows. A metaflow is one address, { Aspect Topic Layer }, that continues through several flows one after another; the flow is its current run, and what is addressed is always the metaflow, never a flow. The default topic is coreCore: the permanent topic, the thing that keeps the machine going, the last topic to stand.

A topic's name is Core, or a coinage: a camelCase identifier, uncapitalized. A flow's id is the hash of the harness's session id, kept to six characters and lengthened when two collide, so a transcript is found from the flow id. Where the harness lets Flow choose the session id, Flow chooses it before the flow starts and the flow reads it in its prompt; where the harness assigns it, Flow learns it when the flow binds. No flow declares its id: every call to Flow is identified by the calling process. Anybody may call Flow, a flow for its own refresh among them: because the CLI checks the calling process, a flow refreshes itself safely.

Every flow has a topic; a flow with no special topic has the topic coreCore, the heart of its aspect. A topic is a core:Name, an extendable enum whose one fixed variant is Core; any other topic is a camelCaseExpressioncoinage, checked when created. A flow's title is one datom struct of its aspect, topic, layer and flow id: { Psyche coreCore Secondary <id> }.
