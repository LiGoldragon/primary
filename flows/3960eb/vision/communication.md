## A figure branches; its nodes link

Context: comment on the linear figure in «Flow: CLI, deployment, long term», 2nd edition.

> If it's only a straight line going from one thing to another, there's no fork. There's no possible option. It's not really a flowchart. Do you even have a point? Show us something a bit more complex than that. Show us that there's actual understanding in here and not just sort of boring, linear, low-density signal. We're going to need to pile up a lot of them and then they won't even relate to each other because there's no branching.
>
> If there's no branching then all of your trees are just one trunk, no branch, and there's no hole. Nothing is linked together so things need to link. All the things need to link together through these vertices, right? We need vertices between the nodes. We need more nodes, more vertices. We need to see stuff. Let's get something changed there in the context soon about this.

-- psyche, STT, 2026-10-14, book comment, relayed by Psyche Core Secondary.
