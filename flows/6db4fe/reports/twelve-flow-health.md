## Sustainable reporting pattern — proposed, not running

For each cell, hold an authoritative designated occupant and role/profile separately from observed native thread, Herdr tuple, HM binding generation, and any process instance. Begin with a snapshot and then consume native/Flow/HM changes as events. Record duty and current task as explicit claims with owner and time; record responsiveness, context, emitted token usage, account quota, and availability as separate observations with source, freshness, and unknown/conflict states. A later user input or compaction invalidates a context proxy. A duplicate candidate, stale registration, or crossover remains visible without automatic retirement. A missing report event is a gap, not permission to wake or launch.

Field ultra-low can compile the small recurring report, Field high coordinates repairs, Mind owns the Nexus query/response/process and SEMA record contract, and Psyche receives content when the living asks. Delivery records should distinguish submitted, transported, presented, read, and completed against the exact target binding. This division is a proposed reporting workflow, not evidence that subscribers, role manifest, report process, or Nexus/SEMA integration are deployed. The running facility is the on-demand `--overview`; existing scheduled Field census remains distinct. Mind's detailed checkup and context/usage contracts remain the design sources rather than being duplicated here.
