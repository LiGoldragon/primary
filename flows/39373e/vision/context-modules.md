## A term with a module of its own leaves the vocabulary

Context: on the Great advancements proposal adding Mentci and Unity to knowledge/vocabulary.

"... I would even add: let's look at the list of vocabulary and find anything that either already has a context module dedicated to it or, since it's a big enough topic, could have a dedicated context module for it. ... the words themselves and their description would usually be the first thing either in the definition or at the beginning of the context module: explaining what this concept even is. This would make the entry in the vocabulary both redundant and problematic in the sense that if we change anything now we have two places to edit.

I would refuse this as it is but suggest that we just propose these words and any other words that could be in a dedicated module to have their own dedicated module proposal."

## Annotated context: desired, possible, fallback

Context: Flows and messages, 4th edition. 2026-10-21T20:28 and 20:24.

"... I think that's why we need a lot more annotation in the context modules to differentiate: "Here's what we would like. Here's what may happen because of such-and-such conditions in the code," and so on. Let's get deeper into that kind of more annotated context."

"... We need different kinds of annotations for different situations in terms of where the vision is, what's currently possible, and what we fall back on.

This is the ideal situation and then we could mark its status, essentially, in terms of implementation: "Implementation status uncertain." This is the desired behavior but the implementation status is uncertain. It describes what the fallback is: what do we fall back on if the implementation doesn't allow this?

Let's go into annotating the context way more than we have been, in terms of certainty levels and all kinds of other things. ... Let's develop a system which will become SEMA. Essentially we're starting to draft a proto-SEMA, which is not real yet but it's starting to show the concepts as a proof of concept. It doesn't actually have code to enforce it but is being drafted in a mockup production."

-- psyche, book comment, relayed by Psyche core Tertiary 4201a3, input mode not shown.
