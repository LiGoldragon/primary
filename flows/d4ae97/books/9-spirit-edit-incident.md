## What editing a compensation skill means

A flow may write and change `compensation-` and `trial-` skills without you. They are machine-authored, then reviewed and upgraded. That permission covers those two kinds and nothing else. The skills repository and the Curriculum signal accept a change to any skill file; nothing checks who stands behind the change. The kind lines in the skills are the only guard.

The batch (`dc7c2f4`) changed 17 files:
- Within its kind: nine `compensation-` skills, `trial-presentation-book`, `trial-succession`, and the standing list in `roles.datom`.
- `operation-flashbook`: it had its own row in the batch report; whether that came from your words was not checked.
- Outside its kind: `spirit` (two edits), `nix-workflow`, `psyche-grasp`, `psyche-interraction`. These are the changes proposed below.
