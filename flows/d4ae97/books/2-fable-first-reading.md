```
# Context modules

A machine accomplishes complex actions with
minimal effort and minimal output tokens. As
much of the context as possible is modularized,
so that naming modules calls a whole context:
100 or 200 tokens call a 100,000-token context,
and naming 7 modules loads 37 through their
dependency chain.

A flow's brief is a small paragraph; 95% or
more of its context comes from modules, a
leverage of 100:1 to 1,000:1 between what the
calling model writes and what the flow
receives.
```
