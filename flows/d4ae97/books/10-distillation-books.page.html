Skill proposals reach the living as a steady flow of short ones, each small enough for a quick yes: a new skill, an edit, an addition, or a dependency added to or removed from a skill or a subagent definition. A subagent definition is a skill.

Each aspect proposes the edits to its own skills; a flow that needs a change in another aspect's skill sends it to that aspect, which judges it, and a change to a vision-, intent- or spirit- skill goes on to the living.
