The caller is witnessed

Every Nexus CLI carries the ancestry it read; the Nexus trusts only what it re-witnesses at the socket and in /proc, at the moment of receipt. The library is generated, built and passing its fixtures on Prometheus, on a branch; it is not on nexus main and Flow does not import it yet.

What a Nexus promises about its caller

skills/vision-nexus.md

File in psyche-skills, «Default clients», after the shared-library line proposed in the fourth edition.

A Nexus witnesses its caller at the moment it receives: the CLI carries the ancestry it read, the first element must be the socket's peer, and the Nexus re-reads the whole chain and refuses any difference. Shells and wrappers stay in the chain. A Nexus promises nothing about a process's later life, and a stored binding to a process lapses with the boot it was observed in.
