Flow, whole, in ethos, fourth edition

Two types leave Flow's Library for libraries every Nexus imports: the judged identifier, with its word check, and the calling process. From the process object on, the file is as you left it.

Distillation

  1. Ruling 2: four layers in every aspect, Quaternary included, as landed, or three.

  1. Ruling 3: the name of the judged identifier type, a variant made on demand, uncapitalized camelCase, more than one word, given by human or machine judgment, checked when created. It lives in the protos library and every dialect imports it.

    1. Coinage: a term coined when needed, which is what the on-demand variant is
    2. Moniker: a name given by judgment, descriptive, not the thing's own
    3. Epithet: a descriptive by-name
    4. Cognomen: the Roman name given by description, added to the formal ones
    5. yours.

Ruling 3b: the check, cheapest first, each step only on what the step before could not pass:

stepwhatcost
splitcamelCase into components, acronym runs kepta few KB
listeach component against an embedded word list: Hunspell stems plus our own proper nouns, affix-strip retry~230 KB in the binary, ~100 ns, no startup
scorea character-trigram plausibility score for what the list missed~20 KB, ~10 ns per character; 4% of real words rejected, 4% of gibberish accepted
judgea System-1 model call for what scored low or is short, the score as certaintyone cheap call
    1. this shape
    2. list then model, no scorer
    3. model only
    4. yours.
