## What the attempt leaves for the design
The missing-name channel needs its eventual caller policy: the proof reports and omits it, without choosing whether a live launch should proceed. Inserted panels need their own provenance if the consumer is to name every piece that formed a master, rather than only the master's merged hash. Nested-panel dependencies and cycles need an authored policy; this attempt follows first actual inclusion and omits repeats, rather than inventing another diagnostic protocol.

The default engine now determines template syntax and directive whitespace; current fences pass, but future syntax collisions remain possible. The legacy public context_render helper is retained for the separately held renderer candidate; the proof's composition paths use MiniJinja. This branch is an experiment, with no independent acceptance review requested or claimed.
