# Layer vocabulary: research for naming the power layers

e167d8-side independent research, 2026-09-26, done by a subflow of Psyche Opus
e167d8. It is meant to be compared with Fable's independent answer. Nothing here
is ruled: it offers candidates for the living to choose from.

Source request: `flows/e167d8/vision/layerVocabulary.md` (the living, typed,
2026-09-26): find a vocabulary for the power layers that does not overlap
model effort; "mid layer, or something"; "go with Panini and look into
astrological anatomy"; "short is good"; "it can even be an expression".

## The problem, as found in the files

- The words high, medium, low, and ultra low name two separate scales.
  `Intent/models.md` already has to state this ("A flow named high is named
  by tier, not by effort setting").
- The collision is written into the setup itself. `SKILL_VARIABLES.md` has
  both `Psyche medium Claude model` and `Psyche medium Claude effort: medium`
  on consecutive lines. In the first, "medium" names the layer. In the
  second, the first "medium" names the layer and the second names the
  effort.
- Other words already carry ranking in the repo. A new term must avoid them:
  - **power**, **tier**, **energy** (`operational-fieldEnergyLevels.md`), and
    **rung** (`Vision/modelRoles.md`).
  - Seat and model names: Fable, Astra, Opus, Sol, Luna, Terra, Haiku,
    Sonnet.
  - Hosts: Zeus, Ouranos, Prometheus.
  - Repos and terms: core, root, base (as in "base context"), head (git
    HEAD), logos, nomos, ethos, and guṇa.
- Effort vocabulary to avoid entirely (my own knowledge; mark uncertain):
  - Claude: low, medium, high, xhigh, max.
  - Codex: minimal, low, medium, high, xhigh.
  - "ultra" is also a `/code-review` level, and "light" appears in
    `Intent/models.md`.
  - So **ultra-low needs its own word too**, not "ultra".
- Planet names are ruled out because Sol, Luna, and Terra are already model
  names. That removes the planetary-ruler half of astrological anatomy.
- "Older/younger" and "elder" wording is ruled out. `Vision/modelRoles.md`
  already uses "the older Opus" for a model distinction.

A note on the local source: `/git/github.com/LiGoldragon/Ashtadhyayi` has
only Volume 6 (adhyāyas 7–8) extracted as text. Adhyāya 1, where the
definitions below sit, has not been extracted. The sūtra numbers below come
from my own knowledge and are **marked uncertain** unless noted as witnessed.

## Candidate sets

Order in each set is high / middle / low / ultra-low. The STT column is my
judgement, not a test.

### 1. Pāṇini's three persons (puruṣa): Uttama / Madhyama / Prathama

- **Source:** Aṣṭādhyāyī 1.4.105–108 (uncertain numbering).
  - *yuṣmady upapade … madhyamaḥ*: the middle person, "you".
  - *asmady uttamaḥ*: the highest person, "I".
  - *śeṣe prathamaḥ*: the first person, "he/she", the remainder.
  - Witnessed locally: Vol. 6 line 3954 glosses "uttama 'first person'".
- **Fit:** *madhyama* is literally "middle" and is Pāṇini's own word, which
  matches the living's "mid layer".
- **Fatal flaw:** *prathama* literally means "first".
  - A model reading "Prathama" will rank it on top.
  - Uttama is also English "first person". The numbering inverts twice.
- **Length:** 3 syllables each.
- **STT:** Madhyama is fair ("mud-ya-ma", may land as "Madama"). Prathama is
  poor.
- **Verdict:** keep *uttama/madhyama*, but replace the low word.

### 2. Uttama / Madhyama / Kaniṣṭha (+ Kaniṣṭhikā for ultra-low)

- **Source:** the classical Sanskrit ranking triad.
  - The pair uttama/madhyama is Pāṇinian (set 1).
  - The low word is kaniṣṭha, "least". This triad is used in Vaiṣṇava
    literature for the three grades of adhikārī (uncertain as to earliest
    source).
  - The usual alternative, *adhama*, means "vile", so it is pejorative and
    rejected.
- **Ultra-low:** *kaniṣṭhikā*, "the littlest one". It is the diminutive of
  kaniṣṭha and also the name of the little finger.
  - This gives an anatomical tie: hand anatomy names the middle finger
    *madhyamā* as well.
  - The ultra-low word is visibly a diminutive of the low word, which
    matches "temporary ultra-low".
- **Fit:** the words are superlatives, so the rank is in the words: best,
  middle, least. No effort setting uses them.
- **Length:** 3 / 3 / 3 / 4 syllables.
- **Collision:** none in the repo. Kaniṣṭha is strictly "youngest/least".
  The age sense touches the older/newer Opus wording, but it is weak.
- **STT:** fair to poor. "Uttama" can land as "Utama" or "Ottoman", and
  "Kanishta" can land as "Kanishka". A typed canonical spelling with plain
  ASCII (Uttama, Madhyama, Kanishtha) would be needed.

### 3. Pāṇini's pitch terms and Jyotiṣa dignity: Ucca / Madhya / Nīca (+ Atinīca)

- **Source, Pāṇini:** 1.2.29–30 (uncertain numbering) defines accent by
  *uccaiḥ* "high" (→ udātta) and *nīcaiḥ* "low" (→ anudātta).
- **Source, astrology:** Jyotiṣa uses the same two words for planetary
  dignity. *Ucca* is a planet's exaltation and *nīca* its debilitation. The
  deepest point is *paramanīca*.
- **Fit:**
  - This is the one word pair that joins the two traditions the living
    named, Pāṇini and astrology.
  - *Madhya* is plain "middle", the closest to "mid".
  - It is the shortest Sanskrit option: 2 syllables each.
- **Risk:**
  - In Jyotiṣa, *nīca* is a weakness ("debilitated"), and *nīca* also means
    "base, vile" in ordinary Sanskrit. That is a mild pejorative for the low
    layer.
  - STT: "ucca" may be heard as "ucha" or "Utah". "Nīca" may be heard as
    "neecha" or "Nietzsche". "Madhya" may be heard as "media".

### 4. Pāṇini's accents: Udātta / Svarita / Anudātta

- **Source:** 1.2.29–31. Sūtra 1.2.31 *samāhāraḥ svaritaḥ*, "the svarita is
  the combination", is cited in the witnessed Vol. 6 line 34331.
- **Fit:** the svarita is the high and the low joined. That neatly describes
  the middle layer, because vertical routing moves one rung at a time
  through it (`Vision/modelRoles.md`).
- **Fatal flaw:** Udātta and Anudātta differ only by a prefix. That is a
  bad minimal pair for both STT and models.
- **Length:** 3–4 syllables. STT is poor.
- **Verdict:** kept as background, not as names.

### 5. Voice registers (sthāna): Tāra / Madhya / Mandra (+ Anumandra)

- **Source:** the three registers of Indian phonetics and music. Saṅgīta
  texts place mandra in the chest, madhya in the throat, and tāra in the
  head (uncertain attribution: Nāṭyaśāstra or Saṅgītaratnākara). This
  genuinely joins Pāṇinian phonetics with anatomy.
- **Fatal flaws:**
  - *Tāra* sounds almost like **Terra**, the Codex low model. Top and low
    would be confused.
  - *Mandra* is heard as "mantra".
  - Madhya and Mandra share their onset.
- **Verdict:** rejected. The anatomical words underneath, Śiras (head) /
  Kaṇṭha (throat) / Uras (chest), are distinct, but too obscure.

### 6. Astrological anatomy (melothesia, Manilius, Ptolemy)

The zodiac is mapped onto the body, head to feet:

- Aries: head
- Taurus: neck
- Gemini: arms
- Cancer: breast
- Leo: heart
- Virgo: belly
- Libra: loins
- Scorpio: genitals
- Sagittarius: thighs
- Capricorn: knees
- Aquarius: shins
- Pisces: feet

Three possible sets come from it:

- **Head / Heart / Hand (+ Heel), an expression:**
  - Pestalozzi's "Kopf, Herz und Hand" is an existing expression that
    already ranks thinking over judging over doing.
  - It fits `Vision/modelRoles.md`. The high seats do thinking and design.
    The medium seat is chosen "for disposition": it questions and doubts,
    which is heart work. The lower seats "get work done", which is hand
    work.
  - Hand is not literally below heart in melothesia (Gemini rules the arms).
    It is an expression, not a strict mapping.
  - Heel, for ultra-low, is the Pisces end. It alliterates, but "heel" also
    means a cad, and it may be heard as "heal".
  - STT: excellent. Every word is common English.
  - Collision: **Head versus git HEAD**. This is real in agent contexts:
    "head" appears 106 times across the vision records.
- **Latin Caput / Cor / Pes:**
  - It is short.
  - *Cor* is heard as, and read as, "core", which is a repo name.
  - Rejected.
- **The signs themselves, Aries / Leo / Pisces:**
  - Readable, but they carry zodiac-personality baggage.
  - Rejected.

### 7. Astrological house strength: Kendra / Panaphara / Apoklima (Angular / Succedent / Cadent)

- **Source:** in both Hellenistic and Jyotiṣa astrology, the three house
  classes are ranked by strength: angular strongest, succedent middle,
  cadent weakest. Jyotiṣa took the words over from Greek: kentron,
  epanaphora, apoklima.
- **Fit:** this is literally astrology's power ranking by position, which
  makes it the closest semantic match to "power layer".
- **Weaknesses:**
  - There are only three classes, so there is no ultra-low word.
  - "Angular" collides with the web framework.
  - "Apoklima" is 4 syllables and poor for STT.
  - "Kendra" alone is short and clean.

### 8. Rejected quickly

- **Sattva / Rajas / Tamas:** the guṇa word is already in use, and tamas
  ("inertia, darkness") is pejorative.
- **Bhūr / Bhuvaḥ / Svaḥ:** Bhūr and Bhuvaḥ are too close to each other,
  and Bhūr means "earth", which fuses it with Terra.
- **Jyeṣṭha / Madhyama / Kaniṣṭha:** eldest, middle, youngest. The age sense
  collides with "older Opus".
- **Apex / Core / Base:** both core and base are taken.
- **Crown / Heart / Sole:** "sole" is heard as Sol.
- **Upper / Mid / Lower:** "mid" still pulls models toward "medium". It is
  acceptable, but it only weakens the overlap rather than removing it.

## Recommendation: top three

These are e167d8-side independent research, for comparison with Fable.

1. **Uttama / Madhyama / Kaniṣṭha, with Kaniṣṭhikā for ultra-low.**
   - It is the most Pāṇinian: *madhyama* is Pāṇini's own "middle person".
   - The rank is inside the words (best, middle, least), and no effort API
     uses any of them.
   - The ultra-low word is the diminutive of the low word, which fits a
     temporary rung, and it is also the little finger, the anatomy tie.
   - Cost: 3 syllables, and a fixed ASCII spelling is needed for STT.
2. **Ucca / Madhya / Nīca, with Atinīca for ultra-low.**
   - It is the shortest Sanskrit option and the one bridge that exists in
     both Pāṇini (accent) and astrology (exaltation and debilitation).
   - *Madhya* is exactly "mid".
   - Cost: *nīca* is mildly pejorative, and STT robustness is the weakest
     of the three.
3. **Head / Heart / Hand, with Heel for ultra-low**, an existing expression
   that follows the melothesia direction.
   - It is by far the most STT-robust choice, and it describes each
     layer's work (thinking, consideration, doing) rather than only its
     rank.
   - Cost: it is not Pāṇini, and "Head" can be confused with git HEAD.

Across all three, the layer word should be used in titles and types
(`Psyche Madhyama`), and "medium", "high", and "low" should be kept only for
effort. The `SKILL_VARIABLES.md` names (`Psyche medium Claude ...`) are the
first place the change would show. That rename is a separate act, for the
main flow.
