## Cost diagnosis and corrective rule

Observed waste mechanisms include repeated same-task redispatch, repeated readiness narration instead of executable handoffs, distributed approvals with incompatible conditions, failure to distinguish source/evaluation/build/runtime evidence, and broad Home gates obscuring a narrow CLI repair. No measured attribution of tokens or weekly quota is available.

A repeated failure stops that operation, not investigation. Another attempt requires changed evidence or a repaired prerequisite. Preserve a partial working session; do not recreate it merely for procedural neatness. Prefer one bounded source repair and isolated direct test to broad deployment. Do not wake Claude seats or broadcast for this investigation.
