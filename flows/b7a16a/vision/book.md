
## A marked figure keeps its colours

Context: comment on What truly blocks 4th edition, on a flowchart drawn wholly green as an addition.

"Well I'm happy that, for once, for the first time, I see that a flowchart is marked as an addition or removal. By making everything inside the flowchart green (and by the way the flowcharts lately have deteriorated significantly in quality), there's no more color. There's no more different thickness and the illustration is horrible. We don't want to make the whole chart green or red. Obviously even the text itself doesn't need to be green or red. It just needs a sort of marker, either a surrounding color or a column margin color or something. Let's not go crazy and paint the whole world black every time we want to mark something with a color. We don't need to make the whole thing that color."

-- psyche, STT, book comment.
