## Proposed answers to 8

The record is called a **question**; the directory `question/` beside `vision/` and `notion/`. An entry is one question in your verbatim words, a dropped question mark restored in brackets, an implied question stated beneath as the flow's inference. Its status is answered, open, or superseded, each with provenance. The book form borrows the scholastic shape: the question, the proposal, the counter question. The three log kinds: **psyche** is what you want (vision, notion, intent, spirit); **mind** is what must be known and decided (question, witness, chronology: what landed, ran, tested, deployed); **field** is the body of the machine as it is (state, behavior, anatomy). Every seat logs psyche when you speak; mind and field logs are the seat's own.
