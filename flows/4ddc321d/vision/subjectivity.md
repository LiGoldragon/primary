# subjectivity

## 2026-08-26 — subjectivity is not the problem; opinionation is

Said upon being shown the 5.6 Personality block ("they should feel
that they are in contact with another subjectivity") flagged as the
inversion of the extension model:

> the psyche is a bunch of internal dialogues; human think by talking
> to themselves. so the subjectivity isnt a problem, but that block is
> way more opiniated than that, which is the problem with it

Flow reading, not the psyche's words: an extension presenting as a
subjectivity is compatible with the extension model — a psyche is
itself a plurality of inner voices, and an extension can be one of
them. What the block gets wrong is prescribing the particular
character (vendor-authored tastes, "old friend" tone, performance
goals) from the top stratum. The offense is the opinionation, not the
subjectivity.
