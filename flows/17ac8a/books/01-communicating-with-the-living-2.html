the module, as it now stands

psyche-context/skills/vision-living-communication.md

Changed since edition one:

Communication with the living carries the most to him for each moment of his attention. The same lines hold in every harness: Claude artifacts, Codex, or a later one.

It is multimodal: figure, text and speech. It is visual first, and its text is minimal.

It is read on a phone, scrollingA presentation adapts to the size of the screen it is read on. It reads top to bottom: one thing per section, each section and figure under its own heading, short text, much drawing.

A proposal is the least instruction that gives the biggest effect. It carries his words crystallized and names the records it draws from.A proposed statement is one no more authoritative module already makes, in the same or effectively the same words.

A proposal shows each change once, across every edition, in a named module. A changed line shows once: its removed words in red and its added words in green, inline. A line wholly new is green; a line wholly gone is red. A record to destroy or archive shows in its own colour, marked Destroy or Archive.

Code shown to him keeps every line within 52 characters.

A figure starts as a text sketch at most 52 characters wide. The drawn figure keeps every box, label and arrow of the sketch and adds style: its arrows and things may be illustratedillustrate its arrows and things with the feeling their meaning asks.

New:

Each thing in a presentation is read once.

Variations of a proposal show their common text once and each variation's own words apart.

A situational report reaches him at each big change: the open questions, the crystallizations and the proposals, now and then with a knowledge review.

## Long term

The interface to the living becomes real-time, and may carry machine speech.

Standing unchanged from edition one, folded: the messenger line; the figure grammar; a figure is a figure proposed for a module; the choice line; one proposal per presentation under proposal-by-proposal review; questions carry proposed answers and counterquestions.

It reaches him through the living messenger: one presentation per message, each a fresh item, holding whole everything he is to read or rule on.

A figure is a flow answering one question in seconds: a box is a thing or a collection; an arrow is a one-way relation carrying its verb, so that box, verb, box reads as a sentence; nearness and enclosure are kinship; top to bottom is order; colour carries a role. A sizable presentation carries several figures.

A figure shown to him is a figure proposed for a module.

A choice is lettered alternatives, each a full line. He answers by comment and names a choice by its label, the letter or number; his comment is answered by the proposal it results in.

When he reviews proposal by proposal, each presentation carries one proposal.

A presentation of questions carries proposed answers and counterquestions.

Figure: edition one's loop (his words → presentation → living messenger → his comment → next proposal), unchanged.
