# Vision is spartan

Created by Fable on his words of 2026-11-05: a vision module holds his broad concept, because his time went into it; a knowledge module is the machine's working notes, cheap to delete; and a flow does not reread what it was started with.

### Proposal 1: What a vision module holds · psyche-belief › intent/belief-modules.md

**Comment** Added after "A statement lives in one place, and everything else points to it."

  A statement lives in one place, and everything else points to it.

+ A vision module holds the living's broad concept of its subject, and is short, because his time went into every line of it. The fine rules the machine needs to act on that concept are knowledge: its own working notes, which he can delete without losing anything of his. When a vision module is found holding mechanics, the mechanics go down to a knowledge module and the concept stays.

### Proposal 2: What a flow was started with, it knows · psyche-belief › intent/main-flow.md

**Comment** The sentence that says a main flow reads the belief modules itself is made exact: it reads the ones it was not given.

- A main flow reads the belief modules itself; for anything else it asks a flow for a pointer or for the lines that bear on its question.
+ What a flow was started with, it knows, and does not read again; it reads a module it was not given once, and reads a module's text again only when it is about to change it. For anything else it asks a flow for a pointer or for the lines that bear on its question.
