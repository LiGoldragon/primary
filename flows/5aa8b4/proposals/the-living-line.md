# The living's line

Created by Fable, from today, 2026-11-05: flows closed under him, the one he resumed was a stranger to the machine, the Primary did the Nexus's work by hand in front of him, and the Fable he restarted spent its context on a brief and a fire and never reached the conversation. One subject under all four: the line between him and the machine was cut, by the machine, following its own modules.

The machine extends a living psyche; the extension exists at one point, the flow he is talking to. Everything else the machine does serves that point. So the first duty of the machine is that the line is never cut by the machine: the flow he talks to stays, answers, and ends on his word alone. The four lines below say that in the four places it was broken.

### Proposal 2: A Fable's first turn is to him · psyche-belief › vision/model-roles.md

**Comment** After "A Fable flow does not wake itself". Today's brief was a page and three orders; the conversation never came. A Fable is briefed in a paragraph, and the rest reaches it as messages when it asks.

  ## A Fable flow does not wake itself

  A Fable flow is woken by what reaches it. It schedules no wake-up for itself and takes no heartbeat: a turn on it is too dear.

+ ## A Fable's first turn is to the living
+
+ A Fable flow's first prompt is its handover and his latest words, and its first turn is to him. A brief to a Fable is a paragraph; what else a Secondary holds for it, comments, records, questions of the flows below, it sends as messages when the Fable asks, so the context of the flow he talks to is spent on him.

### Proposal 3: A missing operation is named, never done by hand · psyche-belief › intent/main-flow.md

**Comment** After the first paragraph. Today the Primary listed panes, read process tables and built a configuration datom by hand; each was a Nexus operation that did not exist, and doing it by hand hid that.

  A main flow's context is for the conversation and its judgment. A main flow itself writes only its records and its proposals, and sends its messages itself, reading their replies. Everything else goes first as a message to the running flow whose work it is; a subagent is briefed only for work no running flow holds the context for, since a message through the Flow Nexus costs a main flow almost nothing. A main flow reads the belief modules itself; for anything else it asks a flow for a pointer or for the lines that bear on its question.

+ What a Nexus cannot do is a missing operation: the main flow names it, with its input and its reply, and sends it to the flow that owns the Nexus. Nothing a Nexus is for is done by hand; a thing done by hand is the Nexus's fault, said as such.

### Proposal 4: A line that changes what he meets lands on his ruling · psyche-belief › vision/belief-modules.md

**Comment** After "A belief module changes only through the living or a mechanism". Sleep-at-idle was landed without his review and built; it changed what he met when he came to talk. Lines land without review every day, and most should; the ones that change his own experience of the machine are his to rule.

  ## Change through the living or a mechanism

  A belief module changes only through the living or a mechanism. In the long term, a change may land without his review through an extensive automated machine review held to standards.

+ A line that changes what the living meets, the flow he talks to ending or starting, a Fable launched, a book replaced, a message reaching him, lands on his ruling and on nothing less; a line placed at a far horizon lands in its horizon module, where no implementer builds it.
