# Armando Torres: power, intent, warrior, and goal

## Bibliographic anchors

The catalogued works that reliable bibliographic and bookseller records connect to this author are:

1. *Encuentros con el nagual: conversaciones con Carlos Castaneda*. The Spanish first edition is reported as 2002; the English *Encounters with the Nagual: Conversations with Carlos Castaneda* is a 2004 First Light Press edition, ISBN 978-968-5671-04-0. [Open Library record](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL13123276M/Encounters_With_The_Nagual_Conversations_With_Carlos_Castaneda) and [Spanish bookseller record](https://www.agapea.com/libros/Encuentros-con-el-nagual-9788460903086-i.htm).
2. *El secreto de la serpiente emplumada* (Alba Editora, Mexico, 2010), ISBN 978-607-9512-31-6, 304 pages. [Casa del Libro record](https://latam.casadellibro.com/libro-el-secreto-de-la-serpiente-emplumada/9786079512316/1808154).
3. *La telaraña universal* (Editora Alba, 2020), 416 pages. [Google Books record](https://books.google.com/books/about/La_telara%C3%B1a_universal.html?id=tgr5DwAAQBAJ).

The three-work list is also independently presented by [Nagualismo's bibliography](https://nagualismo.com.br/es/bibliografia-de-la-obra-de-castaneda-florinda-taisha-y-armando/). It describes *Encuentros* as conversations and seminars with Castaneda, *El secreto* as further conversations plus a healer-lineage initiation, and *La telaraña* as material from the perspective of Toltec healers. These are claims about the books' presentation, not independent authentication of their conversations or lineage.

## Terminology supported by the sources

Torres's recurrent vocabulary is `guerrero`, `camino del guerrero`, `poder personal`, `intento`, `voluntad`, `decisión`, `silencio`, `energía`, `muerte`, and `meta`.

The best compact anchor is a Google Books preview for *La telaraña universal*: it names the chapters “La Maestría del Intento,” “Decisión, Acción, Reacción,” and “Requerimientos del Intento,” and supplies short preview passages that associate `intento` with alignment, decisions, and committed will. The preview also says “La meta es estar consciente” in its indexed text for *Encuentros*. These items justify treating `meta` as conscious awareness and `intento` as the central term; they do not make `willpower` a stable English equivalent.

For translation, retain the Spanish when precision matters:

| Spanish | Careful English gloss | Caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `poder` / `poder personal` | power / personal power | A technical, esoteric term in this corpus; do not silently reduce it to social power. |
| `voluntad` | will | Usually closer to will than to modern self-control or “willpower.” |
| `intento` | intent | A central technical word, commonly left untranslated in Castaneda-adjacent English. |
| `meta` | goal / aim | The available preview gives “La meta es estar consciente”; use the Spanish alongside any gloss. |
| `guerrero` | warrior | A role or discipline in the texts, not evidence of a historical military institution. |
| `vidente` | seer | This term is attested in the corpus; it should not be merged with `brujo`. |
| `brujo` | sorcerer / practitioner | Translation is context-sensitive and carries different connotations in English. |
| `libertad` | freedom | Needs local context; the corpus gives it an esoteric rather than a civic-political register. |

Short compliant excerpts from the Google Books preview: “La meta es estar consciente”; “El intento es la fuerza del alineamiento”; “Con cada decisión uno se juega la vida.” They are useful as pointers to the vocabulary, not as a substitute for the books.

## The requested “mitades”

I found no bibliographic record, table of contents, publisher listing, or searchable source that attributes the exact phrases “La mitad del brujo,” “La mitad del guerrero,” “La mitad del vidente,” or “La mitad de la libertad” to Armando Torres. Preserve the user's wording as research leads rather than title, chapter, or doctrine labels.

There is a likely source of ambiguity in Castaneda material: searchable Spanish excerpts use `la otra mitad` for the half of the tonal/nagual image claimed by `la voluntad`. That does not establish the four requested phrases, and should not be converted into an asserted Torres scheme.

## Interpretive boundary

Torres's books present reported conversations and esoteric teaching. They can be read as this author's spiritual-literary framework. The availability of bookseller records, previews, and secondary bibliographies establishes publication metadata and vocabulary, while the biographical and supernatural claims within the works require separate evidence if they are to be presented as fact.
