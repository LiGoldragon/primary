## Findings in one paragraph

Armando Torres (Armando Torres Aguilar, Mexico) has two books of
conversations with Carlos Castaneda. The first, *Encuentros con el
nagual* (2002, Spanish; English 2004 as *Encounters with the Nagual*),
contains the material closest to the requested topics: a chapter titled
"El camino del guerrero" / "The Path of the Warrior", explicit passages
on voluntad and intento, and two occurrences of "la meta". The four
phrases "La mitad del brujo", "La mitad del guerrero", "La mitad del
vidente" and "La mitad de la libertad" do not occur in either edition of
the first book, and Google Books finds no match for "la mitad del" in any
Torres title. Either they belong to the second book, whose text I could
not search, or they are the Book of Soul's own titles rather than
Torres's. That is an unknown, not a negative result.

## Structure of Encuentros con el nagual

Four parts; the chapters are grouped by theme, not by date.

- Parte 1, "Un romance con el conocimiento": La revolución de los brujos;
  La importancia personal; El camino del guerrero; Conciencia de la
  muerte; El drenaje energético; La Recapitulación; El umbral del
  silencio.
- Parte 2, "Diálogo de guerreros": eleven sections, about forty short
  chapters, including Saturación conceptual, Un inventario de creencias,
  Creer sin creer, La práctica del silencio, La oportunidad mínima, No
  se necesitan maestros, Conocerse a sí mismo, plants of power, stalking,
  dreaming, the assemblage point, the petty tyrant, the end of the
  lineage, the seers of the new era, the task of the nagual.
- Parte 3, "La regla del nagual de tres puntas": the thirty-page
  manuscript Torres says Castaneda charged him to publish, on renewing
  the lineages of knowledge. Chapters include El propósito de la regla
  and La tarea de los videntes actuales.
- Parte 4, "El mundo de los antiguos videntes hoy": Torres's own account
  of a group of sorcery practitioners.

## Where the requested topics live in the first book

Keyword counts, Spanish edition: guerrero 132, poder 72, brujo 56,
intento 47, libertad 31, propósito 21, voluntad 18, vidente 6, meta 3.

**El guerrero.** The chapter "El camino del guerrero" (Parte 1) is the
primary source. Its argument, summarized: every human is by right a
warrior who won a life-or-death race at conception; socialization
domesticates that inheritance; the warrior's war is against their own
weaknesses, not other people; the warrior's final challenge is to
balance all the attributes of the path, after which their purpose
becomes inflexible and they become owner of their will. The opening
chapter "La revolución de los brujos" carries the line that a warrior is
made to fight and that war is their rest.

**Voluntad (willpower).** Key statements, all in Parte 1 and early
Parte 2:

- Discipline governs attention and leads to will; will lets the warrior
  reshape the world as they want it rather than as imposed. For warriors,
  will is the antechamber of intent ("la voluntad es la antesala del
  intento").
- The tragedy of modern people is not their social condition but the
  lack of will to change themselves.
- Silence begins with an act of will that becomes the Eagle's command,
  but will must be transformed into intent; imposed silence is never
  real silence.
- Seeing reveals a supreme, impersonal will that organizes everything;
  sorcerers tune to it through inner silence.

**Poder (power).** Torres uses "poder" mostly in Castaneda's technical
senses: the nagual makes gifts of power; sorcerers put the power of
passion at the service of being, which drives the only search that
matters, the search for oneself; an apprentice who uses new power to
get rich is the example of a warrior who has not become owner of will.
Personal power as energy reserve is treated in "El drenaje energético"
and "La Recapitulación".

**La meta (the goal).** Two explicit statements:

- In the stalking chapter: breaking routines is only a means; the goal
  is to be aware ("La meta es estar consciente"), and stalking is
  defined as an unbending attention on a total result.
- In the perception chapter: the goal of sorcerers is to perceive all
  that is humanly possible.

The English edition adds "the goal of total freedom" as the goal of the
nagual's party, repeated across Parte 3.

**Libertad.** Torres's introduction states that everything a warrior
does must be oriented to the true purpose of the human being, freedom,
and quotes Castaneda that freedom is an individual choice each person
must fight for. Parte 3 frames total freedom as the collective goal of
the lineage.

**The four mitades.** Not found. Searched "mitad", "mitades", "la mitad
del", "la mitad de la" in the Spanish text and "half", "halves" in the
English text; every hit is ordinary usage ("half the work is done",
"half his time"). Google Books returns zero results for "la mitad del"
with Torres as author. The second book could not be full-text searched.

## Second book, El secreto de la serpiente emplumada

Catalog descriptions say it covers Torres's time with a group of
healers and sorcerers, ceremonies including one to call down the
Plumed Serpent, and further Castaneda conversations. Torres notes he
mixes the healers' vocabulary with Castaneda's nagualism. No table of
contents was reachable. Reviews are mixed on authenticity; one reviewer
questions how close Torres was to Castaneda's circle. Recommend the main
flow obtain the Kindle or paper edition if the mitades must be traced.

## Caveats

- Torres was not a formal apprentice; he says he attended some lectures
  and private talks from 1984 onward and reconstructed conversations.
  Treat his Castaneda quotations as Torres's rendering, not Castaneda's
  verbatim words.
- Public PDF copies were used only for keyword search and short
  summaries; no bulk text is reproduced here or in primary.

## Second wave: passages with context

Two extraction agents worked over the locally extracted text of both
editions (Spanish and English). Each passage gives chapter, a short
Spanish quote, its English-edition rendering, and context. Line numbers
refer to the scratchpad text files and are for provenance only.

Finding about the copies: the Spanish PDF ends at "Retorno a la
esencia" (English chapter 37). Parts 3 and 4 ("La regla del nagual de

### Poder, voluntad, intento

1. **Falta de voluntad** (Un romance con el conocimiento). "La tragedia del hombre actual no es su condición social, sino la falta de voluntad para cambiarse a sí mismo." EN: "The tragedy of today's man is not his social condition, but the lack of will to change himself." Context: Carlos, after the desert anecdote with don Juan curing his smoking; he follows with "revolución contra su propia estupidez".
2. **La antesala del intento** (El camino del guerrero). "Tener disciplina es la clave en el manejo de la atención, porque nos lleva a la voluntad. [...] Por tal razón, para los guerreros, la voluntad es la antesala del intento." EN renders antesala as "starting point on a warrior's new state of intent". Context: enumerating warrior attributes, right after courage as accepting consequences without self-pity.
3. **El umbral de la magia** (El camino del guerrero). "El umbral de la magia es un intento sostenido más allá de lo que parecía posible, deseable o razonable." EN: "The threshold of magic is my intent sustained beyond what might seem possible, desirable, or reasonable." Context: persistence defeats apathy; Carlos admits he nearly quit several times.
4. **El poder de su decisión** (El camino del guerrero). "un guerrero en busca de la impecabilidad sabe que cuenta con el poder de su decisión. [...] la claridad aunada al poder forman una mezcla explosiva." EN: "clarity joined with power makes an explosive mixture." Context: followed by the hang-glider analogy and "los laberintos del poder".
5. **Dueño de la voluntad, la disyuntiva** (El camino del guerrero). "Es dueño de la voluntad y puede ponerla a su servicio personal. [...] caer en el 'quiero esto, quiero aquello', o cultivar el intento. [...] El intento es la sintonía de nuestra atención con la conciencia cósmica." EN: "Intent can be described as tuning our attention to cosmic awareness." Context: the apprentice who uses new powers to get rich; freedom is a fact, not a utopia.
6. **El poder de la pasión** (El drenaje energético). "Han puesto el poder de la pasión al servicio del ser, y ella les da el ímpetu necesario para emprender la única búsqueda que cuenta: la de uno mismo." EN: "They have put the power of passion to the service of their being." Context: closes the discussion of sex and energy drain.
7. **Sin poder de sus decisiones** (La Recapitulación). "Si no recuperamos la totalidad de nuestra energía, nunca llegaremos al poder de nuestras decisiones [...] Y sin poder de sus decisiones un hombre no es nada." EN: "without the power of his decisions, a man is nothing." Context: Carlos's yearly full recapitulation in seclusion.
8. **Vehículo del intento** (El umbral del silencio). "El silencio es un pasadizo entre los mundos. [...] la persona se hace vehículo del intento y todos sus actos comienzan a rezumar poder." EN: "a person becomes a vehicle of intent, and all his acts begin to ooze power." Context: sorcery is "el arte del silencio".
9. **Transformar la voluntad en intento** (El umbral del silencio). "El silencio empieza con una oración, un acto de voluntad que se convierte en el comando del Águila. [...] Hay que aprender a transformar la voluntad en intento." EN renders "oración" as "command". Context: silence as letting go, like a child staring at fire.
10. **Una oferta al espíritu** (La oportunidad mínima). "El intento del hombre tiene que hacer una oferta al espíritu, y éste debe aceptarla, poniendo los medios de evolución en su camino." EN: "A man's intent has to make an offer to the spirit." Context: Torres asks what gives an ordinary man access; today publications can orient him.

### La meta, el guerrero, la libertad

11. **Elección individual** (Introducción). "Yo no prometo nada, yo no soy gurú. La libertad es una elección individual y es responsabilidad de cada uno luchar por ella." EN: "Freedom is an individual choice, and each of us must assume the responsibility of fighting for it." Context: first meeting, October 1984; then the attack on idolatry and the "herd stage".
12. **Su descanso es la guerra** (La revolución de los brujos). "Un guerrero está hecho para combatir, su descanso es la guerra." EN softens to "while they are at war they are comfortable." Context: a guest asks whether sorcerers are pacifists.
13. **La revolución de los brujos** (same chapter). "la única salida del ser humano como especie, es hacer una revolución contra su propia estupidez. [...] El objetivo de los brujos es la revolución de los brujos, el despliegue irrestricto de nuestras posibilidades perceptuales." EN: "the unrestricted unfolding of all our perceptual possibilities."
14. **Al alcance de la mano** (same chapter). "La libertad que nos ofrece el camino del guerrero está al alcance de la mano, ¡aprovéchenla!" EN: "The freedom which the warrior's way offers us is within the reach of your hand." Context: the parable of the loaded beast.
15. **La única guerra que vale la pena** (El camino del guerrero). "La disciplina, tal como la entiende el guerrero, es creativa, abierta y produce libertad. [...] la única guerra que vale la pena: la del conocimiento." EN shifts to "the battle for increased self awareness."
16. **Impecabilidad** (El camino del guerrero). "disciplina, sobriedad, desapego y capacidad de análisis. Tales atributos [...] constituyen en su conjunto el estado de ser que ellos llaman impecabilidad. [...] Es consecuencia del ahorro de energía." The English lists three attributes and folds analysis into energy saving.
17. **El desafío final** (El camino del guerrero). "El desafío final del guerrero es equilibrar todos los atributos de su camino. Una vez que lo consigue, su propósito se torna inflexible." EN: "their purpose becomes inflexible."
18. **La libertad es un hecho** (El camino del guerrero). "Para ellos, la libertad es un hecho, no una utopía. [...] el objeto de su lucha es la libertad total." EN: "the object of their fight is total freedom." Context: modern man's trap of eight-hour work; "¡Sean guerreros, detengan eso!"
19. **La meta es estar consciente** (La marca del nagual). "La libertad no obedece a razones. [...] Romper rutinas no es la finalidad del sendero, sino apenas un medio. La meta es estar consciente." EN: "Freedom does not obey reasons. [...] The goal is to be aware." Context: walking with Torres; "This people went out to live their last day."
20. **Monos sublimes** (La homogenización perceptual). "La meta de los brujos es percibir todo lo que es humanamente posible. Ya que no podemos salir de nuestra condición biológica, ¡seamos monos sublimes!" EN: "let us be sublime monkeys."
21. **Cazador, no oportunista** (Retorno a la esencia). "Un guerrero es un cazador, no un cínico oportunista." EN identical. Companion line: "Ser un guerrero es una lucha interminable por ser implacable."

Runners-up noted by the wave: "La libertad se logra sin pensar" (El
umbral del silencio); "El propósito de la libertad es absolutamente
limpio y desplaza a todos los demás" (La transmisión del acecho); "Un
guerrero es alguien que ha aprendido a acecharse" (Acechando al pinche
tirano).

Translation drift observed between editions: antesala → "starting
point"; oración → "command"; "su descanso es la guerra" → "while they
are at war they are comfortable"; four attributes → three. The Spanish
should be treated as primary.

## La antesala · The Antechamber

*Draft composition for the Book of Soul, dualingo. Original text; it leans on
Torres's rendering of Castaneda (will as the antechamber of intent, the goal
is to be aware, the warrior's war is against their own weaknesses) without
quoting him.*

| Español | English |
| --- | --- |
| Nadie te da la voluntad. Se templa, como se templa una hoja: calor, golpe, agua fría, y otra vez. | Nobody hands you will. It is tempered the way a blade is tempered: heat, blow, cold water, and again. |
| Primero viene la disciplina, que no es castigo sino cuidado de la atención. Donde pones la atención, ahí se junta tu poder. | Discipline comes first, and it is not punishment but the care of attention. Where you put your attention, that is where your power gathers. |
| La atención sostenida se vuelve voluntad. La voluntad es una antesala: un cuarto pequeño donde uno espera, con la puerta cerrada, sin saber si la abrirán. | Sustained attention becomes will. Will is an antechamber: a small room where you wait, door closed, not knowing whether it will be opened. |
| El error es querer vivir en la antesala. Imponerse silencio y llamar a eso silencio. Empujar la puerta y llamar a eso libertad. | The mistake is wanting to live in the antechamber. Imposing silence on yourself and calling that silence. Shoving the door and calling that freedom. |
| La puerta se abre desde el otro lado. Lo que la abre no es el esfuerzo sino el abandono del esfuerzo cuando ya el esfuerzo hizo su trabajo. A eso los brujos le dicen intento. | The door opens from the other side. What opens it is not effort but the letting go of effort once effort has done its work. That is what sorcerers call intent. |
| ¿Cuál es la meta, entonces? No es ganar. El guerrero no pelea para ganar, pelea porque pelear es su descanso, y su enemigo es la propia flojera, la propia lástima, el propio yo. | What is the goal, then? It is not to win. The warrior does not fight to win; they fight because fighting is their rest, and the enemy is their own laziness, their own self-pity, their own self. |
| La meta es estar despierto mientras sucede todo esto. Percibir todo lo que un cuerpo humano alcanza a percibir. Y si al final hay libertad, será porque uno ya no la necesitaba. | The goal is to be awake while all of this happens. To perceive everything a human body can reach. And if at the end there is freedom, it will be because you no longer needed it. |

## Tooling findings

**Diagrams.** The psyche's recorded rule (vision-raw/visuals.md,
2026-08-21): visuals printed in a response are ASCII; in an artifact
they are Mermaid. There is no recorded rule about a subflow converting
Mermaid to SVG. Without instruction I would have followed the recorded
rule and used Mermaid inside the artifact. For a better-looking vector
result, D2 (Terrastruct, v0.7.1, in nixpkgs) with the ELK layout engine
and sketch mode is the current standout; it writes SVG directly with
embedded fonts, has themes for light and dark, and renders in under a
second. Tested here: `claude-partner-warrior-path.d2` →
`claude-partner-warrior-path.svg`.

**Rasterizing and watermarking without a thinking machine.** resvg
drops D2's embedded @font-face fonts, so text vanishes; Inkscape and
libvips (vips 8.18) both rasterize D2 SVGs correctly. The watermark
"legaldragon.com" is injected as a vector `<text>` element into the SVG
before rasterizing, so it survives at any scale. For raster sources,
libvips (`vips text` + `vips composite2`) is the modern fast choice;
ImageMagick 7 still works and is what is installed in the user profile.
The Rust-native stack for the same job is resvg/usvg for SVG plus the
`image` and `imageproc` crates for text overlay, if this becomes a
repo tool.
