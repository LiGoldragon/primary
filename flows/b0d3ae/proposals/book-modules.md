# The book modules, slashed

Created by the Primary; modules gathered by a Tertiary

**Why** The failure you saw: knowledge:book-making has the module text of a proposal written inside a text fence, line by line signed, and the builder sets a fence in monospace. Prose in a fence is the knot. The vision below says prose is prose and a fence holds code alone; knowledge:book-making and the role procedures are then redone by Mind from it, since they are the machine's and you skim them.

### Proposal 1: vision:book · psyche-belief › vision/book.md — whole, retaken

## Books

A book carries belief modules, as they stand and as they are proposed to change. Prose is rendered text, wrapping on a phone. A fence holds code alone, labelled with its language, Ethos and datom in the vertical form. A figure is drawn from a Mermaid flowchart in the figure grammar.

## What a book shows

A module is shown whole, under its repository and path. A line added shows in green, a line removed in red; a replaced line shows both, with only the changed words marked. A new module shows whole in green, a deleted one whole in red. The headings naming the module and the kind of change, and one comment per proposal, are the book's only words of its own.

## Figures

A figure shows one thing and how it moves: boxes joined by arrows that carry verbs, so that box, verb, box reads as a sentence, readable on a phone in seconds.

**Removed** The forms list: comparison, the moved line, the dependency change, the figure proposed and replaced. The Mermaid diagram of the forms. "Every sketch carries its arrows before it is sent" and "a figure that lost an arrow is returned", which are the illustrator's role. "A sizable book carries several figures." The Pictures section, said once under Figures.
