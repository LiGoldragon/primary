# Prometheus phone Wi-Fi: fresh failure and WAN recovery scope

The living's exact request and retry are in `../vision/prometheusWifiReliability.md`. This report separates the observed boot-time WAN delay from the fresh phone radio failure. The phone's randomized MAC is not yet user-confirmed.

At 2026-09-21 20:33 UTC, Prometheus `goldragon.criome` on `wlp195s0` had hostapd, Kea DHCP4, dnsmasq, and nftables active; `br-lan` was `10.18.0.1/24`, `eno1` had DHCP `10.44.0.148` and default route via `10.44.0.1`, IPv4 forwarding was enabled, and HTTPS from Prometheus returned 200. Zeus over the AP at `10.18.0.108` used `10.18.0.1` as its gateway and also got HTTPS 200. Kea alone bound UDP 67; dnsmasq bound DNS 53. This proves one current client Internet path, not the phone's path.

The boot log shows hostapd, Kea, DNS, and nftables starting at 12:05 local, while `eno1` did not acquire its WAN DHCP address/default route until 12:28. Thus clients could join the AP for roughly 23 minutes without a WAN route. The later route acquisition followed networkd reconfiguration. CriomOS branch `field-wifi-wan-recovery-753e69`, remote `114fcbd10294f02a9536223720d778498cc7c58e`, adds a two-minute router WAN check that reconfigures only the declared WAN interface when carrier exists but its IPv4 default route is missing. It leaves AP, bridge, Kea, DNS, and networkd running. The branch is source only: no Prometheus generation, timer, or live network change is claimed. The focused check ran on Prometheus through Nix remote builders with local fallback disabled; module/timer evaluation and the no-carrier, healthy-route, missing-route, and failure-propagation cases passed. The full CriomOS flake gate still needs a real Lojix-provided system input; the default stub fails before tests.

After the living's fresh retry, hostapd logged station `5a:47:10:23:ef:12` associating at 14:42:47 local and completing WPA's four-way handshake. Kea received its request at 14:42:48, allocated `10.18.0.100`, and logged an attempted DHCPACK. The station remained in the AP table but went inactive; the router's neighbor entry for `10.18.0.100` was `FAILED`. Its roughly 80 transmitted packets had 306 retries and 13 failures, with 6 Mbit/s transmit and 1 Mbit/s receive rates at about -64 dBm. Other associated clients had stronger radio rates and far lower failure ratios. The AP reported 3 dBm transmit power; regulatory/PHY capability had earlier shown a 20 dBm ceiling. These facts support a radio/client-path problem for that station, but the MAC has not been confirmed as the living's phone, and the DHCPACK log proves a send attempt rather than client receipt.

The WAN retry source addresses the measured boot gap. It does not explain a phone failure while Zeus and Prometheus have Internet. The bounded phone diagnosis remains with 9ddcbc; 753e69 coordinates, and no one has changed AP power, restarted services, or activated a new generation. A live transmit-power trial remains held for phone-MAC confirmation, a recoverable prior state, and the required direct network attempt witness; it must preserve existing clients. Read-only work found no current Mind or Field Wi-Fi mutation in progress.
