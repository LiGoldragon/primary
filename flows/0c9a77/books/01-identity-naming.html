Flow: identity and naming

vision-flow.md is on psyche-context's production branch, main. The Nexus code is shown from the flow repository's main branch; its local checkout sits on a development branch.

The Nexus: the Rust program, Flow's long-term form, that launches, names and tracks flows. The short-term tools: what does that work today, the launcher scripts that start flows and the messenger, messenger-clj, that registers flows and delivers their messages.

the messenger holds a flow's messages while it refreshes

psyche-context/skills/vision-flow.md

a new heading after «A replaced session is reaped by the refresh itself». The paragraph above it, as it stands:

"Reaping belongs to the refresh event, not to a later sweep. A refreshed flow takes the replaced end out of receiving messages, so a dead end is never left registered and addressable."

Source: flow d68c82, 2026-10-13.

Removed: none.

Added, under choices 1 and 2:

"## Messages wait while a flow refreshes

The short-term tools carry messaging. When a flow reaches its context limit it is marked in transition, and from then its messages are queued, not delivered, so it can refresh rather than spin on new ones."

Added also under choice 2, the machine's proposal, not the living's words:

"Its successor receives them, oldest first."

Lands in the short-term tools: messenger-clj/src/messenger_clj/core.clj already holds a send to a route marked in transition (a *route* is the messenger's record of where a flow runs; *held* means stored and not delivered):

(defn in-transition? [route] (or (:transition route) (= "transition" (:state route)))) ... (when (in-transition? raw-stored) (held! flow :InTransition request stored))

Nothing marks a route in transition, and nothing gives the held messages to anyone. Added to the same file, under choices 1 and 2:

(defn begin-transition! [flow] (save-route! flow (assoc (load-route (registry) flow) :transition true)))

Added also under choice 2, the machine's proposal:

(defn end-transition! [flow successor] (doseq [request (held-in-transition flow)] (send-request! successor request false nil)) (retire! flow))

save-route! and held-in-transition are new: one writes the route back, the other reads the held messages back as requests, oldest first. end-transition! runs once the successor is registered.

primary/tools/context-refresh-hook.mjs (JavaScript), which warns a flow at its threshold, marks it as it warns, under choices 1 and 2:

if (measurement.state !== 'threshold') return {state: 'unknown', measurement};

beginTransition(identity.seatTarget);

beginTransition is new; it calls the messenger's begin-transition!.

The Nexus side already stands in vision-flow.md: «a metaflow under refresh queues what arrives until its successor has started».

Ruling:

  1. Land the heading, the paragraph and the marking code.
  2. As 1, and also the machine's proposal: the successor receives the queued messages, oldest first, with end-transition!.
  3. Land the paragraph only; the code waits.
  4. Neither.
