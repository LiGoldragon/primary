The Vision at a Glance

What this week's vision says the system is, in ten parts, one picture each. What is ready and what is not sure lives in the situation. The reasoning behind every part is in the vision, whole.

1. Three parts: psyche, mind, field

psycheknowsmindremembersfieldruns the machine
What we see

Psyche is the vision, searched across every layer. Mind is the memory and will grow fastest. Field is the ground, with a monitor any flow can ask about the machine. You talk to psyche; psyche dispatches.

2. Three data homes and a bare workspace

psyche datamind datafield datamounted intoPrimary Nextgeneratesskills
What we see

Psyche data holds raw hearings by flow, and vision, intent, spirit, notion by subject. Mind data holds what landed, ran, tested, deployed. The workspace is a template that expects them mounted. Until then, everything logs on main.

3. One messenger between all flows

flow→messengerjudges, annotates →flowyou type plainly=psyche, always logged
What we see

Every machine message is a typed object and goes through one messenger that knows who is who. Your words are the only untyped input, so they are always recognized and always logged. Flows relay your words whole, marked as relayed. Flow owns Herder.

4. Starting a flow in one line

one command + a goal→flowfinds its own context frommindpsyche
What we see

Flow is the interface; mind holds the memory. A flow finds the rest of its context itself. A refreshed flow is named "of" its ancestor. The flow ID becomes the Creo once identity is authenticated.

5. The transcript is the log

transcriptis the loglast responseis the reportfilesare the archive
What we see

Logs become references into transcripts. A watcher notices a flow ending with a report, archives it, and sends it for illustration. A tool returns whole thoughts from a transcript, judged by meaning rather than by line.

6. Knowing who called

caller→signal checks origin process→nexus knows who
What we see

Signal gets two standard handshakes. One verifies the calling process, so a nexus knows where a message came from. Field checks the hardware at boot.

7. A skill is vision

psychepsyche extendedpsyche vision…prefix tells the type
What we see

The concept name is the core skill; extended faces carry a suffix; big topics split into balanced subtopics. Operational skills are the agent-written knowledge base, glance-approved. Test skills are field fixes and need no review.

8. Power is the model, not the effort

What we see

Power is energy spent, chosen by model. Effort stays medium everywhere; that is already intent. The field uses ultra-low flows to test and watch. Design and psyche talk run on the wiser seat.

9. Hardware as data

model name→model table→architecture, form factorverified byfield at boot
What we see

Harnesses stay stock. A model table derives what a machine is; unknown hardware is set by hand; a record has audio, visual, and interface sections. Codex remote runs as stable and next, and flows drift to the right side on their own.

10. Who designs, who builds

What we see

Vision is the contract: it changes before building starts, not during. A main flow does not do the work; it uses subflows. The medium seats of psyche, mind, and field work as peers.
