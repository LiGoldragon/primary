## Book instructions are goal-oriented

Context: relayed by Psyche core Tertiary; on the book «Data out of code», whose prose was hard-wrapped by a flow following the 52-character rule.

"Yeah these instructions are too specific. ... They're making the agents confused about how they should actually make them, with proper instructions that would be goal-oriented, not like, "Put your left foot out of the door first and not your right foot." Who cares which? What are you getting out of the fucking door with? ... That's a stupid thing to waste content context on for training agents to start counting on their fingers ... It's fucking useless, useless fucking noise. ... We need to start trimming that out so that agents don't get caught up in tiny little narrow corridors of how they should do things."

-- psyche, STT.
