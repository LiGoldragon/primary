`watch-tests.py` uses blocking inotify readiness and parses owned files on filesystem events, deduplicating lifecycle rows by file/line. C4 and H6 each captured their tested normal end exactly once. The watcher was a bounded test instrument, not a production observer. Its deadline wait is process supervision, not polling for lifecycle state. It did not test rotation, truncation, queue overflow, crash recovery or multi-turn background wakeups. The two positive captures establish feasibility for those exact modes/runs, not delivery guarantees, supported transcript stability, global idleness or universality. A transcript watcher also cannot infer completion after an abrupt process death that wrote no end row.
