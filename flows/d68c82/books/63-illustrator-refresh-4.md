# Refreshing the Illustrator

The module that governs a flow's refresh today is shown as it stands; the vision proposals are beside it.

## field-context › compensation/launch.md, as it is

```text
A flow at or above its layer's configured refresh
threshold receives no further work. A Fable flow
receives no further work at 15% of its observed
context window, or 100,000 observed tokens. Its
secretary orders its successor started at once; the
successor takes its topics from the handover. Read
that threshold from the layer configuration. When the
configuration or a supported context observation is
unavailable, report that absence rather than
inventing a value. Retire a predecessor only after
its fresh successor has answered on a supported
route and its native role and binding have been
verified; then never reuse or message it.
```

## Proposal 1: psyche-context › vision/flow.md

+ A flow that never wakes back up is an ephemeral flow.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 2: psyche-context › vision/flow.md

+ An ephemeral flow is a continuous job flow: it takes jobs as long as it runs, its context cached.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 3: psyche-context › vision/flow.md

+ An ephemeral flow has a default size, up to which it runs jobs.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 4: psyche-context › vision/flow.md

+ When an ephemeral flow's context climbs too high, it gets no new jobs and another ephemeral flow takes them.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 5: psyche-context › vision/flow.md
