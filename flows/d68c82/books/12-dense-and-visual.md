# Dense and visual

## Crystallization

### 1. A book shows each change once

File: psyche-context/skills/vision-book.md, after line 8

  A book is a proposal into context modules, and the whole of it is its crystallization: a line removed shows in red, a line added in green, and a figure in it is a figure proposed for a module.
+ The living's time, energy and thinking are the most valuable resource. A book shows each change once, as visually and in as few words as it can, and quotes none of the words it crystallizes.

### 2. Modules are dense, and a statement lives in one

File: psyche-context/skills/vision-context-modules.md, after line 57

  Knowledge modules are audited from time to time for truth and currency against practice; a variation found goes to the type that holds what is unclear.
+ A context module is dense and precise: the fewest words for the largest effect, spirit and intent most of all. A statement lives in one module only.
