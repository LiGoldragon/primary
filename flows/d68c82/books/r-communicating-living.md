# Communicating with the living

This book is about how what the machine makes reaches you: how a figure is drawn, the three kinds of thing you are shown, and how code is put to you. The figure line now lives in the vision of books, so its change is proposed there.

## Proposal 1: psyche-context › skills/vision-book.md

- A figure shows how a thing moves, one question per figure, readable in seconds. Its grammar: a box is a thing or a collection; an arrow is a one-way relation carrying a verb, so that box, verb, box reads as a sentence; nearness and enclosure are kinship; top to bottom is order; colour carries a role or is absent. A list in boxes, a tally, or a sentence in a rectangle is not a figure. A diagram in a book's source is ASCII in that grammar, laid out vertically and no wider than 52 characters so a phone shows it; a sketch without arrows is rewritten before it is sent. The published book never shows the ASCII, but a figure drawn from it by a Tertiary or Quaternary subflow that is given the grammar with the sketch, keeps every box, label and arrow, and adds nothing the source does not say; a figure that lost an arrow is returned. A sizable book carries several figures.
+ A figure is a graph of concepts: nodes linked by edges, with the forks and joins its subject has, each label a few words.
+ An edge carries its verb, so that node, verb, node reads as a sentence; nearness and enclosure are kinship; top to bottom is order; colour carries a role or is absent.
+ A list in boxes, a tally, or a sentence in a rectangle is not a figure.
+ A diagram in a book's source is ASCII in that grammar, laid out vertically and no wider than 52 characters so a phone shows it; a sketch without arrows is rewritten before it is sent.
+ The published book never shows the ASCII, but a figure drawn from it by a Tertiary or Quaternary subflow that is given the grammar with the sketch, keeps every box, label and arrow, and adds nothing the source does not say; a figure that lost an arrow is returned.
+ The figures of a book link to one another, and a sizable book carries several.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 2: psyche-context › vision/living-communication.md, a new file

+ ## What reaches the living
+ What reaches the living is of three kinds.
+ Psyche he approves.
+ Mind he reviews: knowledge and inquiry.
+ Field he glances at: its metrics, the gist of its modules, and its oddities, seeming inconsistencies, misreadings of instructions and conflicts with higher modules.
+ A question to him is an inquiry: a type, presented as a context module.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.
