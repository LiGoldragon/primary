# Chronos

Chronos tells astrological time, and it is the simple example on which the Nexus and Ethos are tested. Its module, and the types Ethos takes directly from Rust, are now proposed in «Ethos, Nexus and Chronos: the vision». This edition carries the choices still open, each written at its default; amend one to choose another.

## Proposal 1: psyche-context › vision/chronos.md, a new file

+ A derivation is reached by the trait methods of the Library, which any client calls on an arc it holds.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.
