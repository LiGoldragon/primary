# Only psyche reaches you

Nothing in your words is ambiguous. The ambiguity is in the instructions this seat loads. Most of them tell a flow to propose a fix in whatever module "owns" the behaviour, and most behaviour is owned by machine modules. Only one sentence points the other way, and it describes what happens to a line after you review it, not which lines may reach you at all.

```
 you correct a behaviour
          |
          v
 +-------------------------------+
 | "present the line for the     |
 |  owning skill"                |
 +-------------------------------+
          |
          v
 +-------------------------------+
 | the owner is a Field or Mind  |
 | module                        |
 +-------------------------------+
          |
          v
 +-------------------------------+
 | "a vision-, intent- or other  |
 |  skill"  — so it is allowed   |
 +-------------------------------+
          |
          v
 +-------------------------------+
 | a book puts a machine line    |
 | in front of you               |
 +-------------------------------+
```

The sentences that sent proposals the wrong way:

- "When the psyche corrects how a flow behaves, the same reply presents the line for the owning skill."
- "A distilled statement lands as lines in a vision-, intent- or other skill's authored source on the living's explicit approval."
- "The reply gives its cause and the line that changes the behavior that produced it." This one names no kind of module.
- The book renderer's list of targets includes Operation, Compensation and Trial.

Only one sentence points the right way, in intent-psyche: anything you have reviewed is psyche, and leaves the Mind or Field module it sat in. No sentence says that only psyche lines are put to you.

The result today was 46 proposals across 15 books that targeted Field modules, Mind modules or tool files.

Every one of those machine sentences is now with Field, to be changed without your review. Field is also moving the 42 modules of retired types (32 operation- in Mind, 10 trial- in Field) into compensation or knowledge. What is left for you is the one missing sentence.
