# The heartbeat

Important topics stall when nobody comes back to them. A heartbeat looks at Flow, Ethos and the Nexus four times a day and gets stalled work moving again. Nothing is switched on until you rule.

## Proposal 1: psyche-context › vision/flow.md

+ Every six hours a heartbeat checks that Flow, Ethos and the Nexus are being carried forward.
+ Each topic has one owner, its current order, its latest useful result and any blocker, and the heartbeat reads these with the owner's real state.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 2: psyche-context › vision/flow.md

+ When the work is open and its owner sleeps with nothing queued, the heartbeat sends the owner one order.
+ When the owner is gone or blocked, the heartbeat tells the responsible Secondary what is wrong, and starts no competing flow.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 3: psyche-context › vision/flow.md

+ Opus guides the work, and Fable's ruled design travels with it; the heartbeat wakes Fable only on the living's word.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 4: psyche-context › vision/flow.md

+ After each major effort, the owner hands in a report book: what now works, what remains, and what needs the living.
+ A new comment on Flow, Ethos or the Nexus refreshes the affected proposals at once.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.
