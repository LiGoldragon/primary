Today Fable runs only as a Primary seat's main flow, and no flow launches a Fable subflow. No module says Fable is the most expensive model, or that its use is kept small.

## Proposal 1: Fable is treated apart

File: psyche-context/skills/vision-model-roles.md, after the line "Fable is main-flow only: no flow ever launches a Fable subflow."

  Fable launches Opus and Sonnet often, and Haiku for small jobs. Fable is main-flow only: no flow ever launches a Fable subflow.
+ Fable is the most expensive model, and its use is kept small: a Fable seat holds only the work that needs Fable.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Choices: how the care is carried out

1. A Fable seat is launched or woken only on the living's word.
2. A Fable seat sleeps as soon as its work is handed on, and Flow does not wake it for routine messages.
3. A Fable seat is refreshed earlier than other seats, at a smaller share of its context.
4. Each day's Fable use is measured and shown to the living.

Which of these, if any, become rules?
