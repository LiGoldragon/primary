# Flow: main-flow mode and roles

This book is about what makes a flow a main flow, what role each flow carries, and how flows of different layers speak to one another. It also covers who may launch whom, where the layer-to-model table lives, Psyche's three own topics, the operator's notes, and a Primary that only poses questions. Where the first edition offered choices, each line below is the reading the records support best, for you to land, amend or reject.

## Proposal 1: psyche-context › skills/intent-main-flow.md

+ You write no code and no script.
+ A small thing to run is a brief to a subflow on a low layer, which writes it, runs it and reports what happened.
+ Your model and your context are the dearest in your aspect; what a subflow can do with the right guidance, a subflow does.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 2: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ What makes a flow a main flow is in its system prompt, and only there, where it outweighs the harness's own instructions.
+ Each flow is loaded with the context modules its role needs.
+ The module for a bounded job is loaded into the subflow that does it: a declared subflow with its own modules and instructions, which a main flow calls by name.
+ For the main flow, the module is the subflow; for the subflow, the module is a module.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 3: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ A flow is a struct of its flow id and its role, and nothing that the role already carries.
+ The role is an enum.
+ A main flow's role is its aspect, carrying its topic and its layer.
+ Every other role is a job run on the smallest, most concentrated context that can be assembled for it: a system audit, living interaction, an implementation, a vision audit, and the roles yet to be made, each aspect having variants of its own.
+ What a role needs is the data of its variant.
+ The effort is the data of the model variant, each model having efforts of its own.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 4: psyche-context › skills/vision-model-roles.md

- | Layer | Claude (Psyche) | OpenAI (Mind, Field) |
- |---|---|---|
- | Primary | Fable | Astra |
- | Secondary | Opus | Sol |
- | Tertiary | Sonnet | Luna |
- | Quaternary | Haiku | Luna, low effort |
- Every seat runs at medium effort, except the OpenAI Quaternary.
- ### OpenAI side
- Luna launches only Luna. Sol is not launched lightly; it is getting expensive. Astra launches Sol and Luna — try Luna first. Astra is main-flow only: no flow ever launches an Astra subflow.
- ### Claude side
- Sonnet launches Sonnet and Haiku. Opus launches Sonnet and Haiku, and sometimes Opus, but rarely. Fable launches Opus and Sonnet often, and Haiku for small jobs. Fable is main-flow only: no flow ever launches a Fable subflow.
- The layer selects the configured model through the table above, so the title names the layer, never the model.

+ A Quaternary launches Quaternaries.
+ A Tertiary launches Tertiaries and Quaternaries.
+ A Secondary launches Tertiaries and Quaternaries, and a Secondary rarely, since a Secondary is not launched lightly.
+ A Primary launches Secondaries and Tertiaries often, and Quaternaries for small jobs, the lower layer tried first.
+ A Primary is main-flow only: no flow launches a Primary subflow.
+ The layer selects the configured model through the layer-to-model vision, so the title names the layer, never the model.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 6: psyche-context › skills/intent-main-flow.md

+ You speak across at your own layer, one layer up in your own aspect, or to any layer below, in any aspect.
+ A Secondary is its Primary's secretary: it keeps track of what the Primary works on, and what climbs from below reaches the Primary through it.
+ A Primary designs and implements through its own subflows.
+ Below the Primary, you ask the layer above for authority and a mandate when you are not sure, and you say that you do not know: the layer above infers better than you.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 7: psyche-context › skills/vision-model-roles.md

- Horizontal communication joins aspects at the same layer. Vertical communication stays within one aspect and normally advances one layer at a time.
+ Horizontal communication joins aspects at the same layer.
+ Speech climbs one layer at a time, within one aspect, never skipping a layer.
+ It descends to any layer, across aspects too.
+ The Primary layer is spoken to least.

## Proposal 11: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ A flow poses a question in its reply and makes no call for it.
+ A hook forwards the question as the turn ends, and the answer comes back queued for the flow's next turn, with whatever else was queued for it, the psyche's new words among them.
+ The Psyche Primary, so served, has hardly any tool access, and its context goes to judging, inferring and summarizing; the shoveling is the lower layers'.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.
