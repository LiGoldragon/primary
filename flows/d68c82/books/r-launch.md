# Flow: launch and lean start

This book is about how a flow is started: what its first prompt holds, who gives it its name, what it says first, and who may start it again. Its lines come from what you said between mid-September and mid-October, and none of them has been ruled yet. Where the first edition offered choices, this edition takes one reading for you to amend.

## Proposal 1: psyche-context › skills/intent-startup-prompt.md

+ The order of the startup skills in the block carries nothing.
+ The block is lean: the flow's id, its mode, its modules and its brief, and nothing else.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 2: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ The process that starts a flow claims its id and makes its directory before the flow starts.
+ The id stands in the startup prompt and in a file the flow cannot write, where its subflows find it.
+ No flow or subflow claims an id.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 3: psyche-context › skills/intent-main-flow.md

- You run a shell command for two things only: claiming your Flow ID, and the writes you own: your log, your psyche records, and your summary. Leave Primary edits for the sole standing committer; never stage, commit, push or take PrimaryPublish yourself. In other repositories, commit and push your own changes. Everything else that reaches into the harness, the cluster, or the code goes through a subflow.
+ You run a shell command for the writes you own only: your log, your psyche records, and your summary.
+ Your Flow ID was claimed for you as you started and stands in your startup prompt.
+ Leave Primary edits for the sole standing committer; never stage, commit, push or take PrimaryPublish yourself.
+ In other repositories, commit and push your own changes.
+ Everything else that reaches into the harness, the cluster, or the code goes through a subflow.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 4: psyche-context › skills/intent-startup-prompt.md

+ ## The first response is a presentation
+ A new flow's first response is a presentation of its context.
+ Having loaded it, the flow has subflows question and reinforce it, then distills it for its role, as a proposal for how to make things better.
+ Psyche distills into vision, intent and spirit; Mind into knowledge, questions and implementation; Field into its own roles.
+ No flow is told to answer that it is ready.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 5: psyche-context › skills/vision-design.md

+ ## A flow is given its mode at start
+ A flow is told at start whether it designs or builds a prototype; it does not find out.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 6: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ A main flow is reachable remotely from the moment it starts, and is seen on the desktop in a normally coloured terminal.
+ Short term: every flow is started with the harness's permission prompts off, and a flow that was not is relaunched.
+ Short term: the launcher starts every flow under Herdr.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 7: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ Short term: the launcher installs a hook on every event the harness offers, each calling the messenger with the flow's configuration.
+ What is done with the event is the messenger's.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 8: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ Flow knows which harness a flow runs in and what that harness can do.
+ How a flow is spawned and spoken to takes the harness's own form.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 9: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ Short term: every new or refreshed flow starts in a Herdr pane, and the launcher registers it with the messenger only once the pane's agent is seen bound to the flow's session.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 10: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ Flow's start is one central call carrying every setting of a launch.
+ A shorthand fills most of it from a declared flow, so that a launch asks little of whoever starts it, and the simple command the living remembers is a shorthand.
+ Every main feature has this shape: one central call and its shorthands.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 11: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ Every flow that may be started is declared in Flow's configuration with its model and effort, and a launch takes what is declared.
+ An effort no declared flow uses is refused, as is a second flow on one role.
+ What is added to a flow's prompt comes from files, named in that declaration.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 12: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ A Primary is never without its Secondary.
+ Starting a Primary first starts the Secondary of its metaflow, and a Primary found running without one has it started.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 13: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ A Primary is launched in the shared workspace on main, not in a worktree or on a branch, unless the launch names another place.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 14: psyche-context › skills/vision-flow.md

+ A flow is restarted by a command to Flow.
+ A request whose flow id matches the flow's provenance carries all the authority needed.
+ A harness that locks itself out of relaunching its own flow is a design defect.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.
