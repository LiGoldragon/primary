# Why your orders get lost

```
 your order
     |
     v
 handed to another seat
     |
     v
 that seat waits on a
 broken start
     |
     v
 nobody tells you;
 nobody does it another way
```

Three causes, each seen this week.

- The Ethos, Nexus and Chronos edition was handed to the Ethos seat's refresh, which waits on the broken starter. Your order stood still and you were not told.
- Book headings named file locations from a layout planned but not built, and one proposal put a module in the tools folder. Nothing checked the location against the files, and no rule said where modules live.
- This seat carries many open orders at once, kept only in its conversation, which compaction shortens.

## Proposal 1: mind-context › skills/operation-main-flow.md

+ When work the living ordered waits on another seat, the flow tells the living what it waits on in the same turn, and starts the work by another route.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 2: mind-context › skills/operation-main-flow.md

+ Every location a book's proposal names is checked against the files before the book is published.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 3: mind-context › skills/operation-main-flow.md

+ A main flow keeps every open order of the living in one list in its flow directory, read at the start of every turn and after every compaction; an order leaves the list only when done or withdrawn.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.
