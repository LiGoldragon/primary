### Proposal 1: Replace · psyche-context › vision/book.md

**Comment** These lines replace the one that shows each alternative as its own proposal, because that form repeats the text alternatives share and leaves the reader to find what differs. The two wordings say the same thing; they differ in how they name the comment and the difference.
