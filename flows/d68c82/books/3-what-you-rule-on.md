# What you rule on sits in a proposal

You said you read almost nothing in a book outside its proposals. A book is for you to approve or reject something by commenting on it.

The book skill tells a book to open with context. It never says where a finding or a stand-in goes when you must judge it. In «The Source nexus», the hash was written as four Integers because ethos has no byte type. That stand-in sat in the context, where you only came across it by chance.

## Distillation

### Proposal 1: in compensation-book-distillation

File: `field-skills/skills/compensation-book-distillation.md`. As it stands, the paragraph above the change reads:

A book opens with the context its proposals rest on, conceptual and as visual as possible: how things are, what is wrong or out of line with vision, what is highlighted; the one operational fact it states is whether a thing is in production or in a development branch, and what that development is about. How the system runs today is knowledge, landed without the living's review; no runtime version, socket or store path, or operational command reaches a book; the authored file targeted by a proposal stays named.

Added, as a new paragraph after it:

Whatever the living is to rule on, notice or approve, a fork, a stand-in or a finding, sits inside a proposal; nothing outside a proposal asks his judgement.
