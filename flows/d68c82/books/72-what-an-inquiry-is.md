### Proposal 1: an inquiry states what Mind is still working out · psyche-context › vision/context-modules.md

Mind's book «Approved vision into working systems» filed six inquiries that read as decided plans with two questions appended.

- Mind's second type is inquiry: it holds what is unclear, and what an audit finds varying from stated knowledge.
+
+ An inquiry holds what Mind is still working out.
+ Each inquiry states its open question specifically, what is already known, what is being tried, and what would settle it.
+ An inquiry is how the machine comes up with question books for the living: each open question goes to him specific enough to accept, comment on, or reject.
+ His answer to an inquiry becomes knowledge or vision.
+ A variation an audit finds between knowledge and practice becomes an inquiry.
+
  Knowledge modules are audited from time to time for truth and currency against practice; a variation found goes to the type that holds what is unclear.
