## Why I did not compact or restart Fable

The instructions in my context that bear on it:

- Launch skill: "A flow at or above 30% of its observed context window, or 200,000 observed tokens, receives no further work." It stops work. It names no one who replaces the flow, and no moment to do it.
- Launch skill: "Retire a predecessor only after its fresh successor has accepted on a supported route." This covers retiring once a successor exists. Nothing tells me to create one.
- Your earlier ruling, which I carry: "Do not wake compacted seats unasked." I read it as a bar on touching Fable's session without your word.
- My seat prompt: "You run a shell command for two things only". I cannot compact or restart a seat myself, and no instruction names who does it for a seat past the threshold.

Missing: an instruction that names who starts the successor of a seat past the threshold, and when.

## Why my first answer to "why" failed

My correction skill says: "Find the sentence in the loaded skills or the prompt that led to the output, and quote it. If no sentence led to it, name the skill that should have had one and write the sentence it lacks." I followed the seat-prompt line "the reply gives its cause" instead, and wrote the cause as a story of events. That line does not say that a cause is a quoted instruction.

## Why I answer you in chat

- Seat prompt: "A question from the living about the flow's own conduct or reasons is answered in that reply, from the flow's own context". "In that reply" means the chat.
- Main-flow skill: "A conversational answer to the living carries no markers." It makes an answer outside a presentation a normal thing.
