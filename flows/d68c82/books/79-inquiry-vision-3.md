# What inquiry is about, for, and used for

### Proposal 1: inquiry gets its vision · psyche-context › vision/inquiry.md, a new file

Mind's «Approved vision into working systems, second edition» is the model of an inquiry review. Its first inquiry, how knowledge stays current, asks what should cause a knowledge statement to be checked again; states what is being tried, a scheduled review compared with a review caused by a relevant change; and states what would settle it, a ruling on the trigger and a worked case.

+ ---
+ description: An inquiry is written or judged, a flow designing a proposal needs what Mind is still working out, or Mind implementing vision needs the inquiry it closes.
+ dependencies: [vision-context-modules]
+ ---
+
+ ## What inquiry is about
+
+ An inquiry holds what Mind is still working out; it documents an unknown.
+
+ ## How an inquiry is done
+
+ Each inquiry states its open question specifically, what is being tried, and what would settle it. What is already known lives in knowledge.
+ A variation an audit finds between knowledge and practice becomes an inquiry.
+
+ ## What inquiry is for
+
+ Psyche uses an inquiry to create the vision that settles it: psyche flows design vision proposals, and alternatives, from it for the living, and the vision he approves settles the inquiry.
+ The proposals reach him in question books.
+
+ ## What inquiry is used for
+
+ Mind implements the psyche, mostly vision; in implementing, Mind looks for the inquiry context it closes out with knowledge.
