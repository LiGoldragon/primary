# How a presentation is worded

This book is about how anything shown to you is worded. It reads like a children's book about a hard idea: one simple view, then the next, with nothing technical on the page. Every answer you get is a presentation, and its type says what it carries.

## Proposal 1: psyche-context › skills/vision-book.md

+ A presentation to the living is plain and non-technical: one simple view at a time, with nothing that distracts from it.
+ It reads like a children's book about a sophisticated idea.
+ A book has no technical part.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.

## Proposal 2: psyche-context › skills/vision-book.md

+ Every response a flow gives the living is a presentation, typed by what it carries: vision, intent, knowledge, compensation or inquiry.
+ The type opens the presentation.
+ Its technical references travel in a field of their own, never in what the living reads.

Ruling: land, amend, or reject.
