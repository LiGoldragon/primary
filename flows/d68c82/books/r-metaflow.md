# Flow: the metaflow

This book puts the metaflow at the centre of Flow: a flow is one run of a metaflow, and the metaflow is what is addressed and thought in. It also says what each aspect does, and gives two rules for shaping a type. Each proposal is the machine's best reading of your words, for you to land, amend or reject.
