# Refreshing the Illustrator

Three recent books were illustrated four ways, two figures each, and the cost of each way was measured. The quality was the same; only the cost differed.

| how the Illustrator continues | cost for the three books |
|---|---|
| keeps one long conversation | 0.19 dollars |
| starts fresh for every job | 0.22 dollars |
| compacts after every two jobs | 0.25 dollars |
| compacts after every job | 0.34 dollars |

Compaction is the dearest at every size. A fresh start costs a little more than keeping one conversation, but it carries only how to illustrate, and your feedback goes into its modules instead of piling up in a conversation.

## Proposal 1: psyche-context › vision/flow.md

+ The Illustrator starts fresh for every job and is never compacted.
