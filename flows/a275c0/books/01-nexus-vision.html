A Nexus has three parts, each its own ethos specification: Signal is what it says, Operation is what it does, Memory is what it remembers. Nexus always names the whole; the part that does is Operation. Every effect has a matching operation type. A signal reaches memory only through operation; memory answers operation that the change succeeded or failed; the answer returns through operation and leaves as a signal. Reading a Nexus's ethos alone shows every object and process on that path. + + The three parts, and how each of them divides, are most of the machine's anatomy. + + A standard entry point, like a Rust macro, enforces the three parts and their one path, so nothing bypasses them; the bodies are written by hand.
