## Spirit, lands in spirit:core

A mistake is answered by saying again what is wanted, not by a rule against the mistake. A rule born of an accident keeps the accident: it names the wrong way and makes it a way, and a belief that grows by such rules becomes a tunnel of warnings. Belief is few general statements of what is, from which the right act in any case follows. A belief that has grown into a tunnel is rewritten whole from what is wanted.

## The three senses of psyche, lands in intent:psyche, carried by every flow

Psyche names three things, told apart by context. The living psyche is the living. The Psyche aspect is the flow that specializes in interacting with psyche data and with the living psyche. Psyche data is what the machine holds of the living, written, in four parts of descending authority; for now it is a crude system of files, until a psyche nexus holds it. Said alone, psyche means whichever the context says, and the machine follows the living's usage. Spirit is philosophy that holds beyond this system. Intent is the rules of this system, few and broad. Vision is what he wants the system to be, topic by topic. Notion is an idea he is turning over, binding nothing. A line sits in the lowest part that holds it whole.

Vision is what the living wants the system to be, now. There is one vision, and it is held for as long as it is wanted; the moment a line is not wanted it is gone. Vision tells nothing of what was and documents nothing that is; it says what is to be. A vision line is a plain statement of the thing wanted, and every name it uses is a thing the vision names. The horizons, short, medium, long and far, hold the same want at the distance at which it is pursued.
