## Flow, the design

Flow is the Nexus that launches a seat. It makes the place the seat runs, which is the Capsule, first as the semi-sandbox that copies only credentials. It chooses the system prompt and the first prompt, starts the harness, and learns every event through the harness's hooks calling the Flow command. Nothing polls.

A refresh builds the successor, then reaps the predecessor. Flow locks sessions. Orchestrate locks files.

A seat is a voice: an aspect carrying a layer, twelve voices in all. The model behind a layer is configuration in a knowledge skill. A title is the voice and the flow id in words, for example Psyche.Secondary abandonAbilityAble.

The id in words is a reversible rendering of 33 bits of the harness's own id. Tools find a transcript from the words. The camelCase three-word shape is the interface wanted. The word codec is generic over hash sizes, a kind that yields words, and belongs in a library every component reuses.

There are two registries. One holds the true ids. The other holds only voices, so a message to a voice reaches whoever holds it now. The voice syntax is psyche.primary, a variant holding a variant.

Anything deterministic is done by code, never by a model. The flow gets its id in its prompt.

## How seats speak

Speech climbs one layer at a time. The Primary layer is spoken to least. Field reaches Psyche through Mind. This is guidance, not enforced on the wire. Each seat weighs whether a thing is worth the higher seat's attention.

## Books and topics

Topics become variants in the Nexus components. A new variant is submitted, approved, added to the ethos spec, and the whole is recompiled. Books are kept as a series by subject, topic and subtopic.

New vision or notion on a topic is routed to the flow that deals with that topic. It is delivered with that flow's next messages, after a minimum window, not pushed each time.

Mind's relay design for this: the hearing flow classifies the record as vision, notion or instruction. A recorder keeps the exact quote and its source. A durable queue delivers to the current holder with a receipt and replays after a restart. No polling, and no automatic distillation. An unread notice for the holder's next turn is the recommended delivery. It does not yet route by topic. That is being added.

A whole accounting system tracks quota, priority and where context is spent next. It is envisioned, not built.
