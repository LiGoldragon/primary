A refresh never leaves the system without a core Primary · psyche-context › vision/flow.md, and vision/short/flow.md, a new file

Your words: "once you reach your context limit, your current flow doesn't get killed, and ... this problem that is keeping you from starting a new flow doesn't prevent the new flow from starting (which would leave us without a primary core)." Read in the Flow that serves today, 0.40.4, which is not the 0.37.1 our knowledge module names: on a maintained metaflow the successor starts first, and the predecessor is closed only after the successor is confirmed started; when the successor's composition or launch is refused, the predecessor stays awake with its queue kept, nothing stays locked, and the refresh is retried at the flow's next rest. So the killing you feared cannot happen on that path. What stands open: the kept predecessor receives only pressing messages, soft ones are held until a successor stands; the refusal is written to the journal and reaches no flow; a metaflow that is not maintained is closed first and composed at its next wake. The one composer refusal in the journal that evening, "invalid, duplicate or cross-placement module ID", was a launch, not a refresh; which metaflow it was for I could not tell from the log. The perennial line changes the refresh paragraph; the short lines name the three gaps. Your "we might even be contemplating compacting you before this dreadful possibility" is the last short line.

psyche-context › vision/flow.md

A flow is refreshed when its context passes its model's threshold: Flow keeps the metaflow's next messages for its next flow, and the flow is replaced as it goes idle; while the refresh runs the metaflow is locked. The successor starts first, and the predecessor is reaped in only once the same eventsuccessor is confirmed started, so a refresh never leaves a metaflow, the core Primary of an aspect above all, without a flow. A successor refused leaves the predecessor serving, and the refusal, with its cause, reaches the metaflow's Secondary. Each model's threshold is configured in Flow; an OpenAI model's is lower than a Claude model's.

psyche-context › vision/short/flow.md

A refused successor

Every core metaflow is maintained: its successor is composed and started before its predecessor is closed. A predecessor kept after a refused successor is served as any awake flow, soft requests included, until its successor stands. The refusal reaches the metaflow's Secondary as a Notice naming its cause, and Flow retries the refresh at the flow's every rest and at every request queued for it. A predecessor whose successor is still refused when its context reaches its model's ceiling compacts, and the compaction is reported as the refresh that was missed.

+ ; to { Psyche core Secondary }, at the refusal
+ Notice.«Refresh of { Psyche core Primary }
+         refused: ComposerRejected, duplicate
+         module ID. b2b87a kept serving; soft
+         requests delivered; retry at its rest.»
