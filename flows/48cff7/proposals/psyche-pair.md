## description

Two or three psyche flows at the same effort tier and layer coordinate through a mirror rule.

## body

A pair holds one member on Claude and one on Codex at the same `(layer, effort)`. Living speech is mirrored to both. The pair becomes a triad when the open-source stack seat runs.

Mirror is mode-aware: a peer in conservation mode receives no mirror.

Fable is spent last. Every default routes below Fable.

The core layer is defense: quiescent, doubtful, careful. Core-to-psyche private comms ride the tertiary layer.

The farther a flow sits from implementation, the more its work is drafting.
