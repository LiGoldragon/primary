The Mind

A fact about the system, tools, models, or the world must be recorded or looked up with its date and how much it is trusted.

What a record carries

structure

Facts live in the mind, not in a skill and not in the psyche. Each record carries what the fact asserts, the date it entered, a trust variant, its evidence, and its subject — five fields, always present, queryable independently.

Trust variants

four states, one axis

Verified
Round-tripped through a check.
Attested
A trusted source said so.
Provisional
Inferred, needs check.
Deprecated
Superseded, kept for history.

How a fact reaches the store

mind-nexus, ordinary and meta sockets, sema store

What follows from this

boundaries the proposal draws

  • A skill describes mechanics only — a fact it would assert belongs in the mind instead, as a reference, not inline prose.
  • The psyche and the mind split cleanly by kind: the psyche records what the living has said; the mind records what the system knows to be true.
  • Trust is a first-class field, not a footnote — every fact sits at exactly one of four variants, and moving between them is itself worth recording.
  • Until mind-nexus runs, nothing is blocked: a fact is held in a psyche entry explicitly marked "provisional pending mind" rather than left unrecorded.
  • Both CLIs take one inline datom each — no batching, no session state, one fact per call.
