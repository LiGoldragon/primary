## The kinds

Skill derivatives (per subject `<S>`):

- `<S>` — the skill body. Imperative, minimal, positive-shape.
- `<S>-rationale` — the why, the concepts, the historical context. Loaded by psyche-facing flows or on demand.
- `<S>-extended` — the broader, general aspect of the domain. For an agent that wants the wider view, not the sharpened action.
- `<S>-experimental` — in-flight material being tried; not standing.
- `<S>-undecided-proposal` — drafted, awaiting the living's word.

Psyche levels (author-side, not skill derivatives):

- **Notion** — a brainstorm, binding nothing. Lives at `flows/<flow>/notion/<topic>.md`.
- **Vision** — what can be seen clearly to try now. "Let's implement it." Lives at `Vision/<topic>.md` (distilled) or `flows/<flow>/vision/<topic>.md` (raw).
- **Intent** — where we want to take the project. Lives at `Intent/<topic>.md`. Distilled only.
- **Spirit** — how we approach everything, how we work, how we behave. Lives in the spirit skill.

## The Curriculum-nexus

The current Curriculum is authored source at `/git/github.com/LiGoldragon/Curriculum/skills`, generated into `.claude/skills/`, `.codex/`, `.agents/`, `.pi/` per CLAUDE.md. The living has now named that Curriculum will become a Nexus.

Proposed shape, per the loaded `nexus` skill: `curriculum-nexus` binary; two sockets; sema store; `signal-curriculum` with variants for each derivative kind (`Body`, `Rationale`, `Extended`, `Experimental`, `UndecidedProposal`) and each psyche level (`Notion`, `Vision`, `Intent`, `Spirit`); `curriculum` and `curriculum-meta` CLIs each taking one inline datom. Deterministic naming from `(subject, kind)` → filename means no author picks the filename by hand.

## Consequences for prior work

- `flows/48cff7/proposals/*.md` — these fit the `-undecided-proposal` kind. Once accepted, they promote to skill body (or into the appropriate derivative) at deterministic names generated by the Curriculum-nexus.
- The existing `<skill>-rationale` pattern already in skill-designing aligns exactly with the taxonomy's `-rationale` kind.
- Distilled Vision (`Vision/*.md`) and Intent (`Intent/*.md`) folders already exist; the taxonomy leaves them where they are and names them.
