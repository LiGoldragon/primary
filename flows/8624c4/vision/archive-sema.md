## Rich expression: meaning beyond the text

Context: answering a survey of annotation standards and tools for context-module lines.

"I don't really see the point of all these half-ass solutions. Besides the annotations would not be read separately. I call it rich expression. Maybe the term paralinguistic also applies. It's beyond just the text. It relates also to what I want to do with AI-driven speech interpretation, where I want the tone, the cadence, the hesitation, the change of pitch, and things like that.

Eventually long term, I would even see it learning the speech style of a certain person to start picking up on particular nuances that this individual is expressing through his speech. Even facial expression is going to come in, body expression, heart rate, blood pressure, and eye movements, eye iris dilation and contraction. There's no limit to how much I see the system breathing the living through as many channels with as many nuances as possible.

In the same way or in parallel on a similar topic, this rich expression format, Sema, right? The language of meaning. It's more than just language. Now it's meaning. It's way richer than just language. It uses language, obviously, as a medium to express meaning.

If we had a speech-to-sema, basically, the text that you're going to get would probably be three times as big or maybe twice as big. ... possibly even twice as big. I don't know. I guess it would really depend on the individual event, on the individual dictation, on the individual reading, and whether or not more things are being read than just audio. Is there video? Is there a heart monitor? Is there, etc., etc.

I think I would love to start developing Sema and we should, I think. We need to start on a design and it's going to be a long road."

-- psyche, STT.

## Sema is only the dialect of meaning; the database is memory

Context: book comment on the book Sema, on the proposed description "Sema, the language of meaning, or Sema, the database engine of a Nexus, and its record types ...".

"No, Sema is not the database anymore. That name was taken away from that and I gave instructions to change the name of the repo, the name of the library, every mention of Sema as a database system, and every code that uses the old name (obviously, which is changed to memory). Absolutely every trace of the concept that Sema is a database must be completely and utterly eradicated and replaced by the concept that it is memory and that Sema is and only is the [protos] dialect to represent meaning."

-- psyche, STT, book comment. Transcription corrected: "protose" → "protos".
