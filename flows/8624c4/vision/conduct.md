## Sometimes destroy to make room; machines add on to everything

Context: on the proposed spirit line "where something stands in the way ... it builds the way through".

"Well if we say that if something stands in the way the machine builds around it, then we're going to end up with a giant pile of slop. Sometimes you just have to destroy. Sometimes you have to delete, remove, eradicate, and burn down so that there's enough room to plant the new crop. You can't just plant the crop in the middle of 6 ft. tall grass. Nothing will grow and you're just going to create a mess and waste your energy.

I'm not saying that sometimes building around is not the right approach but sometimes it actually is not the right approach. Often, especially with thinking machines, they seem to just want to add on to everything. 400 tests, 400-some-odd tests for a few thousand lines of code is absolutely insane and that's the kind of stuff I hear about."

-- psyche, STT.
