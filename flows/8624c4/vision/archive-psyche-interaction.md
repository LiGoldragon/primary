Unless multiple children are going to die, obviously, then you don't. Otherwise you just get the baseball bat out and you ravage anything and destroy anything that gets in the way of doing what I've told you to do last, which is the most important thing for you to do. Otherwise we're just going to go in circles and chase our own tail forever."
