# Datom syntax distillation proposal

Second cut, after the living's correction that the first cut mixed
datom with ethos: datom is data. Only statements about the text form
of data remain. Nothing stands until the living approves it.

## Syntax — additions

- S1. The dot opens a delimiter: a Head is bare text ending in a dot
  written immediately before the delimiter it opens, with no space.
- S2. The old request form wrapping a verb and its noun in
  parentheses is obsolete. A request is a root variant carrying its
  Head.
- S3. A position expecting a map holds a bracket block of its entries
  and nothing else: no Head names the map, since the position already
  knows its type.
- S4 (under consideration). Map entries resolve by position: key,
  value, key, value. A Head is then always a variant.

## Repository and migration — addition

- R1. No Dotos files remain.

## Meaning — addition

- M1. The name Meaning is provisional — it smells of a verb — and is
  reopened together with the type.

## Open — new section

Questions raised or left unruled; no statement proposed:

- O1. Numbers: how a decimal or float is written, and whether a float
  position reads a dotted number such as a version.
- O2. Comments: early examples used a double semicolon; no ruling.
- O3. Newlines and indentation: no ruling.
- O4. Absent values: no ruling.
- O5. Whether a Head is ever other than a variant — asked by the
  living; the map-entry key is the one dot-prefix in data that names
  no variant.

## Withdrawn from the first cut as not datom

- Shape vocabulary, bare-symbol naming, parsing contexts, the
  shape-to-type table, logic planes, one parser — protos substrate.
- Realize and Textualize, real and signal forms — protos traits and
  forms of a value, not the data text.
- Guillemet reservation, colon in string position, imperative variant
  names, typed-input-only programs — Ethos syntax, type declaration,
  and program architecture.
- Where datom is used (wire, config, registry, harness) — usage, not
  syntax.
- The structured-string design details — too early by the living's
  own word.
