Presentation.{ «Quota pacing: how much, until when» }

### 1. Lead with the time remaining

**Hypothetical example:** 40% remains, with five hours until the 02:00 reset.

Show those facts together, followed by **8 percentage points per hour to use the remainder by reset**. Keep the local reset time visible. Each quota window has its own numbers.

### 2. Show a chosen plan separately

If the plan is to use that whole remainder between 21:00 and 22:00, the planning rate is **40 percentage points per planned hour**, while the clock still shows five hours.

A different plan could reserve a share for autonomous overnight work. The tool must not invent either choice or infer it from when you send messages.

### 3. Keep allowance separate from measured use

The remaining-time rate is a budget calculation. It is not a measurement of recent consumption or a forecast of exhaustion.

Those forecasts need compatible usage history. Missing history stays visible as unavailable. The existing weekly pacing reference remains available.

### 4. One optional policy remains

The clock-time display can ship now without a schedule decision.

For the additional planned-hours view: **which usage intervals should it plan around, and should it reserve a share for autonomous overnight work?** That plan may be a one-off stretch of time rather than a recurring routine.
