# «Metaflows that expand and contract: three to seven, by demand and availability»: rulings

## Summary

Every line below is the flow's proposal; none is his word.

1. The floor: three Metaflows that never close, the trimūrti acts each with its guṇa (strand) and graha (planet): keeping, sthiti (maintenance), sattva (clarity), Candra (Moon); making, sṛṣṭi (creation), rajas (activity), Budha (Mercury); clearing, saṃhāra (dissolution), tamas (inertia), Śani (Saturn). They are voices: Metaflows with no known ending.
2. The lattice: four Metaflows open out of a floor line of the same guṇa (BPHS 3.22): Sūrya (Sun) out of keeping by a ground above; Maṅgala (Mars) out of clearing by function; Śukra (Venus) out of making by the recipient kāraka (role); Guru (Jupiter) out of keeping by delegation. The full seven, in Chaldean order: Śani, Guru, Maṅgala, Sūrya, Śukra, Budha, Candra.
3. Contraction: a line folds into its lattice parent with its topics, queued messages and open proposals; its flow takes no new work and is reaped at idle; the floor never folds.
4. Demand and availability: pressure = queued messages + living records + 2 × open proposals + flows past the changeover share; availability = quota left against the 1/7-a-day rhythm, and a seat free. Readings at events, never on a timer; one change per reading.
5. Clusters: a goal- or ruling-scoped set of 3, then 5, then 12 Metaflows, 12 offered as 4 × 3 like the rāśi (signs); a purpose, an end condition, a judge, a home line that takes its leftovers.

## Rulings

1. The base triad.
   (a) The trimūrti acts with their guṇa and graha, as drawn: the acts are a cycle of work, the guṇa lets the seven inherit by BPHS 3.22, and pañcakṛtya keeps the three intact when more are added. The flow's recommendation. Contested: the trimūrti is late and Purāṇic.
   (b) The guṇa alone, as qualities of work rather than acts.
   (c) The vyāhṛti (bhūḥ earth, bhuvaḥ the space between, svaḥ heaven), with the seven loka (worlds) as the lattice instead of the seven graha.
2. The order of opening.
   (a) By demand: whichever closed line crosses the threshold opens; ties go in the lattice order Sūrya, Maṅgala, Śukra, Guru. The flow's recommendation: his "based on demand and availability".
   (b) Fixed, in the lattice order Sūrya, Maṅgala, Śukra, Guru.
   (c) Chaldean: Guru, Maṅgala, Sūrya, Śukra.
   (d) Weekday: Sūrya, Maṅgala, Guru, Śukra.
3. The thresholds. His numbers, or the flow's defaults:
   expand at pressure 8 held 3 readings; contract at pressure 2 held 6 readings; at least 6 readings open before a contraction;
   quota due left = 1 − day/7; seat ceiling 7 for the lattice and 12 for each cluster; one change per reading.
4. Who judges a cluster ended.

   (b) Jev decides: the Noul above a line he sets ends the cluster.
   (c) A seat alone: the flow that opened it.

   (a) They stand beside the lattice: aspect and layer say who answers and at what power; the Metaflow says which line of work. The flow's recommendation.
