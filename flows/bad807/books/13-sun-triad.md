Presentation.{ «The sun in the triad» }

> How can we keep the sun out of the basic triad? That seems like sacrilege. There's nothing without the sun. We call this the solar system. The Surya Siddhanta is almost entirely about Surya and the Rigveda names Agni almost more than any other divinity.

-- psyche, 2026-10-04, on «Metaflows that expand and contract: three to seven, by demand and availability».

How to read the marks.
**S**: the tradition itself states it. *R*: a reading only. **Contested**: the sources disagree.
**The flow's proposal**: the lines of work, the lattice, the order.

## 1. The Vedic triad of the three worlds

### Yāska's three
```
Nirukta 7.5   tisra eva devatāḥ (there are only three gods), say the nairukta (etymologists):    S
              agniḥ pṛthivīsthānaḥ          Agni (fire), whose place is the earth
              vāyur vā indro vā antarikṣasthānaḥ   Vāyu (wind) or Indra, whose place is the midspace
              sūryo dyusthānaḥ              Sūrya (the sun), whose place is heaven
```
Yāska reads every other god as a form of these three (**S**). The sun holds the top of the oldest triad there is.

### Agni and Sūrya in the Rigveda
```
Hymns (Griffith's tally)   Indra 250 · Agni 200 · Soma 123 · Vāyu 12 · Savitṛ 11 · Sūrya 8
First word                 agnim: hymn 1.1 is to Agni                                        S
Family books 2 to 7        each opens with its Agni hymns, then Indra's                        S
```
Agni stands second only to Indra by count. Sūrya has few hymns of his own, but Savitṛ (the impeller) and Mitra are solar too.

### The Sūrya Siddhānta
Its frame: an emissary of Sūrya teaches it to the asura Maya (1.2, **S**); its matter is the motions of all seven graha (planets), eclipses and time.

**Contested.** "Almost entirely about Surya": its authority is Sūrya's; its reckoning covers all seven.

## 2. The amended base triad

The flow's proposal. The three lines of work are the three worlds of Nirukta 7.5.

```
World                       Line           Holds
dyu (heaven)                Sūrya (sun)    the self at the centre: psyche, vision, judgment, the seat that sees everything
antarikṣa (midspace)        Vāyu (wind)    breath and movement: messaging, flows, upkeep
pṛthivī (earth)             Agni (fire)    transformation and making: building, implementation
```

### Why each maps so
Sūrya: "the soul of all" (BPHS, Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra, 3.12, **S**); the seat above that sees every line.
Vāyu: prāṇa (breath) is wind inside the body; messages and flows are what moves between seats (*R*).
Agni: fire turns the offering into what the gods receive; making turns a design into what runs (*R*).

### Their graha
The seven are graha (planets). Two base lines have one by plain tradition; the third has two candidates.
```
Sūrya    Sūrya (Sun)            itself                                                    S
Agni     Maṅgala (Mars)         Mars rules agni, the element fire (BPHS 3.20)             S
Vāyu     Śani (Saturn)          Saturn rules vāyu, the element air (BPHS 3.20)            S
 or      Guru (Jupiter)         if Indra is chosen: Indra is Jupiter's deity (BPHS 3.18)  S
```
**Contested.** BPHS 3.18 also makes Agni the Sun's own deity: the earth's fire and heaven's fire as one.

### The strands
```
Gītā 14.18     ūrdhvaṃ (up) sattva (clarity) · madhye (middle) rajas (activity) · adhaḥ (down) tamas (inertia)
Read on        Sūrya sattva · Vāyu rajas · Agni tamas                          R, a reading only
BPHS 3.22      Sūrya sattva · Śani tamas · Maṅgala tamas · Guru sattva          S, per graha
```
**Contested.** The Gītā's middle is the human world, not the midspace; and by BPHS the three graha do not make a set of three strands.
So the strands stand beside the lines as each graha's own (BPHS), not as the triad's frame.

## 3. The lattice re-derived: three to seven

The flow's proposal. Base: Sūrya, Vāyu as Śani, Agni as Maṅgala. Four open, each out of a base line, by one split rule from book 12.

### The opening table
```
Step  Opens              Out of   Split rule (source)                                            Takes
4     Candra (Moon)      Sūrya    function: citta (memory), the inner organ named by what it does (Vivekacūḍāmaṇi 93–94)   memory: records, Memory, stores
5     Budha (Mercury)    Vāyu     delegation: prāṇa (breath) appoints officers (Praśna Upaniṣad 3.4)                       exchange: messages, protocols
6     Guru (Jupiter)     Sūrya    delegation: the seat that sees appoints teachers (Praśna Upaniṣad 3.4)                   teaching: skills, curriculum
7     Śukra (Venus)      Agni     recipient: sampradāna (the kāraka, role, of the one given to; Pāṇini)                    delight: books, what the living touches
Base  Śani (Saturn)      Vāyu     the midspace line's own graha                                                            limits: reaping, quota, hygiene
```
Meanings of the graha: Moon mind, Mercury speech, Jupiter knowledge, Saturn grief (BPHS 3.12–13, **S**); which work each takes is *R*.

### The order
```
Weekday     Sūrya · Candra · Maṅgala · Budha · Guru · Śukra · Śani     the week begins on the sun's day
Openings    4 Candra · 5 Budha · 6 Guru · 7 Śukra                       the weekday order, base lines skipped
Chaldean    Śani · Guru · Maṅgala · Sūrya · Śukra · Budha · Candra     slowest first
 its        4 Guru · 5 Śukra · 6 Budha · 7 Candra                       the alternative
```
The weekday order comes from the Chaldean by the planetary hours (Cassius Dio 37.18–19, **S**). The flow proposes the weekday: it starts at the sun.

If Indra is chosen, the midspace line takes Guru; Guru no longer opens. Śani opens out of Agni by function (fire consumes: clearing), at step 6.
