```
Line              In charge of today                           The seat or program that is it now
Budha · Mercury   hears first, routes                          the quaternary, the secretary, messenger-clj
Candra · Moon     keeps and interprets the record              psyche logging, distillation, the raw records by flow
Śani · Saturn     limits and measurement                       quota, context occupancy, reaping on Replace, the Field publisher
```
*Reading*: the columns "in charge of" and "the seat" are the flow's. Your words give hearing to Mercury, interpreting to the Moon, limits to Saturn.

### What each holds, as it runs today
```
Budha     the quaternary is your first contact when unsure whom to message
          the secretary places, drafts and moves on the main flow's rulings
          messenger-clj carries every message; it sends only what acts, delivers or blocks

```

## 3. The Sun

**His**: the Sun is the primary layer, woken, not always on.
```
State       What happens
asleep      nothing is cooking; no seat runs
woken       the secretary brings a question that needs judgment
judging     it rules; each ruling becomes a context module
unwinding   things calm down; the decided is already in modules
handover    its successor receives only the undecided: in debate, fuzzy, unruled
```
