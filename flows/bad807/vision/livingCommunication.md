# Communication from the living

## The main failure of machines: not differentiating content types, from poorly or non-annotated text

Context: typed in chat to this flow, following his words on distillation turning specifics into generals.

> Something that sort of haunts me and I might probably talk about on a daily basis: probably the main failure of machines is their inability to seem to differentiate between content types, probably because they're using poorly or non-annotated text. It doesn't convey the emotion, the sarcasm, the heartfeltness, the coldness, the warmth, the emotion behind the words, the emphasis, the silences, the volume changes, or the tempo. It's just acting on a fraction and trying to decipher too much.
> ... There are so many different types of communications from the living and we need the machine to start to be able to better differentiate between them.

-- psyche, typed, 2026-10-04.
