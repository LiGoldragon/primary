vision:composition, the rules of composing a flow · psyche-context › vision/composition.md, a new file

A flow is composed from the slices that match its address: the universal modules, its layer's module, its aspect's module, a module for its aspect and layer when one is witnessed, and the modules of its topic. A statement names the one scope it varies on and lives in that scope's module alone; it is never repeated in another.

A statement is universal only when witnessed as needed by every flow; every universal line is paid for by every flow, and a flow follows fewer instructions the more it is given, so a lower layer composes fewer. A module for one aspect at one layer exists only for a witnessed interaction between a layer line and an aspect line.

Two modules that contradict each other for one address fail at composition, and the author resolves the contradiction; no scope overrides another. Composed text goes wide scope first, narrowest last, and each address's composed text can be read and diffed whenever a wide module changes.

Each aspect's module declares which modules each of its addresses loads and why; Flow's configuration is derived from the three declarations, and a proposal of a new module carries its entry in the declaration of the aspect it trains.

A subflow is composed the same way, from its role's declaration and that role's dependencies; a brief carries the task alone, and nothing a role already holds is repeated in it.

the three nexuses · psyche-context › vision/anatomy.md

Psyche, Mind and Field are three nexuses. Psyche holds what is wanted: the psyche data, synthesized and kept, guiding every flow's action and every implementation's design, and auditing whether an implementation follows its design. Mind holds what is known and what follows: facts, documentation, issues, branches, merges, and the links from each psyche statement to the implementation that rests on it. Field holds what is running: the state of the machine, and the actions its events call for. A change in what is wanted lands in Psyche, in what is known in Mind, in what is running in Field, and in no two of them.

In the long term, Mind keeps a link from each psyche statement to every artifact built on it, taken from what a flow was witnessed reading, never from what it reports. When a statement is edited, every artifact linked to it is marked suspect; the mark clears when a flow redoes the artifact or confirms it still holds, and spreads no further than where the result differs.

In the long term, Field holds the declared state of the machine and keeps closing the gap between it and what it observes; an event lost or a restart costs nothing. A local model reachable on the local network takes over reconnecting the internet when it is down, as one case of the loop.
