Presentation.{ «Vertical ethos, the skill line» }

`Curriculum/skills/vision-ethos.md`, the layout line and the comment line.

Now:
> Ethos expands vertically: a structure with more than one element opens on its line and its elements hang beneath the first, aligned; the closing delimiter ends the last element's line. Nothing that has a next layer sits on one line.
>
> Ethos carries a comment on every section and on every line that has a next layer, saying in plain words what the machine reads there; a comment runs from ; to the end of the line.

Proposed:
> Ethos expands vertically: a structure with a next layer opens on its line, and each of its elements starts on its own line beneath it, indented two; the closing delimiter ends the last element's line. Nothing that has a next layer sits on one line, and no element follows the opener on the opener's line.
>
> Ethos carries a comment on every section and on every line that has a next layer, saying in plain words what the machine reads there. A comment sits beside its element, at one column shared by the block, or on the line above it at the element's indentation; never below. A comment runs from ; to the end of the line.
