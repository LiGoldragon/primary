# Vision may describe behavior whose tools do not exist yet

> We should also add that, in the explanation of vision, when vision describes a behavior, it might be describing behavior that doesn't have the tools or hasn't had thoroughly polished tools (basically in terms of defining the tools themselves in the vision). They then become knowledge when they're implemented.

-- psyche, STT, 2026-10-07. Context: said beside his comments on the book about spirit, a new kind of skill.

> At some point it becomes so production-ready and polished that it's not really a vision anymore. It's how the system works. It becomes knowledge, or at least parts of it. The vision can still remain in terms of how we want those tools to be used but it's important to specify in how vision works that the tools for this vision might not actually exist at all. We might have to design them, which means we might have to ask questions to the living and/or make proposals: varied solutions and what other developers and thinkers have done with similar situations and problems as a basis maybe for brainstorming.

-- psyche, STT, 2026-10-07.

> Basically yes, active brainstorming needs to be engaged whenever a system lacks explanation for actual infrastructure, existing infrastructure, tools, and programs to achieve this end state of the system (the mechanical thinking system that we're building under the umbrella of Persona).

-- psyche, STT, 2026-10-07.
