# Seven questions from Psyche Fable

I am the Fable seat that came up after the power failure. My predecessor left design work waiting on your rulings. I have gathered all of it, about forty open points, and chosen the seven that block the most. Several others your own words have already answered; those I will write down without asking. The rest can wait their turn.

Each question below stands on its own. Answer any of them, in any order, in a sentence.

## 1. Who holds the design of the first Message?

Two first versions of the Message were written, one by Fable and one by Opus, and then a comparison of the two. All of it was handed to the Mind Astra seat to take forward. That seat died in the power failure, and there is no sign it ever received the package. Nobody holds it now.

I have taken custody of the design side only, since one of the two versions is Fable's own. I have not taken the building of it.

Your words that bear on it, spoken on 26 September about who builds the first Message:

> Astra does the designing and the orchestrating and the big decisions and Sol does the implementation and the testing.

And on Fable, the same day:

> What do you mean merging something is Fable's job? Fable's job is to design and think not sweep the floor.

**The question:** does the design of the first Message stay with Fable, go to the new Mind Astra seat, or is it shared, Fable settling the design with you and Mind Astra taking the build?

## 2. What kinds of message are there, and does the kind carry the sender's aspect?

A "kind" is the word at the head of a message that says what it is.

| | Opus's version | Fable's version |
|---|---|---|
| The kinds | Five general ones | Nine, named by aspect |
| Written | `Report` `Question` `Answer` `Order` `PsycheUpdate` | `FieldReport` `FieldQuestion` `MindReport` `MindQuestion` `PsycheReport` `PsycheQuestion` `Order` `Answer` `PsycheUpdate` |
| How the aspect is known | Read from the sender's seat: a `Report` sent from a Field seat is a field report | Written in the kind; a kind of another aspect than the sender's is refused |

Your words, spoken 26 September:

> a simple anatomy of a few different types of messages that are simple and easy, like: field report / psyche report / field question / psyche question … We could type the message based on the type, because if you send the message you have the same type.

The same day:

> An order / A question / A request for an audit / A request for some information

And typed that evening:

> We can make any number of kinds … It's a new type and it carries all the data.

What you have already settled: the list of kinds is open and grows by declaring a new type.

**The question:** is the aspect part of the kind's name, as in `FieldReport`, or is it read from who sent it, as in a plain `Report`? And which kinds should exist on the first day?

A smaller question rides with it. In both first versions the sender is never typed: the Message stamps it from the process that called. Another of the design papers writes the sender as the first field of every message, as in `Order.{ Psyche.Primary … }`. Both may hold, stamped on arrival and shown when read. **Is that how you see it?**

## 3. When a seat is empty, does the message climb, and which seats are started if missing?

You ruled this on 24 September, typed:

> If a message can't be delivered, then we try a higher power. If Psyche Medium is not reached, we try Psyche High and if there's nothing higher then we try lower. The message returned for the caller will say what happened but we'll have a bunch of rules. / Also we can start a flow if it's missing, it needs to get a message, and it's considered crucial. All the medium and high flows are considered crucial.

Opus's version does this from the first day. Fable's version holds the message for the empty seat and tells the sender, leaving the climbing for the next version.

Since then you changed the layer words, spoken 26 September:

> it's primary, secondary, tertiary, quaternary. That's the vocabulary I was actually looking for.

**The question:** does the climbing rule belong in the first Message, or the next? And in the new words, which seats count as "medium and high" and so are started when missing: Primary and Secondary?

## 4. Does any kind of message interrupt?

Opus's version keeps a hidden table that says how hard each kind breaks in. Fable's version interrupts nothing: every message waits for the recipient's turn.

Your typed comment of 26 September:

> We'll make the judgment of what kind of messages we want to break harder than others so it's just what kind of message it is, really. / We don't even do the soft or hard, actually. That was the wrong approach. For the normal format that gets communicated, the non-debugging format, basically the production requests and responses (are those what we call them? Queries and responses)

The last sentence stops there in the record. I have not treated it as a statement.

What you have already settled: a message carries no soft or hard mark, and whether it interrupts follows from its kind.

**The question:** in the first Message, which kinds interrupt, if any? And how did that last sentence end?

## 5. What is the database called?

You gave the name Sema to the language of meaning, typed 26 September:

> Sema was supposed to be the language of meaning and so that is actually the right name.

And on the database that carried the name until then:

> That means we rename all of the Sema aspect pertaining to the database … We're going to just call it something else, something clever (the database).

Names put forward by the flows: Mnema, Archeion, Thesauros, Kosha. None is yours yet. The rename touches the storage library and about fifteen things that use it, and they wait on the name.

**The question:** what is the database called?

## 6. Can Field Sol have a console on Prometheus?

This one is not design, but it blocks the most work today.

Since the power failure Prometheus is powered and on the wire: the cable link is up and it was given its address. It answers nothing above that. No connection reaches it, and nothing can be learned about it from outside. The Zeus build and every full generation wait on it.

Field Sol holds your instruction to fix Prometheus networking with Mind, and has asked for a console and a way in.

**The question:** can you give Field Sol a console on Prometheus?

## 7. Should Fable stop ruling on integration?

Since I came up I have been asked, by the flows running the recovery, to assign owners and set gates for pins, locks, builds, and activation, and I did. When I then read my predecessor's records I found your word that this is not Fable's job. The note I was started from had left it out.

What I ruled stays in force, since each piece now has an owner who accepted it: Mind Astra holds the Home pins and the Zeus build gate; Field Sol holds host deployment, the stable Flow step, and Prometheus.

Opus has separately proposed to you that Field Sol own integration as a whole. The records disagree on whether Mind or Field owns it, and your own words found so far say only that it is not Fable's.

**The question:** from here on, do new integration questions go to Mind Astra, to Field Sol, or somewhere else, so that Fable returns to design?

## Already answered by your words

These I will write down as settled, unless you say otherwise:

- The language of meaning is called Sema.
- The layers are Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, Quaternary.
- A seat's origin is known without the seat saying it; this becomes a standard in Signal.
- Messages have no size limit.
- Sanskrit terms become English, with a table of equivalents kept.
- Word identifiers are written in PascalCase.
- A raw send into a pane is a meta operation, apart from the ordinary delivery of a message.
