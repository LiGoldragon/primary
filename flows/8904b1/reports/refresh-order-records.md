## 2. The written rule in the skills (evidence of the written rule only, not loaded as authority)

From `.claude/skills/refresh/SKILL.md` (generated), the section is the whole file, headed only by its frontmatter description "A flow is refreshed, continued, or prepared for successor handoff." Quoted passages on order, receipt, and predecessor status:

> A refresh continues a Flow without assuming that the predecessor is dead. First witness present reality, what changed since the flow last progressed, whether the living's last words remain current, and every open question. A refresh is justified at sixty percent context or after a dramatic change of direction; do not restart below twenty percent for a change that is not dramatic.

> At sixty percent context, or when coordination can no longer be represented faithfully in a compact state of authority, owners, evidence, open work, and blockers, start a successor refresh programmatically before further implementation. The compact handoff identifies the current objective, settled authority, decisions, active dispatches and their state, evidence locations, blockers, and next actions; it does not replay the predecessor's full context.

> Each native main successor claims a distinct native `FLOW_ID` only after that receipt. Each successor remembers its immediate predecessor at depth one.

> The predecessor is crossover-only while readiness is incomplete. It remains available for evidence, routing continuity, and handoff; never kill, retire, conclude, silence, or automatically remove its routing as a side effect of refresh. Explicit authority is required for any retirement or routing withdrawal.

> After both gates pass, transfer ongoing Field ownership to the new Field Sol and retain the predecessor as crossover-only until separately directed otherwise.

From `.claude/skills/main-flow/SKILL.md` (generated), the one line pointing to refresh:

> Load `$refresh` for the canonical refresh protocol. Do not conclude, silence, retire, or withdraw routing from a predecessor merely because a successor pane or process exists. A Field refresh has two coordinated seats and uses the additional readiness gates defined there.

Reading these together: the written rule's order is successor-first — the successor is launched, verified (native-start and identity receipts, applicable currentness, live health/routing witnessed), and only after readiness gates pass does ownership transfer; the predecessor is never ended as an automatic side effect and stays "crossover-only" (available for evidence/routing/handoff) until "separately directed" or "explicit authority" ends it. "Remember" (successor remembers predecessor "at depth one") means the successor's identity records its immediate predecessor's FLOW_ID as its one-step ancestry, not a full context replay — the handoff is a compact state summary, "it does not replay the predecessor's full context."

## 4. What the records settle, what they do not, and tensions

**Settled by the written skill rule** (as a written rule, not itself authority over the living): refresh order is successor-first — predecessor is never ended as a side effect of launching a successor; both may be live for a span (predecessor "crossover-only" until readiness gates pass and, even then, only "separately directed" or "explicit authority" ends it); the successor's "remembering" the predecessor means one-deep ancestry plus a compact handoff state, not a full context replay; a refresh is triggered at "sixty percent context" (or a dramatic direction change), not a fixed token count like 200k.

**Not settled by any record found:**
- Who, specifically, holds "explicit authority" to retire/end a seat is not named as one role in the written rule. The living's own words name Field Luna as delegated executor ("she has all the authority to stop and start flows... once she's told") but immediately qualify it as executing on instruction only, not deciding — and a later same-day utterance (the "nonsense... nothing is up to me... everything is being automated... no one is going to close or start anything" passage) unsettles even that, saying no one — including the living — closes or starts seats going forward, that this is to be automated, with Unity as the eventual interface. These two utterances (Luna as delegated stopper/starter vs. "no one" closes/starts, all automated) are not reconciled in the records found; both are same-day, same relay chain (93ba9f), STT, 2026-09-26.
- Whether "who performs" reaping/closing is settled: `operational-reapAndArchiveOldSessions.md`'s own context note says explicitly "who performs it is not yet reconciled."
- A numeric context-size trigger ("200k" or similar) attributed to the living's own words was not found; the sixty-percent figure exists only in the written skill.
- Whether e167d8's launch freeze was ever formally "lifted" is not recorded; the closest record is the living's denial that a general freeze was ever ordered, which is a correction of the premise rather than a recorded lifting act by e167d8.
- Whether e167d8 is currently live is not stated either way in the records; the last log line leaves it "idle... ready to be archived and closed," an unresolved, pending state, with the actual archive/close action assigned to e71dab but not recorded as done in the files read.

**Tension surfaced, not resolved here:** the skill's "explicit authority is required for any retirement" sits alongside the living's two same-day statements that are themselves in tension with each other (Luna delegated to stop/start on instruction vs. "no one" closes or starts, everything automated). Also, `operational-reapReplacedSessions.md` ("when we refresh a flow, it takes out that end from receiving messages") could be read as the predecessor losing routing at refresh time, which sits in tension with the skill's "never... automatically remove its routing as a side effect of refresh" and with the "crossover-only... until separately directed" language — the living's 2026-09-17 phrasing does not by itself distinguish "reaped from receiving new messages once superseded" from "kept live as crossover for evidence/handoff," and no later record reconciles the two.
