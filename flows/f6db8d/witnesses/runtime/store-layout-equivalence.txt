## Byte-shape comparison result
The rkyv-derived active record structs (StoredConfiguration, StoredLock, StoredAllocator)
are textually IDENTICAL between deployed 0.30.0 (5f016531) and released 0.31.0 (1bc55af1).
The tuple 'Previous*' types are likewise IDENTICAL between the two, and in BOTH revisions
they read the pre-0.30 tables 'orchestrate_configuration'/'locks'/'lock_id_allocator',
not the tables 0.30.0 actually writes.
