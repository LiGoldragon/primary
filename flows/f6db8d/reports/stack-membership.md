## Is schema-rust the old generator Ethos Zero replaces?

Not a direct swap — they were built as **parallel** shortcut
generators for two different syntax families (schema-rust for
Schema/NOTA, ethos-rust/ethos-zero for Ethos/Datom). But the standing,
distilled Vision states the lineage handoff plainly:

> Schema is the abandoned ancestor of Ethos, not of Datom.

— `Vision/datom.md`, "Repository" section (distilled; landed on main
2026-08-24, `c2a0223b6`, well before tonight).

> Generating Rust is Ethos's duty, in today's division of labor.

— `Vision/datom.md`, "Nature" section (distilled).

So: **yes in lineage** — Schema, and by extension schema-rust's
generation duty, is what Ethos (and its generator, Ethos Zero) is
superseding, while Dotos/NOTA is what Datom is superseding. It is not
a drop-in technical replacement; nota-pins.md's own finding that the
Nexus daemon emitter is already gone from `schema-rust`'s main
(removed 2026-08-06) is consistent with schema-rust being retired
rather than developed forward.

## nota is dotos — same lineage, same freeze

nota-pins.md's own technical finding is not in question here and I
take it as read: `nota` → `dotos` is a pure rename at one GitHub
repository (`nota.git` = `nota-next.git` = `dotos.git`, identical
HEAD), and `dotos` is "the hand-authored structural reader and codec
... that owned the text value shapes ... for every Signal contract's
text projection." That is exactly the role `Vision/datom.md`'s
"Nature" section assigns to Datom: "Datom is signal's form at the
edge." Dotos and Datom are competing occupants of the same functional
slot, and the living named Dotos the one that "stays behind, frozen."
Migrating a consumer's Signal-contract text projection onto `dotos`
is, on this reading, not a step toward the ruled destination — it is
further investment in the notation the living named frozen.

None of the nine repositories nota-pins.md touched are named,
individually, in Vision/Intent/flows-vision as belonging to one stack
or the other. Membership below is this flow's inference from the
patterns above, not a quotable ruling per-repository.

- **Frozen "incorrect stack" territory** (schema-rust-dependent,
  daemon-era Nexus/contract-crate generation): **harness**,
  **listener**, **repository-ledger** — all three are blocked on
  schema-rust's deleted Nexus/contract-crate emitters, which is
  exactly the kind of code the living said to leave in place. Leaving
  them untouched (listener, repository-ledger's own build.rs) or only
  bumping their version for an unrelated wire change (harness) is
  consistent with the freeze.
- **Live stack meant to move to Datom, but moved sideways instead**:
  **aggregator**, **signal-aggregator**, **meta-signal-aggregator**,
  **dotos-text-query**, **message** (as a producer) — none of these
  have a schema-rust build.rs; all were reachable, and all were pinned
  onto `dotos` rather than moved toward `datom`.
- **Ambiguous**: **router** — its migration to `dotos` was completed
  but cannot resolve (walls in signal-standard/signal-mind, themselves
  downstream of the frozen schema-rust territory), so it sits at the
  seam between the two stacks; **repository-ledger's own two contract
  crates** (signal-repository-ledger, meta-signal-repository-ledger)
  similarly straddle — they were migrated to `dotos` (live-stack
  direction) while their consumer repository-ledger itself is frozen
  by schema-rust (incorrect-stack territory).
