M = Message design (2e64c5 message-design.md); F = Flow design (30e8efc flow-buildable-design.md). Read by this subflow; nothing run.

1. Client's Process: authored (M), unresolved in part. Message reads the peer pid with `SO_PEERCRED` on the accepted connection and pairs it with the start time from `/proc/<pid>/stat` field 22 as `Process.{ Pid Started }` (M 370-373, 890-892). M says itself that no atomic pid-reuse algorithm is specified or proven (M 373-375, 893-895). F only declares the `Process` type (F 92-96) and does not say how Message gets it.
2. Identify / Identified.Address: authored. Message sends `Identify.Process` (M 376, 289). Flow walks ancestors to a Flow record's Process, compares pid and start time, and answers `Identified.Address` or `Unidentified.Process` (F 632-636; M 377-380). The gate does not limit Identify to Message. It covers only Lock, Deliver and Release (F 611-616; M 568-571), and "Every other Flow query stays open to any local peer the socket admits" (M 176-177).
3. Address to Sender: authored only as a statement. "That Address becomes the `Sender` of the lock" (M 383-384; M 179-181). "The Sender of a Lock is that address" (F 637-638, current best). Neither design gives a mechanism that ties a Lock's Sender to an earlier Identified answer. Message carries the value itself (M 402-404, 457-460).
4. Lock-time check on Sender: unresolved. The checks authored at Lock are the gate's pid and start-time match against the bound Message process (F 611-616), Unknown.Address when the Sender names no metaflow (F 598-600, 367-369), OffRoute (F 338-342, 646-647), and Up resolved from the Sender (F 595-598). Neither design has Flow check that the Sender was just Identified, or that it matches a bound or live process. Both are silent on this. The only authored ground is that the gate admitted Message. My inference: Flow trusts Message's Sender. F says "Deliver trusts its lock" (F 607) for Deliver, and neither design says anything comparable for Lock.
