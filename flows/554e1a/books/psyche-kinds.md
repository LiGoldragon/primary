### Proposal 1: How a spirit line is written · psyche-context › intent/psyche.md

  Spirit is philosophy that holds beyond this system. A rule about this system, however broad, is intent at most, on its subject.
+ A spirit line is simple, free of technique, and true as widely as it can be said. A number, a tool or a part of this system in it makes it intent.

### Proposal 2: Vision has two subkinds · psyche-context › intent/psyche.md

**Comment** On 10-13 you ruled two tiers; on 10-29 you spoke of three, actual, next, further. This keeps two; the third is yours to give. Alternative 2 comes from your words of 12:28: actual, next in the pipeline, further down the road.

**Alternative 1**

- ## Vision is short-term or long-term
-
- Vision is what the living says the system should be, in the long term or for now, the short term. A long-term line is marked In the long term; an unmarked line holds now.
-
- The long-term vision is the goalpost; the short-term vision is what the system is to be for now. Either may be implemented or not. A flow holds both apart, each true at its own horizon. When the long-term vision is implemented and documented, the short-term vision and its documentation are removed.
+ ## Vision has two subkinds
+
+ Vision is what the living says the system is to be. Its present subkind holds what the system is to be now, in plain unmarked lines. Its long-term subkind holds the eventual design the present is one step toward, in modules of its own under vision/long-term, loaded by a flow that designs toward them. A long-term module says plainly that it is not achieved, and how its behavior will be configuration the running system takes rather than code. What the system does today that differs from vision lives in knowledge. When a long-term module is implemented and documented it becomes present vision, and the knowledge that differed from it is removed.

**Alternative 2**

+ ## Vision has three subkinds
+
+ Vision is what the living says the system is to be. Its present subkind holds what the system is to be now, in plain unmarked lines. Its next subkind holds the step after the present, in modules under vision/next, loaded by a flow that builds it. Its long-term subkind holds the eventual design, in modules under vision/long-term, loaded by a flow that designs toward it. A next or long-term module says plainly that it is not achieved. When a module of a later subkind is implemented and documented it becomes present vision, and the knowledge that differed from it is removed.

### Proposal 3: Written psyche is read, never touched · psyche-context › intent/psyche.md

  Psyche data is the most valuable thing there is: what the living has explicitly reviewed and approved outweighs any machine inference.
+ A flow never reaches the living; what it reads is written psyche, his words after layers of loss. A record is read between the lines, to infer the living as one reads another mind; it is never ground truth. A later explicit correction on a subject weighs most and erases nothing: both records keep their dates. When records point to incompatible actions, the tension is surfaced to the living. His raw words are preserved, and a paraphrase stands only once he has reviewed it. "Psyche" alone means the written psyche; the person is always the living.

### Proposal 5: knowledge:records, where psyche lives · mind-context › knowledge/records.md (renamed from knowledge/psyche.md)

+ Psyche has four levels, in descending authority: spirit, intent, vision, notion. Vision and notion can be raw; intent and spirit can only be crystallized. A raw record lives in the flow that heard it: flows/<short-id>/vision/<topic>.md and flows/<short-id>/notion/<topic>.md; finding raw psyche means searching flows/*/vision/. vision-raw/<topic>.md is legacy, draining as crystallization touches it. A notion may be drawn upon for suggestions; a flow told to implement without asking relies on a notion only when its need matches it exactly.
