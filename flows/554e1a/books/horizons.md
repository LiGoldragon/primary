# Two horizons

Designed by Fable 5.1; the records gathered by a Sonnet 5.5 subflow of the Psyche core Secondary.

One idea: how a long-term line is held so that it is built modular and configurable, in its time, and never crammed into the next iteration. Flow, whole is revisited after this is ruled.

### Proposal 1: Vision has two horizons · psyche-context › intent/psyche.md

**Comment** Your words of 10-13 kept: goalpost, never a blocker, either horizon implemented or not, the present removed when the long term lands. Your words of today added: a long-term mark means not this iteration, and a long-term line is built only once its abstractions, their composition and its configuration are specified. Medium term, which you floated on 10-23, is left out: you said today that complexity has to be managed.

- ## Vision is short-term or long-term
-
- Vision is what the living says the system should be, in the long term or for now, the short term. A long-term line is marked In the long term; an unmarked line holds now.
-
- The long-term vision is the goalpost; the short-term vision is what the system is to be for now. Either may be implemented or not. A flow holds both apart, each true at its own horizon. When the long-term vision is implemented and documented, the short-term vision and its documentation are removed.
+ ## Vision has two horizons
+
+ Vision is what the living says the system is to be. A present line, unmarked, is what the system is to be now. A long-term line, marked In the long term, is the goalpost the present is a step toward. Either may be implemented or not; a flow holds both apart, each true at its own horizon. When a long-term line is implemented and documented it becomes present, and the present lines it replaces are removed.
+
+ To an implementer a long-term mark says one thing: not this iteration. A long-term line is built only after a proposal moves it to the present with its design: the abstractions it is made of, how they compose, and the configuration the running system takes it through, shown as vision code. Until then the implementer builds the present line beside it, shaped so the long-term line can take its place.

### Proposal 2: A step is one thing · psyche-context › intent/design.md

**Comment** Your picture of today: the nail driver asked to design the house, do the accounting and drive the nails builds a mud hut. The machine does in one step what is several.

  A short-term proposal is one step from what exists: it names what it builds on, and proposes only what can be built next.
+ A step is one thing: the design, or an abstraction the design needs, or the behavior on that abstraction, each judged on its own before the next is begun. A present line that needs an abstraction the system lacks is two steps, the abstraction first.
