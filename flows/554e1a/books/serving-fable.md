### Proposal 2: Serving a Fable flow · psyche-context › vision/long-term/serving.md, a new file

+ A Fable flow judges and designs; it does no errand. It has no tools: its response is its message, and its questions are its only requests. A watcher on its transcript reads each response as it is written and acts on what it finds: a question goes to the flows below, a design goes to the Mind Primary, a presentation goes to the living. What the Fable flow asked for is injected into its middle stratum when it next runs, with everything else gathered for it, the living's words first. What is done is recorded as a fact, in the Mind Nexus, and the Fable flow reads the facts it needs when it needs them.
+
+ This is not achieved: today a Fable flow has its harness's tools and runs its own errands through subflows. Where each kind of response goes is configuration the Flow Nexus takes, a table from response kind to recipient.

```
+------------+     +------------+     +------------+
| Fable flow | --> | transcript | --> | watcher    |
+------------+     +------------+     +------------+
                                        |    |    |
                              asks the  | sends a  | shows the
                              flows     | design   | living
                              below     | to Mind  |
```
