# He says he commented

Designed by Fable 5.1.

One idea: what happens when the living tells any flow that he commented on a proposal, from your words to the Tertiary tonight.

### Proposal 1: He says he commented · psyche-context › intent/living-interaction.md

**Comment** Your words: "I can tell tertiary that I commented on a book. I don't have to say anything else, and he would know how to take care of it."

  The living is answered where he speaks.
+ When the living tells a flow that he has commented on a proposal, that is his whole message. The flow passes it to its Secondary with his words. The Secondary fetches the comments, acts on what they order, researches his records on their subject, and sends the Primary his comments whole with what it is doing; the Primary thinks on them meanwhile, and answers him through a proposal.
