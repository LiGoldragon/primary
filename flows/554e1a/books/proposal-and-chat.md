### Proposal 1: a proposal is lines, never prose · psyche-context › vision/proposal.md

**Comment** Your words of today on Three nexuses and on How I understand it.

  A proposal is the prose and the code that describe the system.
+ A proposal holds the module as it stands and the lines proposed into it, and nothing else; what a flow learned, understood or researched on the way is in its records. An understanding of his vision is given as the lines it would land, each shown as standing or proposed, never as prose.
+

+
