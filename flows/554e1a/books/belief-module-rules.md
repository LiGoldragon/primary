# How belief modules are kept

Designed by Fable 5.1, inventory by Opus 5.5, extraction by Sonnet 5.5; split by 554e1a.

This page comes from Everything open and holds one idea: how belief modules are kept: named, edited, erased and entered.

### Proposal 1: the repositories are named belief · psyche-belief, mind-belief, field-belief

+ The belief modules live in three repositories named for their owner: psyche-belief, whose modules change on the living's word; mind-belief, whose knowledge Mind changes as the code changes; field-belief, whose compensation Field changes without review.

### Proposal 2: a role is a declaration, not belief · psyche-context › vision/belief-modules.md

**Comment** Your word of today: field belief is not where roles go. A role is what Curriculum composes a subflow from: a declaration of its dependencies and its brief shape, data the composing system takes. What a role text says about conduct is intent; what it says about a tool is knowledge. The 22 role files leave field-belief by this line.

  A module declares its placement: the system prompt, or loaded on demand. A skill is the harness's interface for an on-demand module.
+ A role is not belief: it is a subflow's declaration, its dependencies and the shape of its brief, held as data by the system that composes subflows. A line of conduct found in a role is intent, and a line about how a tool works is knowledge; each goes to its kind, and the role keeps only its declaration.

### Proposal 3: belief editing removes first · psyche-context › vision/belief-modules.md

- Removal comes before addition.
+ A belief is edited to raise its signal against its size: first what is wrong or redundant is removed; if what remains does not say it, a line is rewritten; only as a last resort is a line added.
~ The line that changes a behavior lands in the module that produced the behavior, so every later flow carries it. ⇠ intent:main-flow
+ A proposal of a new module carries the entry in its aspect's declaration that says which flows load it and why.

### Proposal 4: machine belief is erased · psyche-context › vision/belief-modules.md

**Comment** Your word of today: machine-generated belief is mostly harmful and is erased. This replaces the provenance pass that only marked.

+ A statement in a spirit, intent or vision module stands on a record of the living's words; a statement no record holds is a machine line, and a machine line is erased. The erasure goes module by module, loaded modules first; each module's erased lines are given to the living as a short list, so he can call one back.

### Proposal 5: not every vision module fits a main flow · psyche-context › vision/belief-modules.md

+ A main flow's declaration carries every spirit and intent module and the vision modules of its own topic; not every vision module fits one main flow, and the rest load on demand.

### Proposal 6: in the long term, every module enters at the middle stratum · psyche-context › vision/belief-modules.md

**Comment** Your notion of 10-29, offered as a long-term line for your ruling.

+ In the long term, every belief module enters a flow at the middle stratum, placed by the Flow Nexus at launch and at every refresh, and no flow loads a module for itself.
