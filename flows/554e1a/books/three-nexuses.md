# Three nexuses

Designed by Fable 5.1, inventory by Opus 5.5, extraction by Sonnet 5.5; split by 554e1a.

This page comes from Everything open and holds one idea: Psyche, Mind and Field as three nexuses.

### Proposal 1: the three nexuses · psyche-context › vision/anatomy.md

+ Psyche, Mind and Field are three nexuses. Psyche holds what is wanted: the psyche data, synthesized and kept, guiding every flow's action and every implementation's design, and auditing whether an implementation follows its design. Mind holds what is known and what follows: facts, documentation, issues, branches, merges, and the links from each psyche statement to the implementation that rests on it. Field holds what is running: the state of the machine, and the actions its events call for. A change in what is wanted lands in Psyche, in what is known in Mind, in what is running in Field, and in no two of them.
+
+ In the long term, Mind keeps a link from each psyche statement to every artifact built on it, taken from what a flow was witnessed reading, never from what it reports. When a statement is edited, every artifact linked to it is marked suspect; the mark clears when a flow redoes the artifact or confirms it still holds, and spreads no further than where the result differs.
+
+ In the long term, Field holds the declared state of the machine and keeps closing the gap between it and what it observes; an event lost or a restart costs nothing. A local model reachable on the local network takes over reconnecting the internet when it is down, as one case of the loop.

### Proposal 2: the word alone names the nexus · mind-context › knowledge/vocabulary.md

+ Psyche, Mind and Field, each word alone, name the nexus. The flows that attend a nexus are its aspect, and are called its flows: a Psyche flow, the Mind core Primary. The modules that train them are belief modules, named by their kind and topic, never by the nexus.
