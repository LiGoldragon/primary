the records of a landing are archived with it · psyche-context › vision/crystallization.md

Your question of 13:05, whether raw records are still archived when you approve an edit, so they do not pile into contradiction and noise.

A crystallized statement lands as lines in a vision- or intent- skill's authored source. A ruling a crystallization lands in a skill is recorded by the landing itself. Intent enters an intent- skill only on the living's explicit word.

The landing of a line archives the raw records it drew on, in the same commit; a record stays raw only while no line of it has landed.

vision is logged from his words alone · psyche-context › intent/living-psyche.md

Your word of 12:30, beside the lines of proposal 1 of Modules by role and function.

A question the vision already answers is answered from it, and is not a record. What he says that the vision already holds is not logged again. An order is read from the time it was given.

A vision record is made from his words alone; a line met in a record or a module is already logged, and is never logged again as vision.

4

a later record that says it all supersedes the earlier · psyche-context › vision/crystallization.md

Your protocol of tonight: when you explain yourself better later and say everything the earlier record said, the earlier one is redundant; a repetition may stay for the emphasis it carries.

Impurities are hunted by the records cleaner and fall through crystallization alike; every destruction is one commit line naming the record.

A raw record whose every point a later record of his states better is superseded: it moves to the archive with one line naming the record that supersedes it, in a commit naming both. A record he repeated for emphasis stays, and the crystallized line carries that emphasis. A record whose subject is gone from the system is irrelevant and archived the same way.
