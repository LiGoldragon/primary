## What was actually wrong

Every background Claude flow **already** gets a Remote Control URL at launch.
The v6 and v7 launchers pass `--remote-control <name>`, and each flow's
transcript carries the receipt: `type: "system", subtype: "bridge_status"`,
`"/remote-control is active · Continue here, on your phone, or at
https://claude.ai/code/session_…"`. Nothing was ever missing from the launch.

What broke is the daemon's idle retirement. `~/.claude/daemon.log`:

```
[2026-09-17T04:21:33.840Z] [bg] bg retire b492510d: settled, idle 8h
[2026-09-17T04:21:34.104Z] [bg] bg settled b492510d (done)
[2026-09-17T08:30:33.919Z] [bg] bg retire 48cff7d7: settled, idle 8h
[2026-09-17T08:30:34.170Z] [bg] bg settled 48cff7d7 (done)
```

A retired session's process is gone, so its bridge is gone, and its claude.ai
URL answers *"/remote-control is no longer active. Run /remote-control to start
a new session."* The eight hours is a compiled-in constant in the CLI
(`ir=28800000` in `claude-code-2.1.263`), not a setting: there is no knob in
`settings.json` or `daemon.json` to raise it, and `claude daemon
remote-control` is refused in this environment with `'daemon remote-control' is
not available in this environment.` So **any main flow that goes eight hours
without a turn stops being remotely reachable**, and that is what happened
overnight to b49251 — the flow f55ec8 had just handed the primary Claude seat
to. That is the whole of "I can't access any other flow on Claude right now".

The remedy that works today: wake the session. `claude --bg --resume
<session-id>` **with no other flags** wakes it with its saved options — name,
`--remote-control`, `--system-prompt-file`, `--model` — and the same claude.ai
URL comes back alive. Passing flags instead starts a *copy* under a new id; the
CLI says so and I undid one (below). Long term, the flows need a turn inside
every eight hours, which is what f55ec8's thirty-minute fallback wake was for.
