The Sun and the Four Friends

A sun and four friends live together. This is how they work.

The same four layers as the big book: Owl is the primary, Beaver the secondary, Rabbit the tertiary, Hedgehog the quaternary — and the sun is the living.

1 · who is who

One mind, four friends

The friend at the top thinks slowly. The friend at the bottom is quick. Each one has two helpers.

Every layer is a pair: a Claude half and a Codex half, and the same message reaches both.

2 · the staircase

You ask the friend just above you

If you want to do something, you ask the friend one step above — and sometimes they say: try again.

Nobody skips a step. A wish must pass Rabbit, then Beaver, then Owl, before it ever reaches the sun.

3 · two teams

Some friends think. Some friends do.

One team thinks up what to do, the other team does it — and one chair is still empty.

Thinking side: Fable, the older Opus, and Astra, who says “let’s go!” first and looks after. The empty chair waits for an open-source friend, large and wise. Doing side: the newer Opus, Sol, Terra and Luna.

4 · the year

Round and round the year goes

Rain, then sun, then falling leaves, then snow — and then it all starts again.

Spring is the living speaking. Summer is Owl growing the idea. Autumn is Beaver picking it and putting it out. Winter is Rabbit and Hedgehog doing the small quick work, so the year can turn again.
