Presentation.{ «Roles and subflows» }

This is how work is divided. A main flow judges and keeps its context clean. Subflows do the work, each tailor-made for one kind of work and started with one or two lines. In time a subflow is a flow of its own, and work passes down the layers. Each proposal is one edit to one context module. Answer with the number, and "1" or "2" where there are two texts.

**1. The main flow judges**
Vision, module main-flow. Edit.
> A main flow keeps its context clean. It decides, and subflows do the work. When the way forward is unclear, the subflow asks back up. The main flow does not micromanage.

Rests on: 18 Sep, 9 Aug.

**2. What the main flow writes itself**
Vision, module main-flow. Edit.
Text 1:
> The main flow writes its own log, records his words and writes beads. Everything else goes to subflows.

Text 2:
> The main flow writes its own log and beads. Recording his words goes to a tailor-made subflow.

Rests on: 3 Sep (text 1); 3 Oct, "they pass everything to a sub-agent" (text 2).

**3. A flow answers for its subflows**
Vision, module main-flow. Edit.
> A flow is liable for its subflows. It never says "I didn't do it" when its subflow did. Asked, it says "I did it through a subflow."

Rests on: 19 Aug.

**4. Tailor-made, not named skills**
Vision, module main-flow. Edit.
> Each kind of recurring work has its own subflow definition, made for that work. The main flow does not name skills in a hand-off. It picks the right subflow. For the main flow, the subflow is the skill.

Rests on: 3 Oct, 24 Sep.

**5. The hand-off is one or two lines**
Operation, module prompt-crafting. Edit.
> A hand-off to a subflow is one or two lines: what to act on and what to return. Standing instructions live in the subflow's definition, never in the hand-off. File contents are not copied into it. Anything left open goes back as a question.

Rests on: 3 Oct, "the main flow should not put anything in every brief".

**6. Detailed context for an implementation**
Operation, module subflow. Edit.
> An implementer's standing know-how lives in its definition. The context gathered for a job reaches it some other way, not in the hand-off.

Rests on: 3 Oct, "one or two lines, very extremely brief"; 14 Sep, a subflow gathers the context first.

**9. How work passes between the layers**
Vision, module main-flow. Edit.
> The primary layer designs and decides. Implementation goes to the secondary layer, whose main flow coordinates secondary subflows to build and test it. The primary layer is disturbed as little as possible. Nobody launches a primary-layer subflow.

Rests on: 3 Oct, 30 Sep, 18 Sep.
