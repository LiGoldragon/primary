Presentation.{ «Nexuses, later questions» }

5. **Is the Capsule also the world a Nexus loads?** On 8 August he spoke of "the capsule or whatever of that program or that universe", the whole program loaded into the Ethos, Nomos and Logos Nexuses. On 2 October Capsule became the name of the component that makes the place a flow runs in. Are these one meaning or two, and if two, which keeps the word?

6. **Is Horizon a settings repository or a Nexus?** On 5 September he called the Horizon settings repository "a perfect place to specify a new generic node setting". On 19 September he asked for Horizon to become a proper Nexus that answers the cluster's current state. Do the node definitions stay as settings the Nexus reads, or move into its database?

7. **Does the Psyche Nexus log Mind too?** On 25 September he said "Psyche Nexus is going to replace how we log Psyche and Mind", and in the next breath that the Mind Nexus replaces how Mind is logged. Was "and Mind" a slip, so that the Psyche Nexus keeps only his words?

8. **Does Field take in the transcript work, or reach a separate Transcript Nexus?** On 20 September he said "We could even put all the transcript stuff in there, unless we keep that as a separate nexus". On 16 September he asked for Transcript to be made a Nexus of its own.

9. **Is one Nexus per harness wanted?** On 17 September he floated that "every different harness should have its own nexus", so a harness can change without restarting the whole stack. Is this a direction to design, or left as an idea?

10. **Is Orchestrate also the router?** On 13 September he said "You have this object, which is orchestrate. That's the router layer." Elsewhere the router is the part that sends Signal across the network by the type of message. Does Orchestrate grow into that role, or does routing get its own Nexus?

11. **How does Mind's database follow each new topic variant?** On 3 October he said Mind "will be recompiled a lot and have its database updated a lot". Should each Nexus carry its own upgrade from one record shape to the next, or should one shared mechanism in the Nexus library do it for all?

12. **Does a meta socket ever join two Nexuses, or only the owner and a Nexus?** His rule says only some connected pairs share a meta link. Which pairs do: Flow and Message, since Flow's configuration today names the Message Nexus it admits, or none yet?
