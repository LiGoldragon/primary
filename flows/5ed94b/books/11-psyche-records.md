Presentation.{ «Psyche records» }

How his words are kept, distilled, routed, and turned into skills. A seat is one long-lived post held by one flow at a time.

## 1. What he wants

### Nothing he says is lost

His words are the one thing the system must never lose.

> If we are missing logging then the most important part is the psyche logging that we really don't want to miss, and the short summary of what happened.

-- spoken, 1 October 2026

> I'm quite upset that I Spoke and then my words were lost.

-- spoken, 9 August 2026

### The transcript is the log

The conversation itself is the record. Files are archives made from it.

> We use the transcript now. The transcript becomes the log. We switch: the log is the transcript, and then we have archives. We have Flow archives. That's what the files are

-- 18 September 2026

So no seat may run with its transcript switched off.

> you keep spawning with this transcript saving off, which, to me, sounds like a really bad idea because we're using the transcript as a database, right?

-- 19 September 2026

> we shouldn't write these things into files. They have to be in the transcript. ... Files are not really necessary unless we're making the book.

-- typed, 1 October 2026

### Write a log line only when there is a reason

A seat's own log is rare and high level. The details are in the transcript.

> Logging should be rare. It should just be to give a very high-level summary. ... The transcripts are there.

-- typed, recorded 1 September 2026

> I see agents logging: "Okay I put my left foot in front of my right foot," ... It's almost like their log is bigger than their transcript, which is absurd

-- 28 September 2026

A helper seat logs nothing while it works; it gives one short account at the end.

> It's better for a small sub-agent implementer to give a [résumé] of what they did once at the end in the log, once their task is complete. That's sort of what their commit message is, right?

-- spoken, 29 September 2026

### Whoever hears him records him

Every seat that faces him records what he says there, because it holds the context to understand it. Words passed on are not recorded again.

> All Flows should log the psyche according to what they understand they have the context to better understand what psyche said, so they should write the log. ... When the psyche gets relayed it doesn't get logged again, even by the psyche.

-- 24 September 2026

### In what shape

A record is filed by topic, a broad subject.

> psyche shouldnt be organized by aspect, but by topic and date

-- 9 August 2026

Each record quotes only the words that bear on it, with gaps marked by three dots.

> only include the bits in the text that concern that particular entry ... Because the original is in the transcript.

-- spoken, 27 August 2026

No clock times; the seat that heard is known from where the record sits.

> no, no timestamps, and the location of the logging (in the flow's directory) means the session id is already implied

-- typed, 27 August 2026

Every record can be traced to the conversation it came from, so a later reader can go back and read him again.

> Psyche logging could be done with the short session ID besides every records, which would let a later agent verify the entire conversation

-- spoken, 14 August 2026

Proposed: a record holds its topic, its level, his words, whether spoken or typed, the day, and the seat that heard it.

### Speech-to-text is read for meaning

He mostly dictates, and the transcriber errs. The record carries what he meant, corrected inside the quote.

> When you log Psyche verbatim and you know there's been a mistake, you have to correct it in the verbatim part because I never said r-e-s-t. ... You're misquoting me if you use r-e-s-t.

-- spoken, recorded 28 August 2026

### Four levels

His words fall into spirit, intent, vision and notion. Vision is the default; a notion is an idea he is turning over, binding nothing.

> Psyche: it means it's trying to express what it wants to see, how it wants, or the vision. ... The things that it's considering, like the notion. ... The things that it really wants clearly, which is intent. ... The things which sort of guide its life, which is spirit.

-- spoken, 1 October 2026

Intent and spirit are never raw; they come only on his explicit word.

> raw intent, as well as spirit, will always be explicitely brought up by the living

-- typed, 22 August 2026

Things move up: notion to vision, vision to intent, intent to spirit.

> Vision is important to distill often, and we should try and get intent distilled out of vision, like with a double distillation, and then out of intent, we distill into spirit.

-- typed, 17 September 2026

### What is never recorded

An order is not vision.

> This is not vision. This is just a direct order that has nothing to do with skills.

-- typed, 2 October 2026

Nor is anything another machine wrote.

> Whenever something is machine-generated ... none of the content in it should be logged as psyche.

-- typed, recorded 3 September 2026

A correction of how a seat behaves can be vision; designing behavior is drawn wide.

> conduct correction could very well be vision. why did you think they shouldnt be? designing model behavior is extremely important work.

-- typed, 27 August 2026

### How it is distilled

Distilling gathers every record on a topic, from all seats, and says it once, clearly, standing on its own. He approves each statement before it stands.

> a distilled record is self-standing; clarified and purified by the model ... but *always reviewed explicitely by the living psyche*

> psyche distillation is the model attempting to articulate the psyche in a more coherent form, agglomerating records made across several flows that touch the same topic, favoring more recent statements, and favouring statements made with more certainty

-- typed, 22 August 2026

The distilled statement is worth more than the raw, which stays archived and linked.

> It's always better to distill. If you're going to bother, if you're going to bother the living, then increase the value. ... distilled psyche has more value than raw psyche because the raw psyche is always archived

-- spoken, 22 August 2026

> all distillation refers to the raw psyche it was distilled from. this was the distillation protocol from the start.

-- typed, 27 August 2026

Fresh words and what is already distilled are distilled together; he sees the result, not a diff.

> If you distill fresh material along with something that's already been distilled, it all gets distilled together. You don't get a diff.

-- spoken, 3 September 2026

A small ruling makes a small statement, split into the skills it belongs to.

> You have to split up the vision into the right skills.

-- typed, 2 October 2026

### Continually

Distillation is a running habit, not an occasional pass, and covers the whole record from newest back.

> as we go ... agents could almost at every second or third turn propose the distillation of the vision that's been accumulating so far

-- spoken, 27 August 2026

> When I distill I distill everything not just today's. It's just that we start from the most recent and then we go back

-- typed, 2 October 2026

New words are checked against standing vision, newest first, by a capable model.

> Always audit against vision and raise conflicts in vision by scanning the latest raw and giving recency more power.

-- 18 September 2026

### Routed by topic; Psyche notified

When any seat records vision, Psyche is told. Each seat holds topics; new vision or notion on a topic goes to the seats holding it, riding their next delivery after a minimum wait.

> Are my words being logged as vision when it is vision by any Flow that takes it and then is Psyche notified that there's new vision?

> If we know that a psyche is dealing with a certain topic and a new psyche vision or notion that touches that topic arrives, it should be routed to that flow. The next time that it receives messages anyway, right? ... We might wait at least a minimum window.

-- spoken, 3 October 2026

Proposed: one delivery carries every pending record for that seat, each verbatim with its origin; the wait is a setting he chooses.

### Stored once; skills generated from it

All recording, his vision and the system's own knowledge and operations alike, is the single source the skills are generated from. Each kind has its own rules on who may edit it. He wants a steady stream of small proposals he can approve quickly.

> I want skills that do skill edits. I want a constant flow of small skill edit proposals, not huge ones, so that I can say yes quickly. ... I want that whole knowledge/vision/operations/everything logging to be the source that we use to generate our skills. We only store the stuff once, and we have different rules for who can edit what and what type.

-- typed, 3 October 2026

> Why the fuck are the skills not living in three repositories right now: psyche, mind, and field?

-- spoken, 3 October 2026

The skill is the vision concentrated.

> concentrating the vision and just taking the parts that are sort of important to know to understand the concept is how we create skills.

-- spoken, recorded 30 August 2026

Unmarked records are his; records marked operational belong to the mind, the system's own working knowledge.

> Operational is mind, and when it's unprefixed, it means it's all psyche.

-- 19 September 2026

A failure that recurs means a skill lacks a line.

> every time there's a failure like that, it means that something wasn't written to a skill.

-- spoken, 3 October 2026

### Remembering a flow

All seats are one mind. Told "you said" of something another seat said, a seat remembers it: reads that seat's records, its transcript if needed. The remembering goes in its log with what mattered; no separate report.

> all flows are the same subjectivity. ... the flow in question must endeavor to try to remember, with an appropriate level of detail

-- typed, 26 August 2026

> If the flow pulls things into its context, it has remembered. why create more artifacts? ... the main log file should have the remembered flows.

-- typed, 22 August 2026

A successor starts with the most concentrated vision of the seats before it.

> Let's always get the concentrated vision from the current flow into the next one.

-- spoken, 1 October 2026

### Archives

What matters is kept apart so transcripts can be cleared; he decides what to keep first.

> Maybe we have an intermediary spot that at least only picks the important things, like what the psyche said and the last responses that the model gave every time it gave a final response

-- typed, 17 September 2026

> if I'm keeping the transcript even for a bit, it gives me the chance to decide: do I want to make it and put it into a file before I delete the transcript later on?

-- spoken, 29 September 2026

## 2. What exists today

Witnessed. There are 20 distilled vision topics and 10 intent topics. The older raw collection still holds 90 files. Raw records sit in the folders of 228 flows: 1,485 vision files and 59 notion files. The intent topics list their sources; the distilled vision topics list none. The rule seats follow says: record his words as spoken, before acting, one statement per write, in the hearing seat, by topic, his words corrected for transcription, ending spoken or typed. Distilled statements stand only on his approval; replaced records move to archive files. The skills are still written inside Curriculum. Routing by topic exists as one book, with no comments on it yet.

## 3. Questions

**1. Is vision itself the reason to record?** Say he states one design sentence in the middle of a deployment talk.

> No I don't want you to log anything except when there's a reason to log it. I never told anyone to log everything I said ever.

-- typed, 3 October 2026

> We need to, every turn, have some little bit of vision distilled. Even if it's just a little, let's just try doing that all the time.

-- typed, 17 September 2026

Answer yes if any vision is reason enough to record it at once. Answer 2 if it waits in the transcript until distillation finds it.

**2. May a seat change distilled vision directly?** Say he contradicts a statement that already stands.

> I just want to see the vision. I'll read it, and if I agree with it, then it lands. That's it.

-- spoken, 3 September 2026

> If what I'm talking about is something in the distilled vision, like obviously you can log what I say, but then you can also just apply what I say directly to the distilled vision

-- spoken, recorded 30 August 2026

Answer yes if the seat applies it and shows him after. Answer 2 if it proposes and waits for his yes.

**3. Is a raw record a file?** Say he speaks about books today.

> Actually, the report becomes everything is in the transcript. We don't want to make files anymore.

-- typed, 17 September 2026

> We write the data in a place that is used by the curriculum tool to generate the skills.

-- spoken, 3 October 2026

Answer 1 if his words are written to the topic's record at once. Answer 2 if they stay a marked passage in the transcript until distilled into the store.

**4. Where does the single store live?** Say a seat looks up everything said on one topic.

> We already agreed that there's a repo called Psyche where all the vision is going to go, and that would generate skills with curriculum.

-- 20 September 2026

> Use components like Psyche instead of this makeshift file system for logging Psyche that we have.

-- typed, recorded 15 September 2026

Answer 1 for a Psyche repository of text the generator reads. Answer 2 for the Psyche program's database, with text as its export.
