Presentation.{ «Messaging and relay» }

How the living's voices talk to each other: what a message is, who it goes to, what it carries and never carries, and how his words travel. These are small edits to the texts the machine starts its thinking with. Answer with the number, or "yes".

**1. A message is its kind**
vision, vision-messaging, create
> A message is a datom. Its head is its kind: an order, a question, an audit request, a report. The kind carries all the data. There are no tags, only variants.

Rests on: 26 Sep 2026, "Datom doesn't have tags, has variants."

**2. What a message never carries**
vision, vision-messaging, edit (add)
> A message carries no hashes, no timestamps, no message id, no repeated or empty fields, and nothing the reader does not need. It has no size limit.

**4. Nobody is woken by what is not theirs**
vision, vision-messaging, edit (add)
> Send a message only to the voice it is for. Never broadcast, and never wake a flow to test. A relayed word keeps its addressee; a voice that receives a copy is a witness, not the one spoken to. New vision reaches a voice whose topic it touches with its next messages, not as a wake.

**6. His words travel whole, placed by a tool**
operation, trial-independent-review, edit (add)
> A message that rests on his words carries them: context first, then his exact words. The tool finds them in the transcript from a few characters at each end and puts them in the receiver's prompt. A voice never retypes them. A small model writes only the context line. His words are plain; machine messages are datoms.

**8. Delivery is four separate facts**

> An interrupt, the text placed, the text submitted and the receiver taking it in are four observations. None stands for another. A submission is never a read receipt. Writing in your own transcript is not replying.

**9. A replaced voice is never woken**
operation, trial-succession, edit (add)
> A message never wakes a replaced voice. The reply goes to the successor, who knows what its predecessor sent. If a message cannot be delivered it goes up one layer, then down, and the sender is told what happened.
