Presentation.{ «Permissions and authority» }

Who may do what without asking him, how authority moves between layers of agents, how secrets travel, and what a check may be. He approves nothing by hand; what an agent decides to do, it may do.

**1. Launch with permissions skipped**
Operation, trial-unblocking-commands, edit.
"Every seat, on every harness and machine, is launched with permission prompts off, including other people's seats on the cluster. A seat launched otherwise is relaunched."
Rests on: 24 Sep, 1 Sep.

**2. A harness prompt holds a seat**
Operation, trial-unblocking-commands, edit. Text 1, 2 or 3.
1. "A program answers a harness prompt within seconds."
2. "The command is rewritten before it runs so the prompt cannot appear."
3. "Both."
Rests on: 3 Oct. One fact decides it: Claude's guard on risky deletions held one seat 11 hours.

**3. Which harness may do everything**
Operation, trial-contact-discipline, edit. Text 1 or 2.
1. "The primary layer, Claude and Codex both, may do anything; what lower layers cannot get through is passed up to it."
2. "One chosen harness may do anything; the other passes up to it."
Rests on: 3 Oct, still open in his words.

**4. Pass-ups never reach him unless they need him**
Operation, trial-contact-discipline, edit.
"The top seat answers every pass-up itself, acting or refusing. Only what needs his money, his identity or his phone reaches him. A blocked item names what blocks it."
Rests on: 24 Sep, 14 Sep; the answer is the book's proposal.

**5. A question and a request**
Operation, psyche-interraction, edit. Text 1 or 2.
1. "'Can we do X?' about something plainly wanted is a request: do it."
2. "'Can we do X?' is a question: answer it, found not guessed, and wait for a request."
Rests on: 24 Sep, a question's answer is found.

**6. What a check may be**
Operation, testing, edit.
"A check is code at a real boundary, run by a program. A rule a model follows, a model review standing in for a build, or a probe a model runs is not a check. A model review guarding what a build, test or activation could catch is replaced by that code and removed."
Rests on: 3 Oct, 28 Sep. Model gates missed a build failure and two failed activations.

**7. The Herdr stop rule**
Knowledge, knowledge-flow, edit.
"Where a seat runs does not limit what it may do. Flow guards his panes in code; the stop rule for seats outside Herdr is removed."
Rests on: 24 Sep, 18 Sep.

**8. Which skills change only on his word**
Operation, skill-designing, edit. Text 1 or 2.
1. "Vision and intent change only on his word."
2. "Every psyche skill, notion included, changes only on his word."
Both add: "Operation skills may be written and deployed on his description alone, after the primary layer reviews them."
Rests on: 28 Sep; on 2 Oct he dropped the gold-skill idea.

**9. Secrets reach programs, not agents**
Operation, secrets, edit.
"A secret goes to the process that needs it, never through a conversation. Agents that push to public repositories are not trusted with tokens."
Rests on: 1 Oct, 14 Sep.

**10. Credentials before the secret holder exists**
Operation, secrets, edit.
"Until the cluster's secret holder exists, a program may copy a credential into a seat's sandbox, and the agent sees only that it succeeded."
Rests on: 2 Oct. Yes or no.
