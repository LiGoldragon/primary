**6. How he answers**
operation, operation-book, edit (add)
> He answers by commenting on the message. A comment belongs to the point it sits on, and reaches the flow as his words plus the place in the source. A message he has commented on is never edited and sent again; the reply is a new message. A comment is an example of a way of thinking, not a new law.
