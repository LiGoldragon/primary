Presentation.{ «Context» }

A machine's output is a function of what is in its head. Everything he wants changed comes down to editing, creating, removing, splitting and merging the modules that fill it: vision, intent, spirit, knowledge, operation.

**1. The three strata**
Knowledge, context-strata, edit.
"His words travel in the middle stratum until distilled, then rise to the top. What a flow fetches or says itself is bottom and binds nothing unless anchored in a skill or entry file."
Rests on: 13 Sep, 14 Aug.

**2. What every flow's system prompt carries**
Spirit, spirit, edit. Text 1, 2 or 3.
1. "The top stratum of every flow carries Spirit only."
2. "The top stratum carries Spirit and Intent."
3. "The top stratum carries Spirit, Intent and steady Vision chosen per role."
Rests on: 18 and 19 Aug (spirit on top, universal invariants); 25 Sep for 3.

**3. What a context module is**
Knowledge, context-modules, create.
"A context module is a Markdown file with a type and a name. Types are vision, intent, spirit, knowledge, operation, and a role is a type. Flow keeps a registry of each module's type, name and location."
Rests on: 3 Oct.

**4. Harness-specific rules get their own type**
Knowledge, context-modules, edit.
"A rule that belongs to one harness or model is a module of its own type, placed in that harness's system prompt, not in Spirit."
Rests on: 17 Sep.

**5. Cut the stock prompts**
Knowledge, claude-harness, edit.
"The stock system prompt is cut into typed modules, every line marked behavior, personality, operational safety or harness use, so each can be kept, replaced or dropped alone."
Rests on: 3 Oct.

**6. Different top strata for different jobs**
Vision, vision-flow, edit.
"Different jobs get different top strata, programmable per flow."
Rests on: 30 Aug, which he called drafting. Yes makes it a ruling.

**7. Entry files**
Knowledge, context-strata, edit.
"The entry files are ours entirely; anything particular to one machine goes in linked secondary files. They explain nothing the harness already does. They are one more output of the context standard: the same modules selected for the entry-file place."
Rests on: 22 Aug, 25 Aug.

**8. Smallest, highest-signal context**
Operation, prompt-crafting, edit.
"A new flow's context is placed in full in its first prompt, one block, what matters at the top. Never tell it to read files for its context."
Rests on: 3 Oct, 24 Sep, 29 Sep.

**9. What a subflow receives**
Operation, subflow, edit.
"A subflow receives the harness's built-in instructions, its role's modules, its loadable skills, and a brief carrying only the task. It does not receive its main flow's system-prompt modules or withheld startup modules. Standing context lives in the subagent's definition, never in a repeated brief."
Rests on: 3 Oct, 24 Sep. First witness in each harness's code that the parent's replaced prompt does not reach subagents.

**10. Role packets are templated**
Operation, skill-designing, edit.
"Role packets pass through the same per-harness templating as skill bodies."
Rests on: the book's own question 7; today packets are copied as written.

**11. Commentary stays rare**
Operation, claude-harness, edit.
"Reserve commentary for very rare cases." The same line goes in each harness's system-prompt module.
Rests on: the book's own question 10.

**12. How a prompt edit reaches him**
Operation, skill-designing, edit.
"A prompt edit is proposed as one module, its lines before and after, naming the stratum and roles it reaches. On his yes a program lands it in Curriculum, regenerates every harness's output and registers the module. No flow edits a prompt by hand."
Rests on: 3 Oct.
