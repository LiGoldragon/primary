5. **Roll back or roll forward?** On 15 September you asked for an automatic countdown rollback on big changes. On 24 September you said there was nothing to roll back to and the change must roll forward. Proposed: the countdown applies whenever a working version exists to return to, and the change rolls forward only when none does. Is that right?

6. **Explain first, or just deploy?** On 22 August you asked to have a Zeus update explained first. On 19 August and 28 September you said not to ask again once you say deploy. Proposed: the explanation is sent with the deployment and never holds it. Is that right?

7. **Which monitoring system?** On 3 October you asked which system is best for low overhead, lots of data, pictures and alerts. Should research compare the existing tools before anything is built?

8. **Does the dashboard live on Prometheus,** and is it reached from your phone over the mesh?

9. **What is "Mega"?** On 29 September you said you would rather turn off "this Mega" and connect to your own Wi-Fi. It was transcribed as heard and is not known.

10. **Is the watcher wanted?** On 1 October you said there is a chance you don't want the watcher that was built. Is watching for a USB network device being plugged in the only behaviour wanted?

11. **What is "Yexto" traffic?** On 1 October you said a sharing machine lets a kind of traffic through that was transcribed as "Yexto". Is that IPv6 traffic, or something else?

12. **Stable Ethernet mode.** On 22 September you described a mode, switched by an admin user, in which a laptop on a stable cable becomes a Wi-Fi access point. On 24 September you said the access point turns on by itself when the cable gives internet. Does the automatic rule replace the switch?

13. **Certificates for Wi-Fi.** Should every phone and laptop get its own certificate from your own authority now, or should one shared password stay for the moment?

14. **Reboot from the network and the startup screen without a monitor.** Which hardware should be used for this: a remote power switch, a network console card, or something built into the machines?

15. **Your own mesh, or an existing one?** On 24 September you asked whether an existing tailnet mesh already does what you were building. Should the mesh use an existing tool, with Criome identity added on top?

16. **The two new hosts.** What are their names, and should they be routers, second screens for the persona, or both?

17. **The install stick.** On 5 September you asked for a minimal install image that is added to every cluster by rule. Should it be built now?

18. **Should Zeus's location be fixed** in the cluster data, so its screen warmth matches where you are?

19. **The house router.** Should Prometheus's internet keep coming from the router's cable, with only its Wi-Fi turned off near your bed?

20. **Deploy everywhere at once, or one machine at a time?** Should a failure on one machine stop the rest, or should the others carry on?
