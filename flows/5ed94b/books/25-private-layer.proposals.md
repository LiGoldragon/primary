Presentation.{ «The private layer» }

The part of the system that holds what is his alone: the soul, served only by an open-source model, with Criome proving who is who and Persona running everything. Chartered, not active until its open-source seat runs. Say "yes" to a number to land it; for two texts, name the number.

**1. Every layer has a private part**
Kind: vision. Module: private-layer. Action: create.
> Each layer has a private half beside its public half. The private halves speak to each other, top to bottom, apart from the public ones.

Rests on: 14 Sep.

**2. The soul**
Kind: vision. Module: private-layer. Action: edit.
Text 1:
> The soul, layer zero and the core are three names for one thing: the private part of the persona, with its own private repository.

Text 2:
> The soul is the private half of every layer. The core is only the deepest layer, which is self-hosted.

Rests on: 13 Sep, 17 Sep, 18 Sep.

**3. The deepest layer guards the rest**
Kind: vision. Module: private-layer. Action: edit.
> The deepest layer holds everything private. It checks that a request is meant, not malicious, and truly from him, and reviews code for malice before any update.

Rests on: 14 Sep.

**4. Served only by an open-source model**
Kind: vision. Module: private-layer. Action: edit.
Text 1:
> No commercial model ever serves the private layer. It runs on Kimi K3 through OpenCode, by a provider chosen for privacy, so it stays self-hostable.

Text 2:
> The same, run on an open model on his own machine.

Rests on: 14 Sep, 13 Sep.

**5. Thinking down**
Kind: vision. Module: private-layer. Action: edit.
> The private layer may hand thinking down to a commercial model once the idea, put in general terms, is no longer private.

Rests on: 14 Sep.

**6. Sterilizing a question**
Kind: operation. Module: private-layer. Action: edit.
> Before a question goes down, the private model rewrites it as a stranger might ask it, with every name, place, account, number and relationship removed. If anyone could still tell whose question it is, it stays private. The answer returns to the private layer, which puts the particulars back.

Rests on: 14 Sep, "There's no name, there's no association". The method is proposed.

**7. Nothing refused is sent**
Kind: intent. Module: private-layer. Action: edit.
> Hard subjects are talked through privately first. Only what a commercial model will accept is sent, since a refusal wastes context and can cost him his account.

Rests on: 14 Sep.

**8. When a refusal comes**
Kind: intent. Module: private-layer. Action: edit.
Text 1:
> A refusal stops that line of work at once and the question goes to the private layer.

Text 2:
> A refusal rewrites the question once more in more general terms and tries again.

Rests on: 14 Sep, "it'll get feedback to correct it".

**9. Every refusal becomes a lesson**
Kind: operation. Module: private-layer. Action: edit.
> Each refusal is written down privately, per model, with the general form of the question. The filter reads this list before every send.

Rests on: 14 Sep, "Everything is turned into a lesson". The list is proposed.

**10. What is never sent**
Kind: intent. Module: private-layer. Action: edit.
> Never sent: his name and the names of people near him; his accounts, keys, tokens and phone number; his home, cameras and recordings; anything that ties a question to him; anything the refusal list says would be refused.

Rests on: 14 Sep. The list is proposed.

**11. Criome and Persona**
Kind: vision. Module: private-layer. Action: edit.
> Criome proves who may do what: every layer runs its own process with its own key, replaces SSH, and he authorizes from a cold key through Unity. Persona owns which flows run and where. The soul owns what is private, runs inside the Persona and signs with its own Criome key.

Rests on: 14 Sep, 1 Oct, 25 Sep. The division is proposed.

**12. When the separation starts**
Kind: vision. Module: private-layer. Action: edit.
Text 1:
> Everything is public until the soul's private repository opens; from that day private material is kept apart.

Text 2:
> Everything is public until the open-source seat runs; from that day private material is kept apart.

Rests on: 10 Sep, "We're gonna work towards creating a separation of private data."
