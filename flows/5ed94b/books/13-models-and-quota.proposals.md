Presentation.{ «Models, effort and quota» }

Which model sits in which seat, what effort means, how quota is read, when the system slows down, and what cost is counted. Quota is the share of a subscription's usage window already spent.

**1. Better models, not higher effort**
Operation, compensation-default-effort, edit. Text 1 or 2.
1. "Capability comes from a better model, never higher effort. Effort stays medium and may go lighter for speed. High effort is kept for one case: a quota about to reset unused. Tier words high, medium, low, ultra-low name power, set by the model in the seat, not by effort."
2. As 1, with no exception for a quota about to reset.
Rests on: 18 Sep, 13 Sep; 13 Sep allowed the exception, later words said never raised.

**9. Quota is read by one call**
Operation, quota-reading, create.
"One call returns every subscription, every quota window and every live seat's context use. It is pushed by events, never polled, and uses no model. Figures come from the harness's own records; no seat is asked to report."
Rests on: 3 Oct, 14 Sep.

**10. Quota figures reach seats**
Operation, quota-reading, edit. Text 1 or 2.
1. "Every seat's next message carries five figures: percent used, percent left, time to reset, burn over the last five hours, today's share against fourteen percent."
2. "Only main flows' messages carry them."
Rests on: 18 Sep; the context cost of every message decides.

**11. When low power starts**
Operation, trial-low-power, edit.
"A provider is in low power when a subscription is more than one day's share ahead of the fourteen-percent line, or its five-hour window passes eighty percent. In low power Fable is not called, new helpers drop one tier, and only primary work starts. A low-priority helper may refuse a job the quota cannot pay for."
Rests on: 14 Sep, 16 Sep. The numbers are the book's proposal.

**12. Cost is counted in context**
Spirit, spirit, edit.
"A useless token cost must be eliminated. Switching a running seat's model is a cost: everything is read again."
Rests on: late Aug, 24 Sep.
