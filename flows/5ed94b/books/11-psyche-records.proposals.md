Presentation.{ «Psyche records» }

This is how his words are kept, distilled, routed and turned into the skills. Skills are made from his vision, stored once, and edited by a steady stream of small proposals he can approve quickly.

**1. Record only with a reason**
Operation, psyche-interraction, edit. Text 1 or 2.
1. "Any vision is reason enough: record it at once, by topic, before acting."
2. "Vision waits in the transcript until distillation finds it; log only when there is a reason."
Rests on: 3 Oct and 17 Sep, which pull two ways.

**2. Who writes the record**
Operation, psyche-interraction, edit. Text 1, 2 or 3.
1. "The seat that hears him writes the record; words relayed are not recorded again."
2. "A small helper started by a hook on each message pulls his words from the transcript."
3. "The Psyche seat writes the record."
Rests on: 24 Sep for 1; earlier words for 2 and 3.

**3. Shape of a record**
Operation, psyche-interraction, edit.
"A record is filed by topic. It holds the level, his words as spoken or typed, the day, and the seat that heard it. Quote only the words that bear on it, gaps marked by three dots. No clock times."
Rests on: 9 Aug, 27 Aug, 14 Aug.

**4. Dictation is read for meaning**
Operation, psyche-interraction, edit.
"Correct transcription errors inside the quote; record what he meant, never the misheard word."
Rests on: 28 Aug.

**5. Four levels**
Knowledge, psyche, edit.
"His words are spirit, intent, vision or notion. Vision is the default. A notion is an idea he is turning over and binds nothing. Intent and spirit are recorded only on his explicit word. Words rise: notion to vision, vision to intent, intent to spirit."
Rests on: 1 Oct, 22 Aug, 17 Sep.

**6. What is never recorded as his**
Operation, psyche-interraction, edit.
"An order is not vision. Nothing a machine wrote is logged as psyche. A correction of how a seat behaves can be vision."
Rests on: 2 Oct, 3 Sep, 27 Aug.

**7. How distillation runs**
Operation, psyche-distillation, edit.
"Gather every record on a topic from all seats and say it once, standing alone. Fresh and already-distilled words are distilled together, and he sees the result, not a diff. Start from the newest and go back. A small ruling makes a small statement, split into the skills it belongs to. He approves each statement before it stands."
Rests on: 22 Aug, 3 Sep, 2 Oct.

**8. Distilled vision lists its sources**
Operation, psyche-distillation, edit.
"Every distilled vision statement carries a source line naming the raw records it came from."
Rests on: 27 Aug, and today intent topics already do this.

**9. Contradicting standing vision**
Operation, psyche-distillation, edit. Text 1 or 2.
1. "When he contradicts a distilled statement, apply it directly and show him after."
2. "When he contradicts a distilled statement, propose the change and wait for his yes."
Rests on: 30 Aug for 1; 3 Sep for 2.

**10. Who composes a distillation**
Operation, psyche-distillation, edit. Text 1 or 2.
1. "The main seat composes a distillation; helpers only gather records."
2. "A helper composes the distillation and the main seat reviews it."
Rests on: the current rule for 1; his word that main seats pass work to helpers for 2.

**11. Routing new vision**
Operation, psyche-routing, create. Text 1 or 2.
1. "New vision or notion goes only to the seats holding its topic, in one delivery after a wait, carrying every pending record verbatim with its origin."
2. "As 1, and seats in a shared-psyche cluster also get every update."
Rests on: 3 Oct for 1; 17 Sep for 2.

**12. A recurring failure means a missing line**
Operation, trial-recurring-failure, edit.
"A failure that recurs means a skill lacks a line. Propose that one line to him as a small edit."
Rests on: 3 Oct.
