What Unity is

Most people will meet it as an app. That app is the front face of a persona thinking machine system: a set of thinking machines arranged in layers of authority, holding your knowledge, doing your work, and answering to you.

The Unity basic thinking machine programming system that you can pay someone, anyone, to install.The psyche, 14 September 2026

There is no vendor. Anyone you trust can install it and set it up for you, on hardware you own or hardware you rent. The client is multi-platform and is built on Slint.

Four layers of authority

The layers are named after the Vedas, the old Sanskrit terms for the four layers of authority in humanity, and carry the same intent: at the top is the top authority, and so on down. Each one operates at a different part of the system. Read this as a core sample — the top authority at the surface, the earth below it.

PrimaryFirst layer

Where ideas go

The top authority

The last resort between idea and production. Ideas arrive here. It can monitor everything below it and give orders anywhere down.

SecondarySecond layer

The trunk and the memory

The stable middle

A large knowledge memory system, consistently aware of a great deal, and able to interact with you directly. It is the stable one — the heart, the trunk of the thinking machine — trusted for a fairly reliable, current view of things. When it is not sure, it goes up.

TertiaryThird layer

Voice

The fast, mercurial layer

This is the one you talk to. Speech to text, back and forth, very fast. It uses the middle layer as its quick, good knowledge rather than working things out alone.

QuaternaryFourth layer

Maintenance

The janitors

The public space, the earthy layer. Garbage collection of data that is neither useful nor dangerous, maintenance of the system, monitoring and reporting — here’s something that looks like maybe a trade violation. Cheap, long, continuing jobs that always check everything and clean up.

Layer zeroSelf-hosted

Intent, non-malice, true source

Beneath the four

Layer zero is self-hosted and holds all the private information. It is mostly a double-check of intent, non-malice and true source — confirming that the person speaking to the account is who they say they are, with trusted cameras and a witness cut kept for the archive.

The layers below can ask questions up. To actually go higher, it has to be done by that layer itself — and the top can monitor everything below it.

The soul

Your private layer. It runs on your own cluster, ruled by a kernel process on a dedicated machine, and it is where your speech-to-text models — eventually trained on your own speaking and your own data — live.

The idea is that people have their own cluster, their own thinking machine system, personalized to them.The psyche, 13 September 2026

The soul is the private part of the persona. You can choose where its kernel runs: a machine in your house, or a private provider you pick, even in the cloud.

Criome

The criome is sort of like the concept of this web based on cryptography. It’s like a cryptographic biome. That’s why it’s criome.The psyche, 14 September 2026

Criome is the network Unity lives on. criome.net is the canonical public-facing service — the cryptographic biome network. The operating system side of it is CriomeOS.

Unity packages a Linux sandbox so the rest of the nexuses it needs can be installed alongside it, each a sandboxed, containerized process with its own file system and network. Any traffic that is not local to the sandbox goes through our own transport signal.

Secrets
No secret is stored on a host. Secrets are loaded remotely, into the one process that needs them, using throwaway memory salt so there is nothing to recover. Shut the host down and it loses access. The request to open something arrives as a notification, through Unity, and you decide.
The council
Nothing ships on one machine’s opinion. Large problems go through a council of thinking machines at the top: The three have to agree to push something into production, but only two have to agree to implement the proof of concept.
The messenger
You address the system as a single entity. When you speak to any one of them, the messenger fires and tells the other half — carrying your words between them as you said them. It is a small model doing one simple job.
Authentication
The layer between agents is secured on the machine itself. Messaging and the spawning of new harnesses are authenticated at multiple layers, sandboxed, and controlled by more highly permissioned nexuses.
