Does the right harness make any model?

Three 2026 papers, all read on their primary abstracts.

Harness-Bench. 106 agent tasks, same base models, large variation from the harness alone. Capability should be reported per model-and-harness pair. The dominant failure is reasoning disconnected from tool evidence.
Strong-to-weak transfer via harnesses. A stronger model designs an inference-time harness for a weaker one, no weight changes. Weak-model score on the tested task rose from 0.49 to 0.91. Weaker models gain most. Tested on theory-of-mind tasks, not coding.
Co-evolving harnesses and models. Harness evolution alone lifts a weaker model on seven enterprise agent benchmarks. But copying a stronger model's trajectories wholesale regressed weak models by 4 to 30 points, because they adopt the plan without the competence to execute it. Correcting only the weak model's own failing turns recovers the gains.

Verdict c: your claim has real support. The harness raises the ceiling a lot. The caveat is that guidance must fit the model's own competence; a stronger model's harness copied verbatim can make a weaker one worse.
