## A behavior line blocks nothing

Context: the living, reading the book "What truly blocks", whose behavior lines were marked long-term.

"Some of that stuff that you're marking long-term is just a behavior that we want from the thinking machine. Therefore there's nothing blocking this from being the behavior that we program right now. Anything that is just a thinking machine behavior guideline is non-blocking because there's no software to write. It's just an instruction line.

There's actually nothing quite mysterious or unusual about not locking on things that are not dependencies of the thing we're trying to do. I'm curious as to why we even need this instruction."
-- psyche, STT.
