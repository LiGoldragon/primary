## The living is the living; psyche is an aspect of the machine

Context: the living, reading the book "The living's words reach the decider", on the module intent-psyche-interaction.

"I opened this book that talks about editing psyche interaction. It hit me: both the name of the module and the way the word psyche is used in all of this context overload the word psyche because what we really mean in this context is the living psyche (which we've come to just call the living). I think it's a problem. For now we need to be more appropriate with our choice of words.

The psyche is one aspect or is mostly used to mean one aspect of the thinking machine. Now we're assigning double meaning where psyche means the living and maybe the living psyche is not even necessary. Maybe we just call the living the living and keep it simple like that so that the context module name would become living interaction.

Also when it starts and it says (and I'm going to correct psyche here with living, even though that's not what it says), it would say the living interaction or the main flow interacts with the living. Actually the truth is that every main flow may interact with the living."
-- psyche, STT.
