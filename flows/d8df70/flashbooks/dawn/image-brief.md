# Image brief: "The Dawn of the Meta Harness"

Five illustrations, made with your built-in image generation. The living's rule:
"Our illustrations are supposed to convey information." Each image must carry
its page's information at a glance. Draw the short labels given below legibly
into the scene, spelled exactly as written. Draw nothing decorative and no
unlabelled figures. Do not draw anything running unless the brief says it runs.

Shared style: an isometric cutaway in ink and gouache on warm cream paper, in the
register of the Field's "Persona & the Nexuses" plate. Warm light. Red means
broken, green means moved forward, grey means unavailable. Portrait 2:3.

Save as flows/d8df70/flashbooks/dawn/img/01.png to 05.png, plus img/manifest.json
(file, prompt, generated source path). Commit only those paths.
