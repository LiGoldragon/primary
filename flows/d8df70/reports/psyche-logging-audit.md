## Method and limits

Times are UTC; local time is six hours behind. The window is the UTC day
2026-09-24, which starts at 18:00 local on 09-23 and ends before the living's
09-24 evening. One message counted under 836818 and the three under 47764b
fall in that early UTC stretch, which is the evening of 09-23 locally.

Claude seats were read from their session transcripts under
`/home/li/.claude/projects/-home-li-primary/`; record line numbers are line
numbers in those files. Codex seats were read from rollouts under
`~/.codex/sessions/` and `~/.codex-next/sessions/`; record line numbers are
line numbers in those. A message counts as the living's when it is typed
user input in the living's own voice; launcher and seat prompts, skill text,
system reminders, `Machine.Relay` envelopes, machine provenance headers,
peer reports and subagent hand-backs were excluded. Borderline cases were
resolved by reading the message.

"Logged" means a verbatim sentence of the message appears in the flow's own
`vision/`, `notion/` or `log.md`. Matching was literal, sentence by sentence,
over every sentence of each message; a paraphrase does not count, and several
Mind and Field logs paraphrase the living in the third person. "Forwarded"
means a verbatim sentence appears in 752e0f's or 836818's files or
transcripts.

Field eb7bae's rollout was read and holds no direct living input on
2026-09-24; the living's words reached it only through a Psyche relay. Four
seats have no transcript at all and are listed above.
