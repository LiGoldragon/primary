## 1. A voice idle between answers: still one flow?

A voice (Psyche, Mind, Field, by rank) answers, then its harness sits open waiting for the next message. Is that still one running flow, or did the answer end it and the next message start another? Your words lean one way:

"They're not so much a voice that is reachable all the time as a job that gets done and then it returns and then it's not reachable anymore." -- psyche, typed, 2026-10-02 (on side flows, set against a voice).
"sessions can be reloaded in the new harness one at a time as they go idle. As soon as the session goes idle, the hook kicks in." -- psyche, STT, book comment, 2026-09-30.
"The old flow is closed when it has a replacement, at which point it shouldn't be reachable anymore" -- psyche, typed, book comment, 2026-09-24.

Read together: a voice's flow stays one flow through its idle stretches and ends only when a refresh replaces it; a side flow ends when its job is done. What your words do not say: whether "idle" is a state Flow records beside Running and Ended, or just Running with nothing in progress. Mind Astra proposes no third public state (Running, Ended; the harness's own "stop" signal being unreliable as a proof of ending).

1. One flow through idle; only Running and Ended; idle is not a public state.
2. One flow through idle, but Flow records Idle as a third state every seat can read.
3. Each answer ends the flow; the next message starts a new one.
