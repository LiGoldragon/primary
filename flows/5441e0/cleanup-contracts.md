## Open-request completeness gap
The current ordinary/meta root inputs expose no complete query that enumerates open Orders/Questions/Tasks/reminders BOTH SENT AND RECEIVED for arbitrary address. ObserveSends/ObserveSender/ObserveDeliveries concern delivery snapshots and caller-filtered observations, not a complete answerability ledger. Answer.requestid addresses one already-known id; it is not discovery. ReadSendOutcomes exposes admission outcomes, not a complete open-request set. Queues=[] and attempts=[] therefore DO NOT prove request absence. Unknowns must be kept under the cleanup authority; do not use raw Memory or infer silence is completion. This audit offers no complete bidirectional absence oracle.

## Mutation semantics: important correction
`flow 'Stop.flowid'` is the supported ordinary Stop. a044 crates/flow-nexus/src/lib.rs549-631 reads the recorded node/restart custody, closes the verified pane with its lease where present and records Stopped. It retains the FlowId/session/history/queued work; it does NOT archive every request, delete the row or deregister the permaflow declaration. StopRejected includes UnknownFlow/AlreadyStopped/RouteUnavailable/CloseRefused/PersistenceRefused/Restarting/SpawnUnproven. No Stop was executed by this audit.

`flow-meta 'Retire.flowid'` is a DISTINCT privileged operation, not an alias or an automatic cleanup follow-up. Source lib.rs1265-1336 closes/records Retired, refuses a Maintained current address and unresolved restart. It also retains the row/history. Ordinary64 ethos284-298 explicitly states Stopped/Exited/Retired are all retained records, none deletion. `flow-meta 'Remove.Permaflow.{ Mind topic Sol }'` is separate policy mutation, not Stop semantics and not implied authorization here. No supported single closes+archives+deregisters operation is established.
