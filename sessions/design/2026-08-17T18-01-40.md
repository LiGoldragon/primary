# Design session

The psyche asked to design an aspect focused on surveying all flows, verifying what has and has not been done, and preventing work from getting out of hand or falling through cracks. Candidate language raised by the psyche: “Maintenance,” “survey,” and “Overseer,” with the first two judged insufficient and the last offered tentatively.

Requested evidence base:

- related skills in popular skill repositories;
- the wider theoretical and practical field of meta-level AI flows used to oversee other AI flows;
- work by leading thinkers and developers;
- recovered prior psyche expressions touching this subject.

Skills active for the session: management, session-log, spirit, psyche, psyche-interraction, psyche-acquisition, and design.

Open work: recover existing psyche; conduct delegated repository and field research; synthesize the design anatomy and naming space; continue the conversation with the psyche before implementation.

## Dispatches

- `psyche_acquisition`: recovering exact prior psyche expressions, their authority levels, chronology, and tensions.
- `skill_repo_research`: surveying relevant artifacts in popular AI-agent skill repositories and extracting their conceptual and naming vocabulary.
- `field_research`: surveying the wider theory and practice of meta-level workflow oversight, including contrary evidence and architectural failure modes.

## Psyche acquisition returned

The closest prior ruling is the need to “routinely look at the very high-level view” of the whole engine. Relevant boundaries also surfaced: managers preserve context and do not investigate implementation detail; shards currently have no authority; awareness is general understanding rather than task-state narration; session logs, goals, tests, commits, provenance, and psyche approval are candidate evidence substrates rather than interchangeable proof. The prior naming doctrine favors single-word concepts over person-implying roles, which weighs against `Overseer` as an aspect name. No relevant Intent entry exists. The aspect-vs-Design-subflow-vs-meta-harness-service boundary remains open.

## Research synthesis

All three research dispatches returned. Current skill ecosystems contain strong bounded patterns—fresh verification before completion, PR babysitting, issue/workflow reconciliation, coverage and progress ledgers, work-front mapping, queue triage, handoff persistence, and durable runtimes—but the survey found no mature artifact that inventories and reconciles every flow and work source across a whole project ecology.

The wider field supplies the constituent theories: meta-level architecture, metareasoning, execution monitoring, process conformance checking, provenance, commitments, durable execution, observability, and operational assurance. The strongest architecture is a hybrid of deterministic state/provenance, local bounded monitors, compact hierarchical summaries, periodic semantic reconciliation, independent checks for high-impact judgments, and actuation only where authority is explicitly granted. A single omniscient overseer would create context, authority, privacy, alerting, and correlated-evaluator failures.

The preliminary Design finding is that the aspect reconciles Athena's intended work with evidence of actual state, keeps each discrepancy visible until verified or explicitly accepted, and routes unresolved residue without becoming its implementer. `Reconciliation` is the strongest working-name hypothesis; it is agent-authored and has not been ruled by the psyche. `Assurance`, `Oversight`, `Stewardship`, `Attunement`, `Vigilance`, `Integrity`, and `Closure` remain analyzed alternatives.
