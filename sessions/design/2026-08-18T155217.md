## Objective

Design `flow`, the daemon that sets up and starts a model flow with its
own working directory, system prompt, training files, and instruction
prompt. It replaces the plan for a `training` repo; Curriculum stays
hand-maintained until flow replaces what it does.

## Governing direction

The Designer does not know how flow is designed; the psyche does. Ask,
in clear simple questions, one topic at a time; each question restates
in a line what it is about. No anatomy proposed before asking. When the
design can be stated back coherently and simply, state it and ask
whether it is right.
