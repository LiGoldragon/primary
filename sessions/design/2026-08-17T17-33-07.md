# Design session

The psyche asked to design an aspect focused on surveying all flows, verifying what has and has not been done, and preventing work from getting out of hand or falling through cracks. Candidate language raised by the psyche: “Maintenance,” “survey,” and “Overseer,” with the first two judged insufficient and the last offered tentatively.

Requested evidence base:

- related skills in popular skill repositories;
- the wider theoretical and practical field of meta-level AI flows used to oversee other AI flows;
- work by leading thinkers and developers;
- recovered prior psyche expressions touching this subject.

Skills active for the session: management, session-log, spirit, psyche, psyche-interraction, psyche-acquisition, and design.

Open work: recover existing psyche; conduct delegated repository and field research; synthesize the design anatomy and naming space; continue the conversation with the psyche before implementation.

## Dispatches

- `psyche_acquisition`: recovering exact prior psyche expressions, their authority levels, chronology, and tensions.
- `skill_repo_research`: surveying relevant artifacts in popular AI-agent skill repositories and extracting their conceptual and naming vocabulary.
- `field_research`: surveying the wider theory and practice of meta-level workflow oversight, including contrary evidence and architectural failure modes.

The opening vision was recorded verbatim in `psyche/Vision/surveyingAllFlows.md`. It establishes the desired function while preserving “Maintenance,” “survey,” and “Overseer” as unsettled naming thoughts rather than decisions.
