## Objective

Research the anatomy of context strata and determine whether the top stratum differs between Claude or ChatGPT web apps and Codex or Claude coding TUIs.

## State

The governing skills are Spirit, subflows, design, psyche-interraction, psyche, and session-log. Product research and existing-psyche discovery were delegated and synthesized.

## Findings

“Top stratum” has two relevant meanings that must not be collapsed:

1. The product- or harness-owned system composition presented to the model. This differs between conversational web apps and coding harnesses: the latter includes coding-agent behavior, tool use, environment, permissions, and agent-loop machinery, while the former includes conversational-product behavior and app facilities.
2. The absolute highest service-owned instruction layer. Public evidence does not reveal whether each vendor shares a common hidden prelude between its web app and coding harness or uses entirely separate tops.

For Claude, official documentation establishes distinct Claude.ai and Claude Code system prompts; Claude Code’s internal prompt is unpublished, while `CLAUDE.md` is explicitly loaded below it as a user message. For OpenAI, Codex’s request construction and server-controlled system message are documented, but ChatGPT’s exact hidden system/developer composition and ordering are not.

Therefore the defensible answer is: the effective product top is different; the ultimate inaccessible top may or may not be. Equality of the full top strata cannot be proven from public documentation or available source. Exact settlement would require request capture, provider source, or an authoritative disclosure.

No new psyche ruling was made or logged during this research.
