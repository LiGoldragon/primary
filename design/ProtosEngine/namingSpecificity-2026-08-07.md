# Naming Specificity — 2026-08-07

Ruling from the psyche while reviewing the sealed-trait naming
candidates for Codex's first landing.

## Ruling: names are maximally specific, never generic-categorical

Agent text answered: management proposed `CorePreparationSeat`,
`SemaSeat`, and `SealedSemaConsumer` as candidate names for the
authority trait, leaning on "seat" from the redesign-audit ruling's
category vocabulary.

Psyche: "whats a seat? the terminology is too generic sounding. I would
rather something be called BirdOverlookingTheTopFloorWindow than
PerchedBird, if you grasp my meaning."

The principle: a name should paint the thing in its full particularity.
A vivid, longer, hyper-specific name beats a short generic-categorical
one. Category words from rulings ("seat", "authority", "consumer") are
classification vocabulary, not name material. This sits alongside
correctNaming (correct, legible names; llmTokenOptimizationRulings-
2026-08-04) and the trait-vocabulary rulings: correctness of a name now
explicitly includes specificity.

Consequence for the sealed-trait naming: the generic candidates are
withdrawn; replacement candidates must state exactly what the bound
grants, in full.
