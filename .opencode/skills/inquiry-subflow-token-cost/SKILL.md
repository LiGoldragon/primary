- Which reconstructed concise returns are sufficient without followups or rereads? The five large-read packages were not tested as replacements for their original episodes.
- What fraction of real retrieval episodes has lower parent-context token growth or lower all-flow token consumption through delegation? The selected case sets are not a population sample.
- What was the real probe's exact serialized caller brief, and what would a different minimal predefined role cost? Its archived spawn-message field is encrypted, and the observed existing role is not an optimized-role benchmark.
- What are the corresponding monetary or subscription-allowance costs across different models, cache categories and failed requests? The token records alone do not establish these prices or quota weights.
